## Vocabulary and the object of inquiry {#vocabulary}

**[A.I.]({{ '/1888/' | relative_url }})** (artificial intelligence) participates here in source recovery, comparison, organization and inquiry. **[UFO](https://projectavalon.net/forum4/showthread.php?122463-UFOlogy-UAP-Developments-So-Far-2024-Present)** (unidentified flying object) and **[UAP](https://projectavalon.net/forum4/showthread.php?122463-UFOlogy-UAP-Developments-So-Far-2024-Present/page2#post1595081)** (unidentified anomalous phenomena, also historically unidentified aerial phenomena) are the source field’s terms. The vocabulary itself becomes a research topic when a change in naming changes what material people can find or consider.

“Disclosure” can mean the release of a document, an admission about a programme, testimony becoming public, a change in reporting practice or a much broader confirmation proposed by a participant. The particular meaning travels with each source. A narrower release may create a valuable opening while broader questions remain unresolved.

### Eleven practical starting questions {#footage-method}

The following compact questions reproduce the original numbered checklist in **UFO big dossier(6).txt**. The longer footage file develops their purposes and applications.

<div class="accm-rounded-quote" markdown="1">

1. WHO FILMED IT?
2. WHERE WAS IT FILMED?
3. WHEN WAS IT FILMED?
4. ANY OTHER (independent) SOURCES that confirm what we see here ... if not, why not?
5. ANY LOCAL NEWS REPORTS that conforms what is seen here? ... if not, why not?
6. WHO UPLOADED THE ORIGINAL? (the first) ... What else does he (or she) upload?
7. Is there ANYTHING else that can give more "credence" what is seen here? ... if not, why not?
8. Are there any skeptical inquiries done by anyone? If yes, always allow all sides to be heard!
9. Always ask WHY so many people refuse to dig much deeper before spreading it around helping to make it go "viral" that does not do justice to real UFO research! It only will be used as a "showcase" how easy, so many can be fooled.
10. Are there any other cameras recording the same from a different angle?
11. Any other witnesses? ... if not, why not?

— **John Kuhles 🦜🦋🌳**, supplied dossier; original wording preserved.

</div>

These are investigative openings. An absent witness, unavailable local report or inaccessible original becomes a circumstance to understand. The practical work includes filming known objects under comparable conditions, checking reference points, recovering weather and astronomical context, distinguishing independent recordings from copies, and comparing proposed explanations with the recorded scene. Public aircraft-tracking services and primary radar records have different coverage and provenance; a later case inquiry should state which it actually obtained.

John’s distinction between a **report, recorded observation, interpretation and surviving unexplained feature** helps preserve the object through successive descriptions. A later identification can improve the record; an unresolved feature can define the next question.
