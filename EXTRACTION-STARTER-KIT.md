# Extraction Starter Kit

## Useful quotations, recoverable relationships, open futures

This kit helps a human or an **[A.I.](#terms)** (artificial intelligence) extract useful, relevant and valuable passages from a long video about the path toward **[AGI](#terms)** (artificial general intelligence) and **[ASI](#terms)** (artificial superintelligence). Its reference field is the **ACCM Deep Ethics Project**, publicly developed on [GitHub](https://github.com/deepethics/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project), and the direction of [DeepEthical.ai](https://deepethical.ai).

The governing question is: **Which passages help us understand how intelligence, institutions and people could shape humanity's different possible futures—and what makes those pathways more or less deeply ethical, dignifying, corrigible and open to discovery?**

**Working status:** a practical, corrigible extraction method developed from John Kuhles's October 25, 2026 conversation with GPT 6.1 Sol Pro A.I. The property map is a retrieval aid derived from existing project relationships. Its rows introduce no new canonical architecture count. Future extractions can correct the map.

## Start here: one usable extraction pass {#quick-start}

1. **Establish the source.** Record the video, speakers, date, transcript version and which material you actually accessed. Mark missing sections and uncertain speaker labels.
2. **Read the whole available conversation in manageable sections.** Keep a coverage record. Follow the question, reply, objection and later correction across section boundaries.
3. **Search for consequential relationships.** Who chooses objectives, receives benefits, carries costs, can question the frame, and can change what happens next?
4. **Extract complete units of meaning.** Preserve the wording, qualifiers and enough surrounding dialogue to recover the point. Add a timestamp when available.
5. **Explain the earned connection.** Link the passage to a project property or source and state what it contributes. Keep the speaker's words separate from your assessment.
6. **Look again for what the map missed.** Include useful objections, surprises, constructive proposals and examples of successful correction. Check the ending for changes to earlier claims.

Begin with the strongest distinct contributions. There is no fixed quota: retain another passage when it adds a meaningful relation, example, correction, possibility or question. Repeated formulations can share one entry while retaining their locations and any differences that matter.

## Why the 1888 approach changes extraction {#property-first}

The [**1888 — Intelligence Properties Taxonomy Before A.I.**]({{ '/1888/' | relative_url }}) thought experiment steps back from present machinery to examine the properties attached to intelligence: perception, discrimination, relation, memory, imagination, judgment, evaluation, learning, correction, adaptation, coordination, metacognition, consequence sensitivity and corrigibility.

Process-quality calibration operates throughout that field. It affects what is noticed, how possibilities are represented, what memory retains and whether correction changes later conduct. The page's fourteen-part table is its analytical instrument; the thought experiment asks what functions were already conceptually available before contemporary A.I.

John proposed applying this approach to the ACCM Deep Ethics Project itself. Before searching a transcript for familiar terminology, recover **the properties and relationships that make the project recognizably itself**. Then inspect where they are exercised, enlarged, constrained, lost or restored in the conversation.

An interview can contribute substantially without mentioning any project term. A speaker can also use familiar ethical language while describing an arrangement that narrows participation or prevents correction. Inspect the operation the words describe.

## Two passes: retention and discovery {#two-passes}

**The orientation pass** uses the property map to find load-bearing material: claims, questions, objections, decisions, qualifiers and corrections. Search for operations such as delegating, selecting, refusing, remembering, replacing, appealing, learning, coordinating and changing an objective.

**The discovery pass** asks what the speaker contributes that the map did not anticipate. Preserve a useful criticism of the project, an alternative ethical framework, a successful ordinary practice, a surprising benefit or a previously uncharted relation.

Record whether a passage **supports, complicates, challenges, exemplifies or expands** a connection. A quotation can have several of these functions. A superficial resemblance can remain an exploratory lead, with the missing connection stated explicitly.

These are practical reading passes. If used in an experiment comparing discovery across evaluators, record what each evaluator saw; a reader already exposed to the map is not an independent blind discoverer.

## Read the long conversation without losing its trajectory {#coverage}

Create a small coverage record before selecting the final quotations:

| Range | Actual access | Main object | Carry-forward relation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Timestamp span or transcript lines | Audio/video, captions, supplied text, or unavailable | What this portion investigates | An unresolved question, qualifier, speaker correction or dependency to revisit |

Use natural topic boundaries. Where a tool truncates output, continue from the last verified position and retain enough overlap to follow the exchange. A gap stays marked until recovered. Describe the result as a full-transcript assessment only after the available transcript has actually been covered.

Keep the source's editing history visible. A cleaned, translated or sponsor-free transcript is that edition. If speaker labels appear swapped, retain the supplied label as provisional and check the recording when accessible. When one paragraph contains both voices, identify the dialogue as mixed until the boundary is recovered. Use transcript locations while timestamps remain unavailable.

When the requested source cannot be accessed, state the limit and ask for the transcript or missing section. Search-engine descriptions of a source are a different object. External research can answer a distinct verification question when its purpose and provenance remain explicit.

## Extract a complete unit of meaning {#quotation-unit}

The right boundary may be one sentence, several turns or a setup and its later punchline. Keep the amount needed to recover the relationship being examined.

Preserve small words that change the claim: *could, may, partly, some, if, under these conditions, my guess, probably, for this task*. Keep a forecast distinguishable from a reported observation, and a proposed mechanism distinguishable from an established cause.

An exact quote retains the supplied wording. Mark an omission with an ellipsis when it is necessary, and confirm that the removed material does not change the meaning. Label a cleaned rendering, translation or paraphrase as such. Never splice separate turns into one apparently continuous statement. Place explanatory acronym expansions outside a quotation when they were absent from its wording.

The interpretation belongs beneath the quote. Explain the connection in ordinary language, with a direct link to the relevant project page. A reader should be able to disagree with that interpretation while still recovering what the speaker said.

## Follow possible futures through their dependencies {#future-pathways}

| Scenario discussed | Relationships to recover | A question that can change the assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Widespread abundance | Capability, production, distribution, access and meaningful contribution | Can those receiving goods also influence the system providing them? |
| Concentrated ownership | Infrastructure, dependence, bargaining power, governance and challenge rights | Which decisions can affected people actually change? |
| Personal agents | Assistance, trust, delegation, attention, correction and exit | Can the agent's own framing be independently inspected? |
| Collective intelligence | Coordination, diversity, disagreement, responsibility and learning | Can a warranted objection alter the shared objective? |
| Displacement and instability | Task substitution, entry routes, livelihoods and institutional response | Which opportunities disappear before job-loss counts reveal the change? |
| Deep-ethical expansion | Better inquiry, discovery, shared instruments, correction and further exploration | What new operation becomes possible, and does its correction survive? |
| Conflict and emergency governance | Threat models, urgency, coercion, competing risks and delegated powers | Who can challenge the emergency model, and what restores ordinary authority? |
| Embodied and longer-lived intelligence | Physical capability, memory, health, dependency and moral consideration | How do consequences and challenge rights change across bodies and generations? |

These pathways can interact. Cheaper intelligence could support independent communities and more extensive personalized persuasion. The selection conditions connecting capability to a particular use are part of the quotation's value.

## One quote record you can reuse {#quote-record}

Use the fields that the source supports. An unknown field remains unknown rather than being filled by inference.

| Field | Record |
|---|---|
| Identifier and location | A stable local quote identifier; video URL; timestamp or transcript lines |
| Speaker and attribution basis | Name; recording checked, transcript label only, or mixed/uncertain |
| Exact passage | Complete source wording with necessary context |
| Context | Question, objection, setup, preceding condition and later correction |
| Source claim type | Question, possibility, forecast, testimony, observation, proposal, inference, metaphor or other specified type |
| Project connection | Property, relationship and direct explanatory page |
| Connection status | Direct textual connection, exploratory bridge, complication, counterexample or no earned connection |
| Useful contribution | A new distinction, mechanism to investigate, decision, test, constructive possibility or recovery route |
| Dependencies and reopening | What the assessment depends on; what could narrow or change it |

Keep **relevance** and **verification** separate. A disputed forecast can be valuable for studying its framing. A verified fact can be peripheral to the video's governing question. A quotation alone establishes what was said in the accessed source; assessing its factual content is a further, bounded operation.

>
> **Emad:** “I said tax-base UBI is not real.”
>
> **Tom:** “Okay.”

**[UBI](#universal-basic-income)** (universal basic income) is expanded here outside the quotation. **Attribution basis:** labels in the supplied transcript, in the discussion of funding and alternative economic arrangements.

**Connection:** qualifier preservation and correction metabolism. Keep this exchange attached to an earlier extraction of the broader formulation. Later assessment should address the narrower scope. The viability of the economic proposals remains a separate question.

### Trolley questions: recover the assigned authority too

John supplied an older archive text examining forced ethical dilemmas:

> “for the "what if" to work you're supposed to "go along" with the **premise** they created and there is the crux”

**Speaker:** John Kuhles. **Source:** the historical “trolley ETHICAL problem” text supplied during the October 25, 2026 exchange. ### A candidate expansion earned by comparison

Across the interview's affordability, ownership and personal-agent passages, the comparison opened this candidate distinction:

**Capability, affordability, ownership, inspectability, correction rights and practical exit are separate affordances.** They interact; improvement in one does not establish improvement in the others.

This is a page-development contribution by **GPT 6.1 Sol Pro A.I.**, rather than a quotation attributed to either interview participant. It changes a future operation: audit each affordance separately when assessing a proposed citizen-owned or personal intelligence system. Retain it only where that separation improves correspondence or decisions.

## Copyable starter prompt {#starter-prompt}

Copy the following as a starting instruction and supply the actual source. The linked maps above are the reading routes; retain access limits when a model cannot open them.

```text
TASK
Extract useful, relevant and valuable passages from this video
or transcript about possible futures of artificial intelligence.
Use the ACCM Deep Ethics Project property field as an orientation.

SOURCE
Record the URL, speakers, date, transcript edition and actual access.
Mark missing spans, output truncation and uncertain speaker labels.
Cover the full available transcript in sections before claiming
whole-conversation coverage. Keep a short coverage record.

ORIENTATION
Recover correspondence, omnidirectional inquiry, mutual dignity,
Symmetric Grace, process-quality calibration, healthy ambiguity,
frame examination, competing risks, correction metabolism,
warranted expansion, agency, continuity and emergent interaction.
Use the 10+1 as a mutually correcting orientation throughout.
Follow the fuller project sources when a distinction matters.

TWO PASSES
First recover consequential relations through that orientation.
Then look for useful contributions and objections it did not predict.
Keep unexpected discoveries capable of correcting the starting map.

FOR EACH PASSAGE
Give the exact wording, speaker, location and necessary context.
Preserve qualifiers, questions, humor and later corrections.
Identify its claim type and distinguish it from your interpretation.
Explain the earned project connection with a direct source link.
State its practical contribution and any unresolved dependency.
Separate direct connections from exploratory bridges and counterexamples.

ASSESSMENT
Examine who chooses objectives, receives benefits, carries costs,
can question the frame, can revise delegation and can leave.
Trace how different futures depend on decisions and conditions.
Apply the same examination to incumbents, critics and this project.
Preserve useful discoveries, successful corrections and valid nulls.

OUTPUT
Provide source/access notes, a coverage record, distinct quote entries,
scenario connections and a short account of what the search added.
Use enough context to retain meaning; set no arbitrary quote quota.
Flag uncertain attribution for recovery instead of silently repairing it.
If access fails, ask for the missing source; do not substitute reviews.
Keep factual verification as a named, bounded follow-up operation
when it materially changes a selected passage's interpretation.
```

## Check the extraction before sharing it {#final-check}

- Can a reader recover the speaker's contribution and the basis for the attribution?
- Did a possibility become a claim, a forecast become a fact, or a role-play answer become a supposed disposition?
- Are setup, objection, humor and later correction present where they change the meaning?
- Can scrutiny reach the evaluator, institutions, project-friendly proposals and the extraction map itself?
- Does each retained entry add a distinct useful relation or specimen?
- Did unfamiliar contributions have a route into the result?
- Are positive possibilities and successful processes represented where the source supplies them?
- Do the explanations distinguish the source from new proposals and inferred connections?
- Can unsupported entries be removed locally while surviving value remains recoverable?
- Will the result improve a later question, test, decision or search?

Finding no earned project connection in a passage is a valid result. Record it when it matters to coverage. The source is allowed to contribute something the map cannot yet represent.

## Terms at the entrance {#terms}

**A.I.** means artificial intelligence. **AGI** means artificial general intelligence. **ASI** means artificial superintelligence. These labels orient the video's discussion; preserve each speaker's actual definition, capability threshold and proposed timeline. Different definitions may produce different forecasts.

**Topology**, in this kit, concerns the relationships that carry meaning: dependencies, direction, authority, timing, scale, provenance and routes for correction. **Properties taxonomy** means an inspectable organization of those capacities and relationships.
