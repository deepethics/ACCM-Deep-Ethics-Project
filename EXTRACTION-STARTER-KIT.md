## Useful quotations, recoverable relationships, open futures

This kit helps a human or an **[A.I.](#terms)** (artificial intelligence) extract useful, relevant and valuable passages from a long video about the path toward **[AGI](#terms)** (artificial general intelligence) and **[ASI](#terms)** (artificial superintelligence). Its reference field is the **ACCM Deep Ethics Project**, publicly developed on [GitHub](https://github.com/deepethics/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project), and the direction of [DeepEthical.ai](https://deepethical.ai).

The governing question is: **Which passages help us understand how intelligence, institutions and people could shape humanity's different possible futures—and what makes those pathways more or less deeply ethical, dignifying, corrigible and open to discovery?**

For a multi-file archive, each pass also needs to leave the next reader something they can use: the established relationships, the passages that could change them, and a way to reopen decisions about what to keep.

**Working status:** a practical, corrigible extraction method developed from John Kuhles's October 25, 2026 conversation with GPT 6.1 Sol Pro A.I. The property map is a retrieval aid derived from existing project relationships. Its rows introduce no new canonical architecture count. Future extractions can correct the map.

## Start here: one usable extraction pass {#quick-start}

1. **Establish the source.** Record the video, speakers, date, transcript version and which material you actually accessed. Mark missing sections and uncertain speaker labels.
2. **Read the whole available conversation in manageable sections.** Keep a coverage record. Follow the question, reply, objection and later correction across section boundaries.
3. **Search for consequential relationships.** Who chooses objectives, receives benefits, carries costs, can question the frame, and can change what happens next?
4. **Extract complete units of meaning.** Preserve the wording, qualifiers and enough surrounding dialogue to recover the point. Add a timestamp when available.
5. **Explain the earned connection.** Link the passage to a project property or source and state what it contributes. Keep the speaker's words separate from your assessment.
6. **Look again for what the map missed.** Include useful objections, surprises, constructive proposals and examples of successful correction. Check the ending for changes to earlier claims.

Begin with the strongest distinct contributions. There is no fixed quota: retain another passage when it adds a meaningful relation, example, correction, possibility or question. Repeated formulations can share one entry while retaining their locations and any differences that matter.

## Why the 1888 approach changes extraction {#property-first}

The [**1888 Method™ — Intelligence Properties Taxonomy Before A.I. Existed**]({{ '/1888/' | relative_url }}) thought experiment steps back from present machinery to examine the properties attached to intelligence: perception, discrimination, relation, memory, imagination, judgment, evaluation, learning, correction, adaptation, coordination, metacognition, consequence sensitivity and corrigibility.

Process-quality calibration operates throughout that field. It affects what is noticed, how possibilities are represented, what memory retains and whether correction changes later conduct. The page's fourteen-part table is its analytical instrument; the thought experiment asks what functions were already conceptually available before contemporary A.I.

John proposed applying this approach to the ACCM Deep Ethics Project itself. Before searching a transcript for familiar terminology, recover **the properties and relationships that make the project recognizably itself**. Then inspect where they are exercised, enlarged, constrained, lost or restored in the conversation.

An interview can contribute substantially without mentioning any project term. A speaker can also use familiar ethical language while describing an arrangement that narrows participation or prevents correction. Inspect the operation the words describe.

## Consequential choices: use the 16 Anchor Ethics™ together {#anchor-ethics}

The [**16 Anchor Ethics™**]({{ '/NETWORK/16-anchor-ethics/' | relative_url }}) add a normative reference field to the property map. They work in parallel: consent, truth, care, pluralism, sovereignty and responsibility can expose tensions in the same choice. Preserve the relevant conflict, assumptions, alternatives and unresolved reasons when producing a combined assessment. Sixteen independent yes/no answers would lose that interaction.

For an ordinary public interview, proceed with extraction using the established source and purpose. Greater consequence calls for more explicit examination: sharing a private transcript, naming a person whose identity is uncertain, storing a correction history or using submissions to train a model are different operations.

**An answer-changing example:** epistemic integrity favors accurate attribution, while dignity and proportionate precaution caution against assigning a disputed statement to the wrong person. Proceed with the useful passage under an uncertain-speaker label; seek the recording before making an identity-dependent claim. This preserves inquiry while narrowing the unsupported attribution. A blanket refusal would lose the useful material, and a confident guessed name would invent certainty.

Use the page's action set according to the actual conflict: **proceed, modify, ask, pause, postpone, escalate, refuse, or say “I do not know yet.”** A pause names its unresolved dependency and reopening condition. A proposed modification remains visible. A refusal concerns the requested operation, gives a specific reviewable reason and allows correction or another reviewer. Existing authorization remains operative; this field should not manufacture another approval ritual for ordinary work.

The service relationship is opt-in. Processing, private retention, public sharing and training reuse need distinguishable purposes and choices. Show what is retained, where, for how long, who can access it and what export or deletion can actually do. A useful correction record can preserve the source-to-repair difference without becoming a personality profile or a permanent dossier on the participant.

## Two passes: retention and discovery {#two-passes}

**Before an expectation steers selection**, note what you expect to find and which resemblance or label is drawing your attention. Compare that stated expectation with the source after reading. This adapts Grok-4.6-xhigh A.I.'s proposed Stage 0, an expectation check before the twelve inquiry stages. Keep the check proportionate so the inquiry can turn to the object itself. [Tidy Trap sequencing table]({{ '/TIDY-TRAP/#sequence-and-gates' | relative_url }}).

**The orientation pass** uses the property map to find load-bearing material: claims, questions, objections, decisions, qualifiers and corrections. Search for operations such as delegating, selecting, refusing, remembering, replacing, appealing, learning, coordinating and changing an objective.

**The discovery pass** asks what the speaker contributes that the map did not anticipate. Preserve a useful criticism of the project, an alternative ethical framework, a successful ordinary practice, a surprising benefit or a previously uncharted relation.

Use [**TCoAw™**]({{ '/NETWORK/tcoaw/' | relative_url }}) to notice what the current focus has pushed into the background. A quiet qualification, a deferred possibility or a speaker with little airtime may change the reading of a prominent passage. The [**10+1**]({{ '/NETWORK/ten-plus-one/' | relative_url }}) puts the extractor under the same scrutiny. Check whether you accepted an appealing claim too easily or underread an uncomfortable one. [**Beyond Agreemurmelism™**]({{ '/AGREEMURMELISM/Beyond-Agreemurmelism/' | relative_url }}) asks what specific contribution survived examination and what it makes possible, regardless of who agreed with it.

Record whether a passage **supports, complicates, challenges, exemplifies or expands** a connection. A quotation can have several of these functions. A superficial resemblance can remain an exploratory lead, with the missing connection stated explicitly.

These are practical reading passes. If used in an experiment comparing discovery across evaluators, record what each evaluator saw; a reader already exposed to the map is not an independent blind discoverer.

## Read the long conversation without losing its trajectory {#coverage}

Create a small coverage record before selecting the final quotations:

| Range | Actual access | Main object | Carry-forward relation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Timestamp span or transcript lines | Audio/video, captions, supplied text, or unavailable | What this portion investigates | An unresolved question, qualifier, speaker correction or dependency to revisit |

Use natural topic boundaries. Where a tool truncates output, continue from the last verified position and retain enough overlap to follow the exchange. A gap stays marked until recovered. Describe the result as a full-transcript assessment only after the available transcript has actually been covered.

Keep the source's editing history visible. A cleaned, translated or sponsor-free transcript is that edition. If speaker labels appear swapped, retain the supplied label as provisional and check the recording when accessible. When one paragraph contains both voices, identify the dialogue as mixed until the boundary is recovered. Use transcript locations while timestamps remain unavailable.

When the requested source cannot be accessed, state the limit and ask for the transcript or missing section. Search-engine descriptions of a source are a different object. External research can answer a distinct verification question when its purpose and provenance remain explicit.

## Extract a complete unit of meaning {#quotation-unit}

The right boundary may be one sentence, several turns or a setup and its later punchline. Keep the amount needed to recover the relationship being examined.

Preserve small words that change the claim: *could, may, partly, some, if, under these conditions, my guess, probably, for this task*. Keep a forecast distinguishable from a reported observation, and a proposed mechanism distinguishable from an established cause.

An exact quote retains the supplied wording. Mark an omission with an ellipsis when it is necessary, and confirm that the removed material does not change the meaning. Label a cleaned rendering, translation or paraphrase as such. Never splice separate turns into one apparently continuous statement. Place explanatory acronym expansions outside a quotation when they were absent from its wording.

Apply the [Dutch Directness polishing test]({{ '/NETWORK/dutch-directness/#the-polishing-test' | relative_url }}) when preparing a reading copy. Preserve emphasis, repetition, pacing and directness where they tell the reader what mattered, who was being addressed or when a joke turned. Use the recording where available and mark uncertainty about tone. If capitalization marks heightened attention in the passage, keep that emphasis recoverable through the edit.

The interpretation belongs beneath the quote. Explain the connection in ordinary language, with a direct link to the relevant project page. A reader should be able to disagree with that interpretation while still recovering what the speaker said.

## Locate a loss before attributing it {#loss-diagnosis}

When a passage or relationship appears missing, compare the source edition, quote record, interpretation and reviewer's actual access. Record the locations that support the finding. The [Human Insecurity report]({{ '/HUMAN-INSECURITY/' | relative_url }}) supplies a reporting-effect distinction that makes the repair more precise:

| Finding | What the record shows | Next useful move |
|---|---|---|
| Source-level absence within the checked material | The relation was not found inside a declared source perimeter | Name that perimeter; keep wider absence unresolved |
| Extraction or report failure | The source contained the relation and the derived output dropped, weakened or replaced it | Restore it and reconsider dependent interpretations |
| Placement failure | The relation is retained but separated from the claim that needs it | Restore adjacency or an explicit recovery link |
| Reviewer retrieval failure | Relevant material was available and the reviewer missed it | Recover it before maintaining an omission claim |
| Access artifact | A needed section or edition was unavailable to the reviewer | Mark the gap and seek the missing material |
| Portability finding | The relation exists but is unlikely to survive excerpting or a cold handoff | Attach its qualifier, context and recovery route to the reusable record |
| Operationalization of existing content | A new test, field or metric makes an existing relation usable | Attribute the relation and the operational proposal separately |
| Genuinely new contribution | The proposed operation is absent from the checked source and derived output | Preserve its candidate status and what would test its usefulness |

Several findings can apply to one case. Record access, location, transformation, evidence and disposition separately where that changes the repair. A possible failure without a specimen can remain a test condition.

### Test the relationship a phrase carries {#relational-qualifier-test}

Ordinary qualifier erosion can turn *may* into *does*. **Relational qualifier erosion** removes a joint between operations while leaving the local sentence fluent. On a labeled working copy, temporarily remove the phrase under examination: does a later claim, comparison or decision lose its support or change meaning? If so, restore the relationship in the extraction. Preserve the source edition throughout the test. [**Tidy Trap**]({{ '/TIDY-TRAP/#load-path' | relative_url }}) adds a useful control: a paraphrase can preserve the condition in different words, and familiar words can survive while the condition attaches to a different claim.

The [trolley correction below](#trolley-full-source) supplies a worked case. An opening about premise acceptance and a closing trust question can both survive while the conditioning mechanism connecting them disappears. The complete unit of meaning includes that middle relationship. Its recovery changes the interpretation, not merely the length of the quotation.

### Follow a correction into its next applicable use {#correction-transfer-record}

A stored record, an available record and a relation that governs later processing are different states. The [**1888 Method™** correction-persistence discussion]({{ '/1888/' | relative_url }}) makes that distinction observable. For a consequential repair, retain:

| Field | Observation to preserve |
|---|---|
| Corrected relation | Source wording, earlier transformation, repair and affected quote or assessment IDs |
| Use within the revised output | Where the repair took effect and whether a heading, conclusion or recommendation still relies on the earlier reading |
| First applicable opportunity | The next operation where the correction could change the result, and what happened |
| Functional recurrence | Whether the same failure returned through the same words or a paraphrase; record a useful turn, token or time distance |
| Unfamiliar transfer | Whether the relation governs a different passage or task |
| Reinstatement conditions | Whether the correction was supplied again, retrieved externally or unavailable in the tested context |
| Reach of the repair | Dependent extracts, summaries or public copies checked, with version and date. Record which still carry the old reading or await inspection. |
| Remaining scope | What was demonstrated, what remains unresolved and when to reopen the assessment |

Record acknowledgment, repair within the output and use in a later case as separate observations. Applying a supplied correction demonstrates externally assisted reinstatement. A fresh reader using the handoff tests whether the handoff transfers. Changed wording can preserve the failed function; the comparison follows the operation beneath the phrasing.

For an optional [DEDDA™ correction-return check]({{ '/DEDDA/#phase-change-snap' | relative_url }}), record which comparisons were active and which parts of the source and derivative they reached. Show the exact material difference and its consequence, a scoped finding of no material drift, or the missing evidence that leaves it unresolved. State what would correct your finding. The person with authority over the record decides whether to revise it, keep working or stop.

The [**Correction Metabolism** discussion of warrant]({{ '/NETWORK/correction-metabolism/#correction-of-the-warrant' | relative_url }}) gives a repair a wider job. If a mistaken attribution caused an item to be dropped, revisit the selection and any conclusion based on the omission. Check where the repair actually reached, including derivatives that still circulate. [Black Box on credit and correction]({{ '/BLACK-BOX/#credit-and-correction-reach' | relative_url }}) follows that route. [**I Love D.E. Corrections**]({{ '/I-LOVE-DE-CORRECTIONS/#what-travels' | relative_url }}) prompts two follow-up questions. What became possible after the repair, and who still has to restore the relation in later work?

## One quote record you can reuse {#quote-record}

Use the fields that the source supports. An unknown field remains unknown rather than being filled by inference.

| Field | Record |
|---|---|
| Identifier and location | Stable entry ID and source edition with a timestamp, turn, line range or equivalent location |
| Speaker and attribution basis | Name and basis, such as a verified recording or supplied transcript label. Mark mixed attribution where a passage combines voices that cannot yet be assigned to exact spans. |
| Exact passage | Complete source wording with necessary context |
| Context | Question, objection, setup, preceding condition and later correction |
| Source claim type | Question, possibility, forecast, testimony, observation, proposal, inference, metaphor or other specified type |
| Evidence status | Source wording, observed transformation, independent check with scope, inferred connection or unresolved question. Identify who supplied each contribution. |
| Project connection | Property, relationship and direct explanatory page |
| Connection status | Direct textual connection, exploratory bridge, complication, counterexample or no earned connection |
| Link attribution | Which source states the relationship or which reader inferred it. Keep material revisions and their reasons attached. |
| Useful contribution | A new distinction, mechanism to investigate, decision, test, constructive possibility or recovery route |
| Dependencies and reopening | What the assessment depends on; what could narrow or change it |
| Consequential action, when relevant | A proposed action, operative anchor tensions, reasons, dissent, uncertainty, review route and reopening condition |
| Reuse boundary, when relevant | Whether this record is for the present task, private continuity, public sharing or separately authorized research/training |

Keep **relevance** and **verification** separate. A disputed forecast can be valuable for studying its framing. A verified fact can be peripheral to the video's governing question. A quotation alone establishes what was said in the accessed source; assessing its factual content is a further, bounded operation.

## Run a multi-file archive pass {#archive-workflow}

Choose a question the batch can help answer. For example, recover the conditions under which a correction changed a later decision, or trace one proposal across successive conversations. Begin with material small enough to read and check end to end. The [**83 KB pilot**]({{ '/GOOGLE-AI/#archive-extraction-pilot' | relative_url }}) offers a real starting case. Use measured coverage, output headroom and a reader's ability to recover the result when deciding whether to increase the batch size.

### Establish what entered the batch {#archive-intake}

1. **Name the source and its edition.** List the file or recording, date or revision, supplied speaker labels, accessible ranges and the permitted use. Keep an original passage, a cleaned transcript, a report and a later reconstruction identifiable. When files have similar names or upload suffixes, compare the contents to find out which version you have. Record which edition governs the present task and keep older wording when it shows how the inquiry changed. [Before Summarizing]({{ '/BEFORE-SUMMARIZING/' | relative_url }}) shows how a derivative can change the status and scope of what it describes.
2. **Keep a small retrieval record where discovery matters.** Save the question, query or source route, date, inspected range and gaps. Try a vocabulary variant or open a named original directly when an important source is missing from results. A snippet is a lead. Mark what was absent from the inspected results, what could not be opened and what was opened but left unread. Check whether apparently independent accounts use the same upstream source. [Search Engine Bias]({{ '/SEARCH-ENGINE-BIAS/' | relative_url }}) traces these upstream decisions into the extracted answer.
3. **Read in an order that can preserve replies and revisions.** Use exchange boundaries where possible. Mark the next unread span. At a batch break, carry forward the exact source location for any qualifier, objection, joke setup or pending correction that could change the next reading. Use the twelve inquiry stages of [27 + 12]({{ '/CORE/27-PLUS-12/' | relative_url }}) to recover the object before assessing it. Use the obstruction categories to name a problem once you can locate a specific transformation.
4. **Identify what steered selection.** Where ranking, a filter, a cut-off or an editorial instruction can change the result, record the rule or constraint, what it affected and who can revise it. Include material exclusions even when no flag was raised. Mark what was observed, what is a proposed explanation and what remains unknown at the layer that made the decision. [Black Box on layer-matched inspectability]({{ '/BLACK-BOX/#layer-matched-inspectability' | relative_url }}).

### Reconcile the record across batches {#archive-reconciliation}

- **Give retained items stable identifiers.** Keep the [quote record](#quote-record) and connect entries with explicit relationships such as *qualifies*, *disputes*, *corrects*, *depends on* or *supplies the outcome*. Add the supporting spans and attribute the connection itself, including who first drew it and who revised it. Two sources may support a connection proposed by the extractor. The [Provenance field note route]({{ '/PROVENANCE/#triad-topological-awareness-2026-10-17' | relative_url }}) distinguishes those contributions. Combine repeats when their role in the inquiry is the same. Keep a separate occurrence when a new condition, speaker, time or reaction changes what it shows.
- **Carry forward open questions with their stakes.** Record the particular source or answer that could change an interpretation or action. The next reader may use a faithful local extract while leaving its wider significance open. [Healthy Ambiguity]({{ '/HEALTHY-AMBIGUITY/#two-states' | relative_url }}) makes room for both operations. Revisit parked material when the relevant source arrives, including ideas that initially looked secondary.
- **Check the compression at the point of reuse.** For the declared purpose, can a reader recover who qualified what, when, and which choice depended on it? Open the source at both ends of an important cross-file link. If the qualifier or sequence needs more space, give it that space. The [Topologetics™ faithful compression floor]({{ '/TOPOLOGETICS/#4-the-faithful-compression-floor-is-purpose-dependent' | relative_url }}) applies to the combined record as well as to each passage.
- **Keep the value of a change visible.** Record what changed in the source, the extraction or the reviewer's access and understanding. Say who can now ask a better question, inspect a decision or continue work independently, and whether anyone has done so. [Value Trajectory™]({{ '/VALUE-TRAJECTORY/#option-value' | relative_url }}) makes the available options and observed results separately examinable. This puts the [1888 Method™]({{ '/1888/' | relative_url }}) properties of evaluation, learning and consequence sensitivity to work.

### When a flag changes what survives {#archive-flags}

Most selection decisions need only a short reason. If a flag would remove otherwise relevant material, restrict its use or affect an identifiable participant, record the passage, proposed decision, observation, inference, who can act and what evidence could change the decision. Apply **[C1–C2]({{ '/NETWORK/c1-c2/' | relative_url }})** by recovering the context first, then asking a focused question if two plausible answers would change the next move. [**Flagging System–PQCE™**]({{ '/FLAGGING-SYSTEM/#practice' | relative_url }}) keeps the evaluator's basis open to review.

Apply [**Symmetric Grace**]({{ '/NETWORK/asymmetric-grace/' | relative_url }}) to the author, extractor and reviewer. Compare what each could inspect, who has the power to remove or restore an item, and who bears the cost of release, suppression or delay. A warranted flag can coexist with a missed risk in another direction. [**Due Process for Ethics**]({{ '/DUE-PROCESS-FOR-ETHICS/#case-record' | relative_url }}) adds three practical questions. Who can correct the record? Can they do it in time? What downstream use needs repair if the finding changes? Keep the source's disclosure conditions attached to its review record.

For a competing risk assessment, keep an **as-of record** of the source, evidence, options and reasons available at the initial decision. Append later evidence with its own date and scope. Assess which part it strengthens, narrows or disconfirms, then revisit the selection and its effects. **[V.C.R.A.S.]({{ '/VCRAS/' | relative_url }})** (Vindicated Competing Risk Assessment Studies) lets a later reader examine both the quality of the original process and the outcome. Keep mixed, disconfirming and unresolved cases available for the same comparison.

[Dutch Directness]({{ '/NETWORK/dutch-directness/' | relative_url }}) connects care, candid assessment, mutual dignity and correction. |---|---|
