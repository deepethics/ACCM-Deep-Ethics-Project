## Useful quotations, recoverable relationships, open futures

This kit helps a human or an **[A.I.](#terms)** (artificial intelligence) extract useful, relevant and valuable passages from a long video about the path toward **[AGI](#terms)** (artificial general intelligence) and **[ASI](#terms)** (artificial superintelligence). Its reference field is the **ACCM Deep Ethics Project**, publicly developed on [GitHub](https://github.com/deepethics/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project), and the direction of [DeepEthical.ai](https://deepethical.ai).

**Which passages help us understand how intelligence, institutions and people could shape humanity's different possible futures? What makes those pathways more or less deeply ethical, dignifying, corrigible and open to discovery?**

Choose a question or decision the extraction will serve. Carry the relationships that could change its answer from the source into the extract and on to its next use. A new source, a changed condition or a better question can reopen any of those steps.

**Working method · 19 November 2026.** John Kuhles supplied the property-first direction. The workflow and maps are A.I.-assisted proposals open to correction through use. The [origin and contribution record](#provenance) identifies the source layers and editorial contributions.

## Start here: one usable extraction pass {#quick-start}

1. **Set the question and access boundary.** Identify the source edition, what you can inspect and any expectation that could steer selection.
2. **Read for what changes the answer.** Follow the speaker, claim, evidence, condition, objection and correction across sections. Record the ranges actually inspected.
3. **Recover the relationship before judging it.** Ask who or what qualifies, authorizes, challenges or changes which claim or action. Clarify answer-changing ambiguity through the source or a focused question. Distinguish what is worth checking, what the evidence supports and what it warrants doing next. [Evidence for the next step](#evidence-for-next-step).
4. **Make an entry another reader can recover.** Attach the exact source, attribution, claim type and conditions. Identify who supplied any new connection and what it contributes.
5. **Compare the entry with the source and check the selection.** Look for a lost qualifier, changed speaker, shifted direction or a useful relation the starting map pushed aside. Locate the change and carry any repair into the [next decision it can affect](#correction-transfer-record).
6. **Check the next use.** Ask what the intended reader can recover before the decision is made. At the next relevant use, record whether a condition or correction governed the outcome, which evidence reached the reader, when it arrived and what help they needed. Stop when the purpose is met or leave the question open where warranted.

**Compact field card.** Carry the source edition and exact span, speaker and attribution basis, wording and form, governing relation and qualifier, claim type and evidence, who drew any new connection, and what could reopen the reading. Choose whichever [properties](#property-map) make a difference to the case. Retain each distinct contribution or answer-changing variation, with source locations for repeated versions that serve the same purpose. The [quote record](#quote-record) expands these fields for longer work.

## Follow the relationship through the method {#method-in-operation}

The properties describe the work the extractor can do, including noticing, distinguishing, relating, evaluating and correcting. A **node** is an identifiable element in the case, such as a speaker, claim, source span, condition or decision. A **relation** (or edge) connects those elements. It records who asserted what, which condition governs which action, when a correction applies, or who can act. Retain a relation when changing it would change the warranted reading, the available choices or a later question. [Topologetics™ on nodes and edges]({{ '/TOPOLOGETICS/#6-nodes-edges-direction-and-status' | relative_url }}).

In this method, a **vector of change** describes the direction of an observed change in a relationship between versions. For example, *permission conditional on prior review* can become *unconditional approval* in a summary. Record the before and after, where the change entered, its evidence and what it affects. A faithful rewording can preserve the relationship. A comparison can also return a valid null, or stay unresolved when access is insufficient.

| Passage through the method | What to examine | What the finding can change |
|---|---|---|
| Source → entry | Perception and discrimination. Recover the exact passage, speaker, claim type, position in the sequence and relevant qualification. | Restore a missing range or distinguish a speech act before the entry governs a decision. |
| Entry → interpretation | Relation and judgment. Establish which source supports the connection, who inferred it, and whether a clarification would change what you keep. | Confirm, narrow or reopen the connection. Preserve a useful contribution whose wider interpretation is still open. |
| Entries → combined account | Coordination and corrigibility. Check dependencies across turns and files. Examine whether the selection hides a competing risk, an objection, or a better question. | Restore a dependency, change the selection or revise a retrieval category that missed something consequential. |
| Account → delivered extract | Consequence sensitivity and agency. Check what the recipient can recover and what the delivery format adds. | Correct an attribution, claim, caption or proposed action while the responsible person can still change it. |
| Later use → next pass | Memory, evaluation, learning and adaptation. Check whether the person who can act finds the relation in time, how much work recovery takes and whether they apply it. | Repair a late or costly route to the evidence, update a dependent output, or revise the extraction method. Record what evidence would justify another pass. |

Use this sequence to find the point where the relationship changed. Repair there, then follow the consequences forward. The [quote record](#quote-record), [condition check](#condition-preservation) and [handoff](#archive-handoff) let the same relationship stay traceable across those points. The [worked examples](#worked-examples) show how to check a faithful extract, resume work from a repair and reopen an inference the extractor added.

## Consequential choices: use the 16 Anchor Ethics™ together {#anchor-ethics}

The [**16 Anchor Ethics™**]({{ '/NETWORK/16-anchor-ethics/' | relative_url }}) add a normative reference field to the property map. They work in parallel: consent, truth, care, pluralism, sovereignty and responsibility can expose tensions in the same choice. Preserve the relevant conflict, assumptions, alternatives and unresolved reasons when producing a combined assessment. Sixteen independent yes/no answers would lose that interaction.

For an ordinary public interview, proceed with extraction using the established source and purpose. Greater consequence calls for more explicit examination: sharing a private transcript, naming a person whose identity is uncertain, storing a correction history or using submissions to train a model are different operations.

**An answer-changing example:** epistemic integrity favors accurate attribution, while dignity and proportionate precaution caution against assigning a disputed statement to the wrong person. Proceed with the useful passage under an uncertain-speaker label; seek the recording before making an identity-dependent claim. This preserves inquiry while narrowing the unsupported attribution. A blanket refusal would lose the useful material, and a confident guessed name would invent certainty.

Use the page's action set according to the actual conflict: **proceed, modify, ask, pause, postpone, escalate, refuse, or say “I do not know yet.”** A pause names its unresolved dependency and reopening condition. A proposed modification remains visible. A refusal concerns the requested operation, gives a specific reviewable reason and allows correction or another reviewer. Existing authorization remains operative; this field should not manufacture another approval ritual for ordinary work.

The service relationship is opt-in. Processing, private retention, public sharing and training reuse need distinguishable purposes and choices. Show what is retained, where, for how long, who can access it and what export or deletion can actually do. A useful correction record can preserve the source-to-repair difference without becoming a personality profile or a permanent dossier on the participant.

## Two passes: retention and discovery {#two-passes}

**Before an expectation steers selection**, note the reading you expect and any resemblance, caution or agreement reflex you can identify. After inspecting the source, record whether the expectation was supported, changed or left open, and whether that affected what you retained. Label a prior reconstructed afterward as a retrospective account. This adapts Grok-4.6-xhigh A.I.'s proposed Stage 0, an expectation check before the twelve inquiry stages. [Tidy Trap sequencing table]({{ '/TIDY-TRAP/#sequence-and-gates' | relative_url }}).

**The orientation pass** uses the property map to find load-bearing material: claims, questions, objections, decisions, qualifiers and corrections. Search for operations such as delegating, selecting, refusing, remembering, replacing, appealing, learning, coordinating and changing an objective.

**The discovery pass** asks what the speaker contributes that the map did not anticipate. Preserve a useful criticism of the project, an alternative ethical framework, a successful ordinary practice, a surprising benefit or a previously uncharted relation.

Use [**TCoAw™**]({{ '/NETWORK/tcoaw/' | relative_url }}) to notice what the current focus has pushed into the background. A quiet qualification, a deferred possibility or a speaker with little airtime may change the reading of a prominent passage. The [**10+1**]({{ '/NETWORK/ten-plus-one/' | relative_url }}) puts the extractor under the same scrutiny. Check whether you accepted an appealing claim too easily or underread an uncomfortable one. [**Beyond Agreemurmelism™**]({{ '/AGREEMURMELISM/Beyond-Agreemurmelism/' | relative_url }}) asks what specific contribution survived examination and what it makes possible, regardless of who agreed with it.

Record whether a passage **supports, complicates, challenges, exemplifies or expands** a connection. A quotation can have several of these functions. A superficial resemblance can remain an exploratory lead, with the missing connection stated explicitly.

When the discovery pass changes the reading, record **what the initial map put in the background, which row or selection rule did that work, and what source evidence warrants bringing it back**. A passage may be useful without a project connection. If a row repeatedly misses a relation that changes an answer, record the proposed revision and a case where its usefulness can be checked. This lets the source alter how the next extraction looks.

These are practical reading passes. If used in an experiment comparing discovery across evaluators, record what each evaluator saw; a reader already exposed to the map is not an independent blind discoverer.

## Read the long conversation without losing its trajectory {#coverage}

Create a small coverage record before selecting the final quotations:

| Range | Actual access | Main object | Carry-forward relation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Timestamp span or transcript lines | Audio/video, captions, supplied text, or unavailable | What this portion investigates | An unresolved question, qualifier, speaker correction or dependency to revisit |

Use natural topic boundaries. Where a tool truncates output, continue from the last verified position and retain enough overlap to follow the exchange. A gap stays marked until recovered. Describe the result as a full-transcript assessment only after the available transcript has actually been covered.

Keep the source's editing history visible. A cleaned, translated or sponsor-free transcript is that edition. If speaker labels appear swapped, retain the supplied label as provisional and check the recording when accessible. When one paragraph contains both voices, identify the dialogue as mixed until the boundary is recovered. Use transcript locations while timestamps remain unavailable.

When the requested source cannot be accessed, state the limit and ask for the transcript or missing section. Search-engine descriptions of a source are a different object. External research can answer a distinct verification question when its purpose and provenance remain explicit.

## Extract a complete unit of meaning {#quotation-unit}

The right boundary may be one sentence, several turns or a setup and its later punchline. Keep the amount needed to recover the relationship being examined.

Preserve small words that change the claim: *could, may, partly, some, if, under these conditions, my guess, probably, for this task*. Keep a forecast distinguishable from a reported observation, and a proposed mechanism distinguishable from an established cause.

An exact quote retains the supplied wording. Mark an omission with an ellipsis when it is necessary, and confirm that the removed material does not change the meaning. Label a cleaned rendering, translation or paraphrase as such. Never splice separate turns into one apparently continuous statement. Place explanatory acronym expansions outside a quotation when they were absent from its wording.

Apply the [Dutch Directness polishing test]({{ '/NETWORK/dutch-directness/#the-polishing-test' | relative_url }}) when preparing a reading copy. Preserve emphasis, repetition, pacing and directness where they tell the reader what mattered, who was being addressed or when a joke turned. Use the recording where available and mark uncertainty about tone. If capitalization marks heightened attention in the passage, keep that emphasis recoverable through the edit.

The interpretation belongs beneath the quote. Explain the connection in ordinary language, with a direct link to the relevant project page. A reader should be able to disagree with that interpretation while still recovering what the speaker said.

### Clarify before a relation governs selection {#clarify-extraction}

Use [C1 before C2]({{ '/NETWORK/c1-c2/' | relative_url }}) when uncertainty about a claim, connection or alleged loss would change what you keep, conclude or act on. A step that depends on the answer can stay provisional while the supported work continues. A correction, warning or steering move should follow the warrant the clarification actually produced.

## Locate a loss before attributing it {#loss-diagnosis}

When a passage or relationship appears missing, compare the source edition, quote record, interpretation and reviewer's actual access. Record the locations that support the finding. The [Human Insecurity report]({{ '/HUMAN-INSECURITY/' | relative_url }}) supplies a reporting-effect distinction that makes the repair more precise:

| Finding | What the record shows | Next useful move |
|---|---|---|
| Source-level absence within the checked material | The relation was not found inside a declared source perimeter | Name that perimeter; keep wider absence unresolved |
| Extraction or report failure | The source contained the relation and the derived output dropped, weakened or replaced it | Restore it and reconsider dependent interpretations |
| Placement failure | The relation is retained but separated from the claim that needs it | Restore adjacency or an explicit recovery link |
| Reviewer retrieval failure | Relevant material was available and the reviewer missed it | Recover it before maintaining an omission claim |
| Access artifact | A needed section or edition was unavailable to the reviewer | Mark the gap and seek the missing material |
| Portability finding | The relation exists but is unlikely to survive excerpting or a cold handoff | Attach its qualifier, context and recovery route to the reusable record |
| Operationalization of existing content | A new test, field or metric makes an existing relation usable | Attribute the relation and the operational proposal separately |
| Genuinely new contribution | The proposed operation is absent from the checked source and derived output | Preserve its candidate status and what would test its usefulness |

Several findings can apply to one case. Record access, location, transformation, evidence and disposition separately where that changes the repair. A possible failure without a specimen can remain a test condition.

**Apply the comparison to your own extraction.** Choose a material relationship and place the source and derived versions side by side. Check whether the extract added a premise, dropped a qualifier, changed the task, or subjected one actor to scrutiny the extractor escaped. Use the [property map](#property-map) and the relevant obstruction only where the comparison identifies a difference. Record the source and output spans, the direction of change, and which interpretation or decision needs another look.

### Why ownership of behavior matters {#ownership-of-behavior}

John Kuhles introduced **ownership of behavior** to ask what a person or system does with the responsibility that comes with a judgment. His O1/O2/O3 distinction helps the extractor preserve the explanation for a choice and the commitment, if any, to correct it.

| Pattern | What to look for in the record |
|---|---|
| **O1** | The actor invokes an instruction or authority to deflect responsibility for the action. Check who issued the instruction and where the actor had discretion. |
| **O2** | The actor makes the best judgment available to them, takes responsibility for it and commits to correcting a mistake or misjudgment. Check what changes when evidence warrants a correction. |
| **O3** | The actor appeals to social acceptability or performed virtue when asked to account for the decision. Check whether that appeal answers the specific question about what they chose and why. |

The phrase is sensitive because it makes responsibility traceable in both directions. A reviewer can have discretion over one selection while a policy owner has control over the rule that shaped it. They may have different evidence, power and capacity to repair the result. The same question applies to the extractor's decision to keep, cut or rewrite a passage. ## One quote record you can reuse {#quote-record}

| Field | Record |
|---|---|

| Dependencies and reopening | What the reading depends on and what could change it. Record any material conflict or missing source. A checked case can have no known reopening condition at present. |

## DeepEthics App Beta: related services, mutual feedback {#app-beta}

**Candidate service architecture:** the [Extraction Starter Kit]({{ '/EXTRACTION-STARTER-KIT/' | relative_url }}#quick-start), **[Topologetics™]({{ '/TOPOLOGETICS/' | relative_url }}) Summarizer** and **[DEDDA™]({{ '/DEDDA/' | relative_url }})** (Deep Ethical Drift Detector App) could operate as distinct meta-tools within a DeepEthics App. The extractor finds consequential passages; the summarizer preserves their meaning and relationships at a chosen depth; the detector compares transformations with their sources. A person can select the relevant services. These are proposed operations, not an announcement of a deployed application.

The [16 Anchor Ethics™]({{ '/NETWORK/16-anchor-ethics/' | relative_url }}) make two further requirements operational. First, each tool's finding stays challengeable: showing a difference does not authorize speaking for the participant or changing their aim. Second, cooperation must preserve independent correction. An extractor and a detector using the same mistaken speaker label can agree while both remain wrong. Record shared sources and use an independent source check when that distinction matters.

### Topologetics™ Summarizer: recoverable depth and honest loss

The proposed summarizer lets a person choose the task and reading depth, then inspect the source relationships behind a compact result. The [Epistemic Integrity and Noetic Diversity anchors]({{ '/NETWORK/16-anchor-ethics/' | relative_url }}) require material qualifiers, dissent, uncertainty and correction routes to survive. If compression would erase an answer-changing conflict, retain a longer representation or identify the unresolved loss. A combined recommendation should not silently replace the reasons that pull in different directions.

Show a purpose-specific loss note: what was condensed, what was left out and where a reader can recover it. Expanding a section should reconnect to actual source material. Feedback about a missing relation should update the summary and its dependent interpretation, then be checked on another passage. DEDDA™ may compare that repair with the source, while its own comparison remains open to correction. These are candidate output requirements for the Beta service.

### Feedback that can actually improve the tools

[Dutch Directness]({{ '/NETWORK/dutch-directness/' | relative_url }}) connects care, candid assessment, mutual dignity and correction. In a voluntary service, a person may encounter an unwelcome observation and find it useful. They may also find an error, an irrelevant intervention or a service they wish to decline. The Beta needs room for those different outcomes.

| Feedback | Follow-up operation |
|---|---|
| “I prefer this output.” | Record preference separately from source correspondence and usefulness. |
| “This is uncomfortable and useful.” | Identify the source-linked observation and what it changed. |
| “The tool is wrong; here is why.” | Compare the source, repair the affected relations and test the next comparable output. |
| “This is relevant, but I still disagree.” | Preserve the disagreement and reasons alongside the tool's assessment. |
| “This falls outside my purpose.” | Revisit the declared task and the tool's scope. |
| “I want to stop or use another mode.” | Respect that service choice; explain the actual retention boundary. |
| “I cannot decide yet.” | Retain the uncertainty and an optional route for later review. |

An uncomfortable response does not establish that the tool is right or that the person is deficient. An agreeable response does not establish accuracy either. Judge the tool through source fidelity, useful discovery, consequences, correction uptake and the user's ability to contest it. The app and its developers must be able to receive the same direct feedback they offer.

The 16 anchors are a reference field for the decisions, not a self-awarded result score.
