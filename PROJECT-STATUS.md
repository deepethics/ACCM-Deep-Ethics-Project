# Project Status

> **EARLY BETA — DRAFT — TRIAL & ERROR**

The ACCM Deep Ethics Project is currently in its initial public construction phase.

## What exists now

A large private/source archive exists upstream of this repository. Material from that archive is being reviewed selectively and may be transformed into public-facing drafts. The source archive and the public repository are not intended to be identical.

The repository is currently establishing a navigable structure before substantial public material is added.

## What this repository does not yet claim

It does not yet represent a finished framework, complete implementation method, validated professional standard, or comprehensive publication of the underlying research archive.

Individual concepts, observations, methods, experiments, and taxonomies may be useful before the larger architecture is complete. Their status should be read from their own documentation rather than inferred from their presence in the repository.

## Working status vocabulary

Materials may use labels such as:

- **BETA** — actively developing
- **DRAFT** — presentable enough to inspect, not final
- **EXPERIMENTAL** — being tested
- **UNDER REVIEW** — awaiting further examination
- **PROVISIONAL** — currently useful formulation that remains especially open to revision
- **ACCEPTED FOR CURRENT VERSION** — part of the current project representation, still corrigible
- **SUPERSEDED** — retained for provenance but replaced by a later formulation

## Development approach

The project is being built incrementally. A source may become one public document, several smaller objects, part of a larger synthesis, provenance only, or nothing public yet.

The organizing principle is:

> **Organization should follow the object rather than force the object to follow the organization.**

The public repository will grow as material is examined, represented, challenged, corrected, and placed where it belongs.
