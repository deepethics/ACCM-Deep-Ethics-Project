## Project Avalon: an expanding inventory of potential investigations {#avalon-scope-inventory}

**Purpose:** make the scope of potentially underexamined health, care, institutional and communication questions recoverable. John Kuhles requested scope thinking: identify potential topics and explain why inquiry could be worthwhile before investigating their answers in depth. GPT / Codex supplies the editorial inventory; forum contributors retain authorship of their accounts and interpretations.

**Coverage — 28 October 2026:** text and linked-source descriptions screened across pages **1–100**, posts **#1–2000**, of the supplied thread. Long reproduced articles were skimmed for topic discovery. Embedded videos, image-only testimony, external documents and clinical case verification are further access layers. Entries name investigation opportunities; inclusion supplies neither a causal finding nor a frequency estimate. The full supplied range extends to page 164. **Next unexamined page: 101.**

The method connects [Beyond Agreemurmelism]({{ '/AGREEMURMELISM/Beyond-Agreemurmelism/' | relative_url }}), [Meta Processing]({{ '/NETWORK/meta-processing/' | relative_url }}), **[TCoAw]({{ '/NETWORK/tcoaw/' | relative_url }})** (Thinking, Consciousness, Awareness), and [Topologetics]({{ '/TOPOLOGETICS/' | relative_url }}). A temporary focus remains part of a wider field; sources, directions, consequences and correction routes stay recoverable. The [1888 intelligence-properties taxonomy]({{ '/1888/' | relative_url }}) keeps perception, discrimination, relation, judgment, memory and correction interacting throughout the inquiry.

### The 10+1 as an active inquiry baseline {#avalon-ten-plus-one}

The [10+1 Metaflux]({{ '/NETWORK/ten-plus-one/#eleven-source-ingredients' | relative_url }}) governs selection and continuation together:

| Ingredient | Work it performs here |
|---|---|
| 1. Self-honesty | Record actual coverage and the media still awaiting inspection. |
| 2. Open antenna | Retain clinical, institutional, social and information-access possibilities. |
| 3. Willingness to learn | Allow new outcomes and access barriers to expand the inventory. |
| 4. No self-deception to fit in | Preserve consequential questions across popularity and institutional status. |
| 5. Genuine inquiry | Give each topic a reason and a recoverable research starting point. |
| 6. Correction on merit | Carry source clarifications and later updates alongside an original account. |
| 7. Freedom to differ | Permit unfamiliar observations to enter the field. |
| 8. Care for well-being | Include relief, function, livelihood, companionship and access to care. |
| 9. Wonderment | Notice unexpected connections before familiar categories close around them. |
| 10. Non-projection | Keep an evaluator’s expectations from replacing a particular contribution or constrained life situation. |
| 11. Forgiveness with accountability | Examine consequential conduct while preserving intent as a separate inquiry. |

### Connected investigations worth preserving {#avalon-connected-inquiries}

**A report’s journey.** Follow experience → description → clinical encounter → reporting system → platform or editorial processing → public interpretation → help, investigation or an unresolved gap. Each handoff can change what remains visible and what becomes possible. A patient’s need for care continues while a causal question remains open.

**A call for help contains several objects.** The Caroline Pover account in [post #124](https://projectavalon.net/forum4/showthread.php?p=1417874#post1417874) seeks peer contact, explanation, treatment and research participation. Keeping these four needs distinct gives institutions and artificial intelligence several constructive ways to respond.

**Context can change the ethical meaning of advice.** The extended residency exchange in [posts #346–358](https://projectavalon.net/forum4/showthread.php?113668-Vaccination-Injuries-On-Record-for-Covid19/page18#post1428358) turns an assumed travel preference into questions about work, legal status, return home and limited options. This is a useful [C1]({{ '/NETWORK/c1-c2/' | relative_url }}) clarification-process case: recover circumstances before giving advice authority.

**Preservation can support learning.** The archive supplies chronology, source recovery and opportunities to revisit unanswered questions. Connecting copies, exact products, later care, editorial changes and institutional responses would make that contribution easier to use. [Symmetric Grace]({{ '/NETWORK/asymmetric-grace/' | relative_url }}) preserves participation and places investigative responsibilities upstream too.

**Useful A.I. work:** help organize a person’s timeline with consent; preserve reported experience separately from proposed explanation; locate related questions; maintain source links and updates; track what institutions received and how they responded. These are candidate uses to develop and evaluate with affected people.

**Continuation rule:** read the next ten pages for new topics and answer-changing relationships; preserve existing IDs; add source locations to recurring topics; record media and external-document gaps; publish the delta and its coverage; then continue. A completed topic map remains corrigible. Deeper clinical, legal or causal investigation is a separately scoped next operation.
