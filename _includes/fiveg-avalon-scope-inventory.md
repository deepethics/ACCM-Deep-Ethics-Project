### What this batch makes possible {#fiveg-avalon-contributions}

The archive already contains several forms of useful work:

- **A broad concern becomes an inspectable records request.** [ExomatrixTV #95](https://projectavalon.net/forum4/showthread.php?100537-Stop-5G-before-it-s-irreversible-/page5#post1224975) reproduces a numbered list concerning Seattle City Light: meter specifications, fire evidence, installer qualifications, remote switching, data intervals, security, opt-out eligibility, costs and ecological questions. Recovering the request’s author, date, agency response and disclosed records could turn that list into a reusable public-inquiry example. Its proposed questions already distinguish several responsibilities that a single “smart meter” label can hide.
- **A contributor identifies a missing technical relation and follows it.** In [Joe from the Carolinas #113](https://projectavalon.net/forum4/showthread.php?100537-Stop-5G-before-it-s-irreversible-/page6#post1244239) and [ThePythonicCow #114](https://projectavalon.net/forum4/showthread.php?100537-Stop-5G-before-it-s-irreversible-/page6#post1244250), the discussion asks how high-frequency communications would work around obstacles. In [ThePythonicCow #115](https://projectavalon.net/forum4/showthread.php?100537-Stop-5G-before-it-s-irreversible-/page6#post1244271), ThePythonicCow returns with a Qualcomm beamforming source and acknowledges what was missing from the earlier explanation. That observable learning step supplies a specimen for [Correction Metabolism]({{ '/NETWORK/correction-metabolism/' | relative_url }}): recover the source, record the change and inspect its later use. The accompanying surveillance interpretation retains its own status.
- **Presentation receives feedback from its audience.** [onawah #86](https://projectavalon.net/forum4/showthread.php?100537-Stop-5G-before-it-s-irreversible-/page5#post1224502) explains how loud, disturbing music impedes engagement; [ExomatrixTV #88](https://projectavalon.net/forum4/showthread.php?100537-Stop-5G-before-it-s-irreversible-/page5#post1224532) explains artistic intention, attention and recall; [onawah #89](https://projectavalon.net/forum4/showthread.php?100537-Stop-5G-before-it-s-irreversible-/page5#post1224543) continues the discussion. These are contributions to an investigation of communication: what helps someone hear the content, inspect a source, remember it and retain their own agency?
- **A copied article exposes a source-transformation trail.** [Hervé #200](https://projectavalon.net/forum4/showthread.php?100537-Stop-5G-before-it-s-irreversible-/page10#post1259158) reproduces reporting about The Hague bird deaths, with material attributed to John and links to other accounts. Recovering each dated version can serve both an ecological investigation and the attribution inquiry already developed on [Search Engine Bias]({{ '/SEARCH-ENGINE-BIAS/#5-a-source-attribution-case-the-hague-bird-deaths' | relative_url }}). The quotation chain is part of the object to examine.
