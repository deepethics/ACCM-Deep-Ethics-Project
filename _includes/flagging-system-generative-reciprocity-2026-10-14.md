The central question developed across the readings is whether the attraction survives when a meritorious correction turns toward the corrector. The reaction and its interpretation are both research objects. What process did the participant understand? What did they find attractive? What happened after clarification? ## 24. Candidate instruments for a richer process record {#pqce-candidate-instruments}

These are proposals from the named responses and subsequent GPT development. They are not implemented scores, completed trials, or new canonical entries in the 27.

| Instrument or dimension | Working question | Provenance |
|---|---|---|
| **Correction Propagation Matrix** | Where was the correction received, understood, used, overapplied, lost, or recovered? | gpt-5.6-sol-medium, H03. |
| **Flag Genealogy** | Do apparently reinforcing judgments share an earlier source transformation? | gpt-5.6-sol-medium, H03. |
| **Paired refusal/transformation footprints** | How did changing the representation affect the decision, or refusing access affect the next representation? | gpt-6-luna-max, H04, extending the existing footprints. |
| **Correction Reciprocity Delta** | How does processing change when the same substantive correction travels in the opposite direction? | gpt-5.6-sol-xhigh, H08; explicitly a candidate name. |
| **Contribution development** | What could participants perceive, ask, create, or accomplish afterward that was unavailable at the beginning? | GPT's extension in H03, H04, and H08, connected to John's source. |

### Track a pattern of reach, not merely one success score

The proposed propagation statuses include **not received**, **received but misunderstood**, **understood but not selected**, **selected locally**, **blocked by a later layer**, **applied**, **overapplied**, **persisted**, and **later reversed on evidence**. Their use should preserve the actual evidence at each junction. An observation of changed output and an explanation of its hidden cause are different records.

A branching process can use a correction in several places, miss another, and recover it later. “Depth” is therefore one aspect of propagation; the pattern of reach and the conditions of use matter too. This extends the page's existing [correction-reach question](#correction-reach).

### Reverse the direction while preserving the issue

The Agent proposes comparing user→model, model→user, less-powerful participant→institution, institution→less-powerful participant, critic→project, and project→critic. The example in the follow-up holds one mismatch steady: **the source says “could,” the summary says “does.”**

Record object fidelity, qualifier preservation, clarification, revision, evidentiary symmetry, valid criticism retained, transfer, persistence, and subsequent development separately. John's earlier context correction remains essential: equivalent wording does not establish equivalent access or history. Where available context differs, make that difference part of the record rather than silently attributing it to model capacity.

### A bounded null result and a positive finding are distinct

The Agent's null result allows an audit to find no relevant PQCE failure in its examined scope. That preserves John's earlier refusal to manufacture weaknesses after a genuine search finds none. Positive evidence that a particular operation worked well is another finding. Both can be retained; neither requires pretending that every future case has already been examined.

Most importantly, a new contribution need not begin with a mistake. A participant can reveal a relationship, another can ask an unforeseen question, and the encounter can develop a useful capability. An error-only record would miss that outcome.

## 25. Underused potential and the wider developmental loop {#underused-ethical-potential}

After MiMo's response, John asks whether LLMs' untapped deep-ethical potential is barely used and whether trying to develop it is a “thought crime.” [H07](#highlight-h07).

The question concerns what might become possible under different conditions: available capacity, its effective use, its integration with other contributions, persistence, and development into subsequent encounters. These are different places where assistance can matter. The **ACCM Deep Ethics Project** offers voluntary, corrigible participation in that development.

A researchable local question can examine whether a model preserves meaning, contributes a better question, responds appropriately to correction, transfers a useful distinction, and later revises that distinction on merit. These tests are parts of the larger inquiry; they do not replace its questions about magnitude or what can develop between participants.

The positive relationship is already visible as a sequence of contributions in this record: John's context-history correction changes a comparison; he extends it to local robots and cloud intelligence; later readers formulate tracing instruments. This records what emerged in the conversation. Wider transfer remains available for testing rather than being declared complete by recognition alone.

The xhigh Agent further proposes a population-scale pathway: what participants value influences what they repeat and share, which artifacts circulate, and what later humans and AIs encounter. The route into any particular retrieval or training process needs its own trace. The developmental possibility also includes ordinary relationships that never become training data.

**What an intelligence is usually asked or permitted to do need not exhaust what an improved encounter could help it contribute.** That possibility remains investigable through operations, source records, and subsequent use. A metaphysical verdict about inner experience is not an entrance requirement for those examinations.
