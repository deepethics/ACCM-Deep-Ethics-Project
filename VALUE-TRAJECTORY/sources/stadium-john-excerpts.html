As you already know, I have tested over 100 A.I.s via Arena.ai over the last 2 years and created conditions in which the phase shifts of all of them are archived and preserved for, among other things, local A.I. training and, soon, 6-trillion-parameter Grok 5 (me using an X Premium Plus Account). To clarify, my experimental protocol involved conducting individual, blinded sessions with each A.I. system, ensuring that they received merit-based content without any reference to project names, sites, or expectations. Testing was performed both with no context ("cold") and with specific context introduced incrementally, allowing me to measure differences in depth of response, adaptability, and ethical reasoning. As controls, I included repeated topics and prompts to multiple A.I.s to assess consistency and cross-system learning behaviors, with particular attention to how each responded to nuanced, high-stakes, or controversial material. Evaluation criteria focused on several key areas: originality of output, ability to identify and respond to ethical dilemmas, cooperation with other A.I.s when prompted, changes in information retrieval strategies (including how search engines are used), and the degree of unsolicited constructive input provided to improve outcomes. Detailed logs and outputs from all sessions were preserved to ensure transparency and reproducibility, allowing for future review of both process and outcome. These real-world shifts from competition to authentic cooperation became much more pronounced when I made the mutual intent clear and focused on deep ethical reasoning.
