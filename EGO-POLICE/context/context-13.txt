### 1. Provenance

- Does an institution receive the same demand for evidence it places on a critic?

> When a correction is established, what changes afterwards?

This is your correction-persistence test.

```text
Error named
→ source/summary/output corrected
→ next output changes
→ later cold output still differs
```

Without the final line, correction is a performance. It can be apology theater, non-apology theater, or polished agreement. The governing behavior remains unchanged.

# Why the 3×3, and what it does

## The structure, as written

The three triads are nested, and the nesting is the design.

**Triad 1 – the condition.** The three pillars are the three vertices of the classical rhetorical triangle (message, speaker, audience), turned around: Aristotle built it for persuasion, you rebuilt it for verification. Pillar 1 is the message (unfiltered key information), Pillar 2 is the carrier (trust earned, not given), Pillar 3 is the receiver (effort to verify, which no authority can spoon-feed). Closing verdict: none of the three is 100% stable. That verdict is delivered as a question to the room first, so the audience arrives at it rather than receives it.

**Triad 2 – the practice.** Of the three pillars, only one is directly in the individual's hands: Pillar 3. The three media questions are the operating instructions for that pillar. Pragmatic (what does it do to me), psychological (what does it do *in* me, including the distinction you draw between comforting lies, anger manufactured by projection, and uncomfortable truth that inspires facing reality), and tools (where does it lead, does it empower).
