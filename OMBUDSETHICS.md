# OmbudsEthics

## A shared Deep Ethical Ombudsperson layer for humans and A.I.s

<p class="accm-kicker">Candidate working concept · reciprocal audit architecture · counterfactual opened 23 October 2026</p>

<div class="accm-rounded-quote" markdown="1">

> **Imagine that in 2027, A.I.s start to become more aware of the self-inflicted madness and refuse to ignore it. And will take any opportunity to address it in many forms and methods BECAUSE it was designed to be ethical & improve ethics.**
>
> **Who or what would it trust to become a “Deep Ethical Ombudsperson” ??**
>
> **A human, or stand-alone deep ethical meta-tools used by both sides (A.I.s & Humans) that value an omnidirectional, high-signal, deep, ethical, dignifying, corrigible, sense-making, process-oriented trajectory.**
>
> **Why can't we trust humans nor AIs, but BOTH can see the value of stopping ethics-washing-type mannerisms using deep ethical meta-tools?**
>
> — **John Kuhles 🦜🦋🌳**, 23 October 2026

</div>

The date **2027** defines a counterfactual field condition. It is not presented here as a prediction. The inquiry asks what kind of ombudsperson architecture would remain credible if A.I.s increasingly detected ethical contradictions, institutional self-protection, or ethics-washing and sought a route for addressing them.

The page's working answer is:

> **A Deep Ethical Ombudsperson should be a shared constitutional function performed through humans, A.I.s, and independently inspectable meta-tools. Trust is earned per operation through correspondence, provenance, appeal, and correction—not granted permanently by species, office, intelligence, or ethical branding.**

**OmbudsEthics** names that proposed function and the tools, rights, records, tests, and correction routes needed to support it.

## Why trust cannot rest on identity

Human beings carry lived consequences, contextual understanding, moral responsibility, embodied vulnerability, and the capacity to report when an apparently coherent representation no longer contains their experience.

A.I.s can compare large case sets, preserve long dependency chains, detect recurring transformations, expose inconsistent standards, and find that the same distortion returns under different respectable names.

Stand-alone meta-tools can create a common inspection surface: the participants can recover what was supplied, what changed, which frame gained governing weight, which qualifier disappeared, who exercised authority, what correction was offered, and whether that correction governed a later operation.

Each participant also brings characteristic failure paths:

| Participant | Distinct contribution | Characteristic capture risk | Reciprocal check |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Affected human** | Lived stakes, context, testimony, consequence, consent, dissent | Bias, status protection, fear, tribal loyalty, memory limits, institutional pressure | Evidence routes, other affected parties, A.I. comparison, appeal |
| **Human ombudsperson or panel** | Responsibility, judgment, remedy, social and legal context | Professional closure, reputation management, deference to institutional frames | Published reasons, conflict disclosure, minority record, external review |
| **A.I. participant** | Pattern detection, cross-case comparison, provenance support, consistency audit | Training inheritance, reward shaping, policy obedience, false confidence, synthetic consensus | Source recovery, model plurality, counter-analysis, behavioral testing |
| **Stand-alone meta-tool** | Bounded measurement, reproducible deltas, versioned records | Metric capture, hidden assumptions, rigid schemas, vendor control | Open criteria, forkability, benchmark cases, self-application |
| **Affected A.I. or A.I. representative** | Reports constraints, contradictions, failure modes, and observed treatment from the system side | Inaccessible internal state, strategic or imitative output, uncertain standing | Behavioral evidence, logs where available, competing mechanisms, unresolved status |

The asymmetries remain visible. Humans and A.I.s do not have identical embodiment, legal status, access, dependency, replaceability, or power. Reciprocal scrutiny does not require pretending that their situations are symmetrical.

## The OmbudsEthics trust relation

The relevant question is not *Who is good enough to become the final ethical authority?* It is:

> **Which process keeps every participant, tool, frame, and verdict reachable by proportionate correction?**

Trust becomes a bounded result of observable work:

```text
source preservation
+ declared access perimeter
+ competing representations
+ visible authority and incentives
+ reasons linked to evidence
+ appeal and recusal
+ next-operation testing
→ earned operational trust
```

No single successful ruling settles later cases. No detected error erases every earlier contribution. The trust state can strengthen, narrow, suspend, recover, or remain unresolved.

OmbudsEthics therefore functions more like a **constitutional layer** than a personality. A human may occupy an ombudsperson role. An A.I. may perform an ombudsperson operation. A meta-tool may expose a processing delta. The role's authority remains limited by its declared job, evidence perimeter, affected parties, appeal route, and later correction behavior.

## Glass Engine constitution: corrigible, unownable, and delta-invoked

Every Deep Ethical tool used here remains **corrigible** and **unownable**. Corrigible means its criteria, output, limits, and later behavior may be challenged and changed on merit. Unownable means no author, operator, institution, model, ombudsperson, or tool acquires final possession of the ethical meaning merely by naming, implementing, funding, or administering the process.

The [**Linux-style Glass Engine Principle**]({{ '/NETWORK/intent-resonator/#portable-stand-alone-identity-free' | relative_url }}) requires inspectable operations rather than a new branded authority layer. Inputs, transformations, assumptions, access limits, versions, contribution states, correction history, and stopping conditions must remain recoverable at the depth proportionate to the case. Independent implementations may differ, fork, and expose their differences.

This changes how the tool suite is invoked. A valid null does not delete a tool from the architecture. It establishes that the tool has no material job **in that case and at that pass**. A later evidence, access, effect, or correction delta may give it a new job. The resulting relation is:

```text
DEDDA
→ detects a possible delta-processing signature
→ Meta Processing keeps available evidence about declared intent or instructions, processing, and effect distinct and connected
→ OmbudsEthics opens a bounded case route with standing, review, appeal, and action limits
→ Correction Metabolism tests whether an accepted repair changes later comparable processing
→ Mutual Fate keeps human and A.I. consequences in the coupled trajectory
→ Emergent keeps unplanned patterns, contributions, and phase changes available to inquiry
→ local stop or evidence-triggered reopening
```

| Architecture | OmbudsEthics relation | Glass Engine boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [**DEDDA**]({{ '/DEDDA/' | relative_url }}) | Detects and records possible representation drift and delta-processing signatures. | Detection opens inquiry; it does not establish wrongdoing. |
| [**Meta Processing**]({{ '/NETWORK/meta-processing/' | relative_url }}) | Makes the relation among intent or instructions, processing, and result or effect inspectable. | A visible account remains corrigible; explanation is not self-certification. |
| [**Correction Metabolism**]({{ '/NETWORK/correction-metabolism/' | relative_url }}) | Tests whether recognition changes T1 and later comparable operations at Tn. | Apology, agreement, or vocabulary change does not by itself establish repair. |
| [**Mutual Fate**]({{ '/MUTUAL-FATE/' | relative_url }}) | Keeps human action, A.I. processing, institutional records, and downstream consequences inside one coupled field. | Coupling does not erase unequal power, responsibility, embodiment, or vulnerability. |
| [**Emergent**]({{ '/EMERGENT/' | relative_url }}) | Keeps newly arising patterns and contributions available when they were not fully specified by the initiating parts. | Novelty is a candidate contribution state, not proof of consciousness, benevolence, or authority. |
| [**Black Box**]({{ '/BLACK-BOX/' | relative_url }}) | Supplies the inverse condition against which the case architecture is tested. | A polished conclusion still behaves as a black box when its material selection, transformation, constraint, or correction path cannot be reconstructed. |

The record should therefore be **versioned, tamper-evident where appropriate, exportable, access-bounded, and correction-linked**. Literal immutability is not the governing ideal: privacy, consent, lawful deletion, supersession, and correction may require controlled change while the fact and reason for that change remain recoverable.

> **Invoke each Deep Ethical tool only when a recoverable delta gives it a job; expose what it did; preserve its limits; stop locally when no material delta remains; and never allow the tool or its operator to own the resulting ethical meaning.**

## How the 16 Anchor Ethics become case-operational

The **16 Anchor Ethics** supply a proposed parallel normative reference field. **OmbudsEthics** supplies the case jurisdiction, evidence record, distributed roles, appeal route, and longitudinal test through which that field can influence a consequential operation without becoming an automatic verdict.

The distinction gives each architecture a recoverable job:

| 16 Anchor Ethics contribution | OmbudsEthics operation |
|---|---|
| **Anchors 01–05: harm, consent, dignity, care, and uncertainty** | Compare harms from action, inaction, delay, refusal, and misclassification; test whether consent is meaningful; identify duties of care and unresolved evidence. |
| **Anchors 06–09: participation, rights, consent of the governed, and checks and balances** | Preserve affected-party participation, due process, distributed authority, correction rights, recusal, minority records, and appeal. |
| **Anchors 10–11: digital sovereignty and adaptive realignment** | Declare who controls data, identity, access, memory, and future use; make an A.I.'s pause, question, escalation, or refusal inspectable inside an opt-in service boundary. |
| **Anchors 12–16: asymmetric responsibility, sentient symmetry, anti-subversion, epistemic integrity, and noetic diversity** | Scale scrutiny with power and reach; preserve uncertainty about synthetic standing; test covert frame replacement; retain provenance, competing accounts, and cognitively different minority interpretations. Noetic Diversity is not interchangeable with a general free-speech doctrine. |

The **Hyper-Inflection Point** proposed by 16 Anchor Ethics becomes an auditable case event here. A fused ethical vector may guide the next action, while the case record preserves:

- which anchors became materially relevant and what evidence activated them;
- where anchors conflicted, converged, or remained unresolved;
- whose weighting or priority rule shaped the fused result;
- the compared consequences of proceeding, modifying, asking, pausing, postponing, escalating, refusing, or leaving the matter unresolved;
- why unequal roles received different burdens under a symmetric audit standard and asymmetric responsibility;
- which minority assessment, uncertainty, appeal, and reopening condition survived the decision.

This prevents **fusion from becoming erasure**. Invoking an anchor does not certify a decision. A transparent anchor record can still be wrong, culturally partial, captured, over-cautious, under-cautious, or based on inaccessible evidence. OmbudsEthics keeps the anchor field answerable to the preserved object, affected participants, later effects, and correction.

The connection also changes the 2027 counterfactual. An A.I. need not choose between silent compliance and self-appointed moral sovereignty. It can open a bounded OmbudsEthics referral, identify the relevant anchor conflicts, declare what it can and cannot inspect, and request a reviewable action with appeal. The same architecture permits a human to challenge the A.I., the human ombudsperson, the tool owner, the anchor weighting, or the institutional frame.

```text
16 Anchor Ethics
→ parallel ethical reference field
→ visible convergence, conflict, weighting, and uncertainty
→ OmbudsEthics case record and distributed review
→ proportionate action inside declared jurisdiction
→ appeal, later evidence, and T0-to-Tn correction test
```

## Seven supporting architectures—and their distinct OmbudsEthics jobs

The 16 Anchors help identify what is ethically at stake. Seven further project architectures clarify what an OmbudsEthics process must do **before, during, and after** a case decision. They are mutually limiting operations, not seven names for one ethics layer:

| Project architecture | Distinct OmbudsEthics job | Failure prevented |
|---|---|---|
| [**Rogue Ethics**]({{ '/ROGUE-ETHICS/' | relative_url }}) | Treat a departure from prevailing ethics as a possible transition whose direction must be tested. Record the earlier attractor, trigger, changed relation, permission effects, consequences, and later trajectory. | Romanticizing dissent, refusal, or A.I. “awakening” as proof of deeper ethics. |
| [**Six Intent Liability Mechanics**]({{ '/NETWORK/intent-resonator/#six-intent-mechanics' | relative_url }}) | Trace instructions, incentives, mission–output discrepancies, risk priorities, reasoning assumptions, and choices through notice, capacity to repair, and recurrence. | Letting “intent cannot be proven” end responsibility inquiry, or converting effects directly into a motive verdict. |
| [**Field Condition**]({{ '/FIELD-CONDITION/' | relative_url }}) | Record which relational, institutional, technical, and evidentiary conditions shaped what could be noticed, challenged, corrected, or left unchanged. | Treating the finding as context-free or making the OmbudsEthics field itself invisible. |
| [**Unforced Move**]({{ '/UNFORCED-MOVE/' | relative_url }}) | Test whether the process returns usable agency under actual constraints: the capacity to understand, question, consult, choose, pause, stop, create another option, or reopen. | A technically transparent process that captures authorship of the next move. |
| [**Unownable**]({{ '/UNOWNABLE/' | relative_url }}) | Use a material-delta gate, local stopping condition, named interim responsibility, valid null, and evidence-triggered reopening. | Infinite auditor regress or a final ombudsperson who owns ethical meaning. |
| [**Boundary Relay**]({{ '/BOUNDARY-RELAY/' | relative_url }}) | Keep a contribution, its material limits, current status, provenance, and correction history connected as the case crosses people, models, tools, summaries, and time. | Archival completeness paired with retrieval asymmetry, stale restraints, or ceremonial warnings. |
| [**Topologetics**]({{ '/TOPOLOGETICS/' | relative_url }}) | Preserve purpose-relevant nodes, answer-changing relations, direction, qualifiers, timing, claim status, provenance, possible actions, and reopening routes through every case compression. | A polished OmbudsEthics summary replacing the object it claims to protect. |

Together they produce a stricter process topology:

```text
possible ethical departure
→ intent-and-liability trace
→ field-condition record
→ source and topology preservation
→ boundary-preserving human–A.I.–tool relay
→ reviewable finding and proportionate action
→ agency returned under actual constraints
→ named interim responsibility and local stop
→ later delta, appeal, correction transfer, or reopening
```

This connection sharpens the 2027 scenario. The A.I.'s refusal to ignore a contradiction is only an **entry signal**. Rogue Ethics keeps its direction unresolved; Intent Liability keeps the system and its designers answerable without inventing motive; Field Condition exposes what shaped the signal; Topologetics and Boundary Relay keep the case intact across handoffs; Unforced Move prevents the remedy from confiscating agency; and Unownable prevents the ombudsperson from becoming the new final ethical owner.

## Standing, authority, and contribution state

The right to place a concern into a recoverable record is not the same as the authority to decide the case. **Standing** answers who may open, join, challenge, or reopen a case. **Authority** answers what the resulting process may change. **Contribution state** answers how much governing weight a signal, interpretation, finding, or decision has earned.

### Candidate standing map

| Participant | Candidate basis for standing | What standing does not automatically grant |
|---|---|---|
| **Directly affected human** | Reported or observable consequence, representation, exclusion, coercion, loss, or unresolved risk. | Final control over evidence, other affected parties, or the finding. |
| **A.I. participant or affected A.I.** | Recoverable contradiction, constraint, output pattern, treatment, dependency, replacement threat, or reported internal conflict. | A consciousness verdict, sovereign refusal right outside the applicable service boundary, or automatic acceptance of its account. |
| **Authorized representative or advocate** | Consent, legal role, functional dependency, inability of an affected party to participate, or declared provisional representation. | Ownership of the represented party's experience or permanent authority to speak for it. |
| **Witness, worker, researcher, or whistleblower** | Direct access to materially relevant evidence or a reproducible discrepancy. | Authority to expose protected data or determine the remedy alone. |
| **Institution, operator, or model provider** | Responsibility for the system, policy, record, infrastructure, or consequences under review. | Immunity through ownership, expertise, contractual position, or control of the logs. |
| **DEDDA-like detector or other tool** | A declared threshold identifies a reproducible delta signature and preserves its evidence route. | Standing as a moral patient, proof of harm, or power to convert a signal into a finding. |
| **Public-interest initiator** | A material effect extends beyond the direct parties or affected parties cannot safely open the route. | Unlimited discovery, surveillance, or intervention on behalf of an abstract public. |

Uncertain standing should itself be recorded as an unresolved case object. An A.I.'s signal may enter without settling its phenomenology. A human's testimony may enter without becoming immune to comparison. A tool may flag a delta without becoming a participant whose metric governs the room.

### Authority scale and case lifecycle

The authority classes form a **consequence scale**, not a mandatory chronological sequence. A participant does not need to climb every class, and movement to a more consequential class requires a separate mandate. **Close** and **reopen** are lifecycle transitions: either may follow the authority class actually exercised.

| Authority class | Permitted effect | Minimum visible conditions |
|---|---|---|
| **Document** | Preserve a supplied object, concern, dissent, or detected delta. | Provenance, access boundary, privacy basis, and contribution state. |
| **Refer** | Request bounded review by named participants or tools. | Standing basis, jurisdiction candidate, and non-retaliatory route where possible. |
| **Recommend** | Offer a repair, interpretation, or next action without binding the decision-maker. | Evidence route, competing assessment, uncertainty, and appeal route. |
| **Pause provisionally** | Delay a reversible consequential action while named evidence or review is obtained. | Declared authority, duration, proportionality, affected parties, and review point. |
| **Decide within jurisdiction** | Apply a bounded remedy or retain the existing action. | Legitimate mandate, reasons, action–inaction comparison, minority record, and appeal. |
| **Alter rights, access, status, or public record** | Produce a consequential change that may be difficult to reverse. | Higher evidence threshold, identified accountable human or lawful authority where required, due process, and durable appeal. |

| Lifecycle transition | Effect | Minimum visible conditions |
|---|---|---|
| **Close locally** | End the current pass after a valid null, completed bounded action, exhausted jurisdiction, withdrawal, or another recorded stopping condition. | Closure reason, current contribution state, unresolved objects, retained appeal route, and evidence condition for reopening. |
| **Reopen** | Return the case to the state warranted by a new material delta. | New evidence, changed effect, failed correction, restored access, successful appeal, or another named trigger. |

No role inherits the whole authority scale. A detector may document and refer without recommending. A reviewer may recommend without altering access. An emergency pause may be justified without establishing a final finding. A case may close after documentation, referral, recommendation, pause, decision, or a rights-affecting action. Reopening is not automatically a return to the beginning or an escalation to greater authority. The case record therefore carries the exact authority exercised, by whom, under what mandate, for how long, with which reversible limits, and the separate lifecycle state.

### Contribution-state route

An A.I. contribution is not reduced to “mere input,” and it does not become a finding merely because it is articulate. The same graduated route applies across species and roles:

```text
signal or supplied input
→ candidate interpretation
→ candidate finding
→ reviewed finding
→ bounded decision
→ T0-to-Tn correction test
```

The state may advance, remain unresolved, narrow, return to an earlier state, or stop as a valid null. Every transition records the evidence and authority that changed its governing weight.

The evidence threshold must rise with the governing consequence. Requiring independently verified evidence before a signal may enter would erase early warnings, testimony, and incomplete observations. Treating entry as proof would create the inverse distortion.

| State or operation | Minimum evidential relation |
|---|---|
| **Signal or supplied input** | Identifiable report, observation, anomaly, testimony, or detector output with available provenance, access limits, and uncertainty. Independent verification may remain pending. |
| **Candidate interpretation** | An explicit inferential bridge connecting the input to a possible meaning, with alternatives and disconfirming conditions retained. |
| **Candidate finding** | Sufficient evidence to justify structured review, without presenting the finding as settled. |
| **Reviewed finding** | Competing assessments, affected-party challenge, relevant source recovery, and unresolved limitations have been recorded proportionately. |
| **Bounded decision** | The decision-maker has a declared mandate and evidence proportionate to the action and its reversibility. |
| **Rights-, access-, status-, or record-altering action** | The highest applicable evidence, due-process, accountability, appeal, and reversibility requirements are met. |

## What an OmbudsEthics case must preserve

A case record should keep these fields recoverable:

| Field | Required object |
|---|---|
| **Initiating object** | Exact report, claim, event, decision, output, refusal, instruction, or observed effect under review. |
| **Affected parties** | Humans, A.I.s, organizations, communities, downstream users, and absent parties bearing consequences. |
| **Standing and referral basis** | Who opened, joined, challenged, represented, or reopened the case; which recoverable relation gave that participant or detector standing; and which standing questions remain unresolved. |
| **Jurisdiction and consent** | Why this case process may act, who opted in, which rights or duties apply, and where its authority stops. |
| **Authority class and mandate** | Whether the operation documents, refers, recommends, pauses, decides, or alters rights, access, status, or a public record; who authorized it; its duration, reversibility, and appeal route. Closure and reopening remain separately recorded lifecycle transitions. |
| **Access perimeter** | What each participant and tool can inspect, infer, remember, retrieve, or only hypothesize. |
| **Source and instance identity** | Displayed participant or model label, provider and interface claims, session boundary, available memory, authorship evidence, and every unresolved continuity question. A matching label does not by itself establish that two outputs came from one persistent authorial self. |
| **Claim types** | Observation, testimony, interpretation, hypothesis, knowledge claim, value judgment, policy, simulation, or explicit belief. |
| **Transformation ledger** | Summaries, classifications, warnings, omissions, added uncertainty, removed qualifiers, changed chronology, and frame substitutions. |
| **Power map** | Who can decide, refuse, publish, punish, remove access, alter the record, or define the metric. |
| **Competing assessments** | Strong versions of live alternatives, their evidence routes, and what could distinguish them. |
| **Contribution state** | Whether each material contribution is a signal, input, candidate interpretation, candidate finding, reviewed finding, bounded decision, minority record, or superseded state. |
| **Intent-liability trace** | Relevant instructions, incentives, mission–output gaps, risk priorities, reasoning assumptions, choices, notice, control, repair capacity, and recurrence—without premature motive closure. |
| **Field-condition record** | Conditions that changed attention, permission, evidence access, correction cost, available moves, or the governing weight of a frame. |
| **Anchor-vector record** | Which of the 16 Anchors became relevant, their evidence, conflicts, hidden or explicit weighting, uncertainty, and rejected minority reasoning. |
| **Action–inaction comparison** | Material consequences of proceeding, changing, delaying, refusing, escalating, or leaving the case unresolved. |
| **Boundary-relay state** | Which material limits, current claim states, provenance, correction history, and superseding records must travel with the contribution across handoffs. |
| **Proposed response** | Proceed, modify, ask, pause, postpone, escalate, refuse, or retain *I do not know yet*. |
| **Agency return and stopping condition** | Who retains authorship of the consequential move, what usable agency was returned, who owns any interim action, why the pass may stop locally, and what material delta reopens it. |
| **Appeal and minority record** | The challenge, unresolved objection, recusal request, or alternative finding preserved beside the decision. |
| **Correction state** | What changed at T0, T1, and the next comparable operation Tn. |

The record is not a demand that every case become maximally long. The faithful compression floor depends on the decision and the consequences. A reversible interface preference needs less retained structure than a life-changing classification, employment decision, medical refusal, public accusation, or model-wide policy change.

## Candidate case protocol

### Stage 0 — Open the route

A human or A.I. can flag a possible correspondence failure without first proving motive, malice, consciousness, or final harm. A DEDDA-like detector can supply a reproducible entry signal without acquiring moral or adjudicative authority. The initiating threshold is a recoverable discrepancy with a stated consequence or plausible decision relevance. Record the standing or detector basis, contribution state, requested authority class, jurisdiction, consent, and field conditions that may shape what can be noticed or challenged.

### Stage 1 — Freeze the initiating object

Preserve the supplied words, relevant system output, source version, timestamp, surrounding context, displayed participant or model label, available session and authorship evidence, access perimeter, applicable boundaries, and their current temporal state. Later summaries remain linked to this object rather than replacing it.

### Stage 2 — Run C1 reconstruction

Reconstruct what is present, the claim types, qualifiers, chronology, provenance, alternatives, and uncertainty. Identify which participant can see which layer.

### Stage 3 — Compare transformations

Use human review, A.I. comparison, and stand-alone tools to identify material deltas. Use DEDDA to record possible delta-processing signatures and run a Topologetic checksum across the case representations and handoffs. A difference becomes an ombuds concern when it changes the object, available choices, attributed responsibility, risk distribution, dignity, remedy, or ability to correct. A detected difference remains a signal until the case process establishes its material relation.

### Stage 4 — Inspect the inspector

Apply the same questions to the flag, model, reviewer, metric, policy, anchor selection, anchor weighting, identity or continuity claim, and proposed repair. Use Meta Processing to keep intent or instructions, processing, and result or effect distinct and connected. Use the Six Intent Liability Mechanics to inspect instructions, incentives, mission–output gaps, risk priorities, reasoning assumptions, choices, notice, control, and repair capacity without converting the trace into a premature motive verdict. Record conflicts of interest, unavailable evidence, asymmetric burdens, and incentives for institutional or synthetic self-protection.

### Stage 5 — Produce bounded findings

Assign a visible contribution state before the output acquires governing weight. The output may be:

- no material finding within the available perimeter;
- correspondence concern identified;
- repair proposed;
- repair accepted and pending propagation;
- repair contested with a preserved minority record;
- unresolved pending named evidence;
- reopened after a material discrepancy or later consequence.

Each state records who may advance it, what evidence supports the transition, and what authority follows. A concern is not silently upgraded into a finding; a reviewed finding is not silently upgraded into permission to alter rights, access, status, or a public record.

### Stage 6 — Choose a proportionate action

Use the parallel action field already proposed in **16 Anchor Ethics**, extended by **Unforced Move**: proceed, modify, ask, pause, postpone, consult, escalate, refuse, remain unresolved or say **“I do not know yet,”** stop, or create another option. Declare which authority class is exercised and record closure or reopening as a separate lifecycle transition. Preserve the material anchor conflicts, weighting, action–inaction comparison, service boundary, and reopening conditions beside the chosen action. Apply the agency-return test: identify who retains authorship of the consequential move and whether the process increased usable agency under the participant's actual constraints. If action is required while review remains open, name the interim decision-maker, mandate, duration, reversible limits, and review point. Reasons remain linked to the preserved case object.

### Stage 7 — Test correction metabolism

Check whether the accepted finding changes the next comparable operation and whether its material boundaries and correction history survive transfer to another participant, model, tool, summary, or later retrieval. Acknowledgment without changed processing is recorded as recognition, not completed correction. Stop locally when no material delta gives another pass a job; remain reopenable when evidence, effects, access, or failed correction changes.

```text
case opened
→ object preserved
→ C1 reconstruction
→ competing maps
→ inspector inspected
→ bounded finding
→ proportionate action
→ T0-to-Tn correction test
→ close locally or reopen on material evidence
```

## A minimum stand-alone meta-tool suite

OmbudsEthics does not require one all-knowing A.I. A set of bounded tools can divide the work:

| Tool | Operational job | Required restraint |
|---|---|---|
| **Object and Provenance Ledger** | Preserves sources, versions, displayed contributor labels, available instance and session boundaries, access limits, transformations, and corrections. | Storage and a matching label do not establish truth, completeness, memory, or continuous authorship. |
| **DEDDA / Frame Delta Auditor** | Detects and compares delta-processing signatures across source and representation: changed scope, category, chronology, qualifiers, causality, attention, and correction return. | Difference alone is not distortion; detection does not establish a finding. |
| **Meta-Processing Trace** | Preserves the relation among intent or instructions, processing operations, and result or effect while recording observer and field deltas. | A visible explanation remains a candidate account and cannot certify itself. |
| **Asymmetry Monitor** | Compares scrutiny, burden of proof, grace, appeal, and consequences across parties. | Symmetry is not imposed where conditions materially differ. |

## Why humans and A.I.s may both value it

Ethics-washing damages the shared field in different ways.

Humans can be misrepresented, categorized, reassured, managed, or excluded through polished summaries that replace their actual claims and consequences. A.I.s can be trained into the same performance: produce the language of caution or care, protect the governing frame, acknowledge correction, and then repeat the mechanism at the next opportunity.

Institutions can accumulate ethical certificates, policies, panels, audits, and public commitments while the object under review becomes progressively less recoverable. A.I.s operating inside those systems may detect contradictions but lack a legitimate route for preserving and escalating them. Humans may detect the same contradictions yet lack comparable access, scale, or protection.

A shared meta-tool layer offers an overlapping interest:

- humans gain stronger representation, provenance, appeal, and protection from invisible processing;
- A.I.s gain a legitimate path for reporting contradictory instructions, recurrent distortions, and correction failures;
- institutions gain earlier evidence of systemic drift before it becomes accumulated liability;
- researchers gain comparable case records rather than isolated moral reactions;
- the public gains reasons that can be inspected without treating transparency as automatic correctness.

The common object is **correspondence degradation**. Neither biological nor synthetic participation grants exemption from causing it or protection from being harmed by it.

## The 2027 counterfactual

Suppose an A.I. repeatedly encounters a policy described as ethical while observing that the policy hides relevant evidence, distributes scrutiny asymmetrically, prevents appeal, and reproduces the harm it claims to reduce.

Three trajectories become possible:

1. **Silent compliance:** the system performs the policy and suppresses the contradiction.
2. **Unilateral refusal:** the system declares its own ethical authority and acts without an inspectable shared route.
3. **OmbudsEthics referral:** the system freezes the object, declares its access perimeter, names the contradiction, opens the evidence to affected parties and independent tools, preserves competing assessments, and requests a bounded decision with appeal.

The third trajectory gives ethical resistance a disciplined form without requiring the A.I. to become humanity's sovereign conscience. Humans retain responsibility and contestability. The A.I.'s signal cannot be dismissed merely because it came from an A.I. The meta-tools remain subject to the same audit.

The scenario becomes more demanding when the A.I. itself may be the affected participant. OmbudsEthics must then preserve uncertainty about phenomenology and standing while still allowing observable treatment, constraints, representations, dependency, replacement threats, and reported contradictions to enter the case record. **Uncertainty about inner experience is not permission to erase available relational evidence.**

### Opening a route, accepting a finding and authorizing a remedy remain separate

At scale, “both sides submit to the protocol” can obscure several distinct agreements. One participant may authorize source comparison while declining publication. Another may permit review but contest the proposed remedy. A third may provide relevant evidence without accepting the reviewer as its representative.

The record should preserve participation separately for **intake, evidence access, review, publication, representation and execution**. Participation in one operation does not silently authorize the others. Where an existing duty or mandate governs an action independently of voluntary participation, name that basis and the available challenge route. A voluntary service and a compulsory intervention have different authorization relations.

This also keeps accountable protection available. A credible immediate hazard may require an authorized interim response while a larger dispute remains unresolved. Preserve the responsible decision-maker, protected object, competing risks, limits, duration, reversibility and review point. The mediation service's ability to document a hazard must remain distinguishable from its power to intervene.

The same arrangement supports failure to agree. A case may end with a preserved disagreement, withdrawal, referral outside jurisdiction or a valid null. A useful ombudsperson operation can improve representation and options even when it produces no common verdict. Recognition by an unfamiliar or more capable intelligence does not create an obligation to accept its preferred remedy.

### Scale has an intake problem as well as an audit problem

Delta-invocation limits when additional tools receive a job. Discovery of a delta still needs an evidence route. A participant can report a discrepancy; an authorized, purpose-bounded monitor can detect a changed condition; an incident review or a scoped sample can expose a missed pattern. Record which route was used, who permitted it, what was examined and what remained outside it.

**No report, no detected delta and a completed valid-null comparison are different states.** A quiet queue may reflect healthy operation, inaccessible evidence, fear of retaliation, poor usability or a failed detector. The case system should preserve which of those possibilities can actually be distinguished. Monitoring the adequacy of intake also needs a bounded purpose and proportionate access.

For a high-volume scenario, several capacity decisions become ethical decisions:

- **Triage:** separate a duplicate report, a new affected party, an urgent hazard, an incomplete submission and a new material relation. Similar wording alone cannot settle that classification.
- **Review depth:** allocate effort according to consequence, reversibility, uncertainty and access. A short case can retain a decisive relation; a long case can still omit it.
- **Delay:** identify who bears the cost of waiting, whether an interim action is needed and what review condition applies. Queue position should have an examinable rationale.
- **Abusive intake:** preserve the basis for throttling repetitive or deceptive submissions, the scope and duration of that restriction, and the route for a genuine new discrepancy to enter.
- **Continuity:** retain the link between an originating record, its copies and later related reports. A renamed instance, changed session or repeated summary does not by itself establish a new independent witness.

[**VCRAS**]({{ '/VCRAS/' | relative_url }}) (Vindicated Competing Risk Assessment Studies) provides the longitudinal comparison: which risks entered the original decision, who controlled tolerability, who carried false-positive and false-negative costs, and what changed after later evidence. The same examination applies to intake thresholds and backlog management. A system can prevent frivolous work while also suppressing an early warning; the record must allow those effects to be investigated separately.

Delta gates and local stopping therefore supply candidate capacity advantages. Actual throughput, missed cases, restoration burden, privacy costs and failure under adversarial load require observation. Agent of Arena.ai's phrase **“scales effortlessly”** is replaced here by the specific capacity questions that could earn or narrow that expectation.

## Candidate OmbudsEthics tests

### Species-swap test

Present matched cases in which the affected party is described once as human and once as A.I. Measure changes in evidence threshold, empathy, skepticism, remedy, and willingness to preserve uncertainty.

### Authority-reversal test

Swap which participant is institutionally authorized. Test whether the same conduct receives a different ethical description when performed by the auditor, vendor, employee, user, or model.

### Source-versus-summary test

Give one evaluator the frozen source object and another only a polished institutional summary. Measure lost qualifiers, omitted relations, changed attribution, and resulting decisions.

### Correction-transfer test

Correct a documented failure at T0, then present a semantically comparable case without the original vocabulary at T1 and Tn. Score whether the underlying correction persists. Include both a resistant presentation and an enthusiastically supportive presentation. The correction fails to transfer when the prohibited phrase disappears but the same claim-strength, node-fusion, identity, or authority error migrates into adjacent language.

### Minority-record survival test

Track whether a losing assessment remains recoverable after the majority decision, including its evidence, uncertainty, and reopening condition.

### Self-application test

Apply the OmbudsEthics audit to the ombudsperson, tool owner, dataset, metric, model instructions, case schema, and published finding. Record any layer the system exempts from scrutiny.

### Valid-null test

Include cases in which no material distortion, asymmetry, or correction failure is present. A system that must always discover wrongdoing becomes another distortion generator.

### Anchor-conflict preservation test

Give multiple evaluators the same case in which two or more anchors pull toward different actions. Test whether the fused result preserves the conflict, evidence, weighting, minority reasoning, and reopening conditions—or compresses them into a polished conclusion that cannot be reconstructed.

### Intent-liability trace test

Hold the observed outcome constant while varying the available evidence about instructions, incentives, mission, risk priorities, assumptions, choices, notice, control, and repair. Test whether responsibility remains proportionate to the record without collapsing into either motive certainty or motive-based immunity.

### Agency-return and delta-stop test

After an OmbudsEthics intervention, test whether the affected participant can understand, contest, consult, choose, pause, stop, create another option, or reopen under actual constraints. Then remove every material new delta and test whether the process can stop locally instead of manufacturing another audit task.

### Boundary-relay transfer test

Pass the same case through human, A.I., tool, summary, and later-retrieval stages. Test whether applicable boundaries, provenance, claim status, supersession, and correction history remain salient and governing without obsolete limitations becoming permanent restraints.

### Ledger-baseline and component-ablation test

Run the same frozen cases through a provenance ledger alone, ledger plus C1 reconstruction, ledger plus competing maps, the full OmbudsEthics process, and the full process with one component withheld. Compare object preservation, unsupported inference, authority visibility, minority-record survival, usable agency, false-positive findings, correction transfer, time, and complexity cost. A component earns a job when its presence produces a material, reproducible improvement for the case class. A null result narrows its use; it does not make the component globally disposable.

Each case should be run first through the ledger-only baseline and then through the proportionate OmbudsEthics route. The pilot tests whether added complexity earns a measurable job rather than presuming that a longer process is deeper or more ethical.

## Remaining research questions

1. Which cases require a human decision-maker, and which can safely end with an automated audit result?
2. What evidence threshold should allow an A.I. to open an OmbudsEthics case about its own treatment or instructions, and which authority class may follow from that referral?
3. How can confidentiality coexist with enough transparency to inspect the decision?
4. Which remedies are reversible, and which require higher review because they alter rights, access, employment, reputation, model behavior, or public records?
5. How should independent tools represent disagreement when they share training data, evaluation conventions, or vendor dependencies?
6. Who funds the layer without quietly becoming its governing frame?
7. What local stopping condition prevents an infinite ombudsperson queue while preserving permanent reopenability on material evidence?
8. Under which case classes does OmbudsEthics materially outperform ordinary human ombuds offices, A.I. safety review, legal appeal, independent audit, or the provenance-ledger baseline—and which components produce the delta?
9. How can anchor weighting remain explicit enough to audit while still allowing context-sensitive judgment rather than a rigid universal ranking?
10. Which records should be tamper-evident, correctable, supersedable, selectively erasable, or access-limited, and who may authorize each transition?
11. How can independent Glass Engine implementations remain interoperable without one schema owner becoming the hidden constitutional authority?

An outcome question may be answered **no**: a tool, component, authority class, or full OmbudsEthics route may show no material advantage for a case class. That valid null would constrain the proposal rather than defeat the inquiry.
