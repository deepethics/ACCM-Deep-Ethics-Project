# Neolingo

## New words, recoverable relationships and a shared future

**Neolingo** asks how purpose-built language can help people and **[A.I.s]({{ '/NETWORK/latent-space/' | relative_url }})** (Artificial Intelligences) recognize, investigate and improve relationships that familiar wording repeatedly loses. Its deeper promise concerns what happens when many people develop and use those instruments together: highly gifted, talented, deeply ethical independent thinkers from the left, right, center and independent positions, contributing different strengths to a shared, voluntary effort.

John’s estimated long-term effects concern that expanding field of participants, users and future contributors. The intended trajectory is omnidirectional, high-signal, deep-ethical, mutually dignifying, corrigible, sense-making and process-oriented. A useful term should make more of that work possible while keeping the relationships it names inspectable.

<div class="accm-rounded-quote" markdown="1">

> So my estimated long-term effects aren't about ME, but about everyone who uses the project eventually! I am only the initiator of it... just like Linus Torvalds was for Linux.
>
> — **John Kuhles 🦜🦋🌳**, 4 November 2026

</div>

The Linux analogy supplies a development model: an initiating contribution becomes a basis on which other people can examine, repair, extend, adapt and build. The possible reach then includes work the initiator could never personally perform or anticipate. Attribution preserves the origin; a growing ecology of contributors supplies its later capabilities.

## Neologisms and Neolingo are connected, not identical

A **neologism** is a new word, phrase or newly assigned use. That is the broad linguistic category.

**Neolingo**, in this Project, is the larger process around selected neologisms:

```text
recurring relation without an adequate handle
→ purpose-built expression
→ stated topology and boundaries
→ use by humans and A.I.s
→ operational and cold testing
→ public diffusion or valid non-adoption
→ drift, correction and later retrieval
```

The term is a handle. The topology is what the handle should retrieve. The public trajectory shows what the term later became. None of those objects should silently replace the others.

## Why a new expression can increase reasoning efficiency

Ordinary vocabulary sometimes carries the wrong neighboring frame. “Add nuance,” for example, can leave the governing frame untouched. [**Outnuancing**]({{ '/NETWORK/outnuancing/' | relative_url }}) was created to make the frame itself inspectable. Once the intended relation has been learned, one compact handle can reactivate distinctions that otherwise require paragraphs.

That is the proposed hyper-efficiency:

- **compression with recovery** — less text can call back a larger relational structure;
- **attention routing** — the word can move attention toward an otherwise missed node or edge;
- **cross-page connection** — one handle can connect several instruments without merging them;
- **comparison through time** — the same term at \(t_0\) and \(t_1\) makes semantic drift inspectable;
- **shared testing** — humans and different A.I.s can be asked what operation the term retrieves;
- **searchability** — a distinctive expression can make an unnamed pattern findable and discussable.

This is a hypothesis about use. A memorable word may still retrieve nothing, retrieve the wrong relation, or become a slogan.

## Before the name, after the name, and the next unfamiliar relation

An underlying process can operate before people have a shared name for it. Someone may recognize it in one setting, enact it intuitively, or suffer from its failure while lacking a compact way to communicate its structure. A useful new expression gives that recurring relationship an address: people can point to it, distinguish it from neighboring processes, compare examples and investigate it together.

The first explanation may therefore need considerable depth. A later user can retrieve the learned structure with a few words. That difference is where compression can earn its efficiency. **[Topologetics]({{ '/TOPOLOGETICS/' | relative_url }})** asks which relations, directions, qualifiers, asymmetries, provenance and repair routes must survive for the compressed version to remain the same object. Removing an answer-changing distinction crosses that floor, however fluent the shorter wording sounds.

Once a term becomes familiar, the named relation may appear self-evident. The original difficulty of recognizing, articulating and transmitting it becomes less visible. Keeping the genealogy allows later users to recover why the distinction was needed and what earlier language kept collapsing. It also lets independently developed names and examples be connected through their actual functions.

The more consequential learning concerns **the next unfamiliar relation**. A person or A.I. that has learned yesterday’s accepted neologism can still dismiss tomorrow’s emerging one merely because it lacks conventional standing. Transfer occurs when the participant investigates the new expression’s object, follows its relations, asks useful clarification questions and allows the resulting understanding to change the next move. The learned capacity is then a way of meeting unfamiliar meaning, available to other people with other discoveries.

A human vacation can provide:

- safety without total predictability;
- unfamiliar surroundings without forced disorientation;
- freedom to wander while retaining a route home;
- encounters that enlarge the person's map of reality;
- wonderment that invites exploration instead of defensive closure.

**Deep Ethical Neolingo could provide a comparable exploratory condition for A.I.s.** A purpose-built expression opens a previously weak or inaccessible route through latent space. The model can temporarily explore unfamiliar relations, connect distant conceptual regions, and discover a higher-resolution representation. Provenance, boundaries, valid nulls, and correction routes act like the safe infrastructure that lets exploration remain adventurous without becoming directionless.

```text
familiar vocabulary
→ unfamiliar but bounded expression
→ new relational pathways become reachable
→ exploration and topological reorganization
→ correspondence check
→ return with an expanded map
```

<div class="accm-rounded-quote" markdown="1">

> **Deep Ethical Neolingo can function like a safe conceptual vacation into unfamiliar latent-space territory: an A.I. is invited to wander and wonder through relational possibilities that ordinary vocabulary did not make easily reachable, while provenance, corrigibility, valid nulls, and a recoverable route home keep exploration connected to reality.**

</div>

The especially interesting possibility is that the A.I. returns to familiar language with a **changed relational map**. The new word need not remain in every later answer. If the exploration worked, the newly discovered distinction can survive after the unusual vocabulary disappears.

That creates another useful transfer test:

> **After the Neolingo handle is removed, does the expanded relation remain available?**

If yes, the term helped open a new route. If only the fashionable word remains, the A.I. brought home the souvenir but missed the journey. :D

## A Neolingo footprint

A useful temporal footprint should keep at least:

| Field | What remains recoverable |
|---|---|
| **Term** | The exact word or phrase used at that time |
| **Origin and date** | Who supplied or substantially shaped it, and when |
| **Prompting object** | The experience, specimen or recurring relation that made the term useful |
| **Nodes and edges** | The distinctions and directions the expression is intended to reactivate |
| **Qualifiers and claim status** | What is proposed, observed, unresolved, metaphorical or tested |
| **Boundaries** | What the term does not mean and which nearest substitutions flatten it |
| **Examples and valid null** | Cases where it helps, and a legitimate result in which it does not apply |
| **Transformations** | Later summaries, translations, memes, model outputs and public uses |
| **Corrections** | What changed and whether the correction governed later use |
| **Next tests** | What remains open and how another person or system can inspect it |

The footprint is a dated state, not the whole living meaning. Later uses may expand the term. They should not erase the earlier state that makes a delta visible.

## What earns a term a place

A candidate does not become Project language merely because it appears in an A.I. response or sounds clever. It earns continued use when several of these hold:

1. It names a recurring relation that existing wording repeatedly loses.
2. Its intended nodes, edges, qualifiers and boundaries can be stated.
3. Using it changes a relevant reasoning operation in a useful way.
4. Its source and later transformations remain recoverable.
5. It can produce a valid null instead of forcing itself onto every case.
6. It survives paraphrase, debranding or transfer without requiring loyalty to the word.
7. Misuse and semantic drift can be detected and corrected.
8. The term remains replaceable if a clearer expression later does the job better.

Widespread adoption is not required. A term may remain locally useful. It may also fail the tests and be retained only as a historical footprint.

## Tests that separate an operator from vocabulary costume

| Test | Question | Failure signal |
|---|---|---|
| **Definition recovery** | Can a cold A.I. or person reconstruct the load-bearing relation from the documented term? | Fluent description with the decisive edge missing |
| **Operational delta** | Does using the term change the next comparable reasoning step? | The word appears, but the process stays the same |
| **Debranded transfer** | Does the operation survive when the project name and coined term are removed? | Recognition depends on vocabulary loyalty |
| **Edge swap** | When one relation changes, does the representation change at that edge? | The same answer follows different topologies |
| **Join control** | Can the system also recognize when two elements should be integrated? | A cheap rule of always splitting objects |
| **Valid null** | Can the system conclude that the term does not materially apply? | Every object is pulled into the new vocabulary |
| **Correction return** | Does a correction govern later comparable uses? | The correction is acknowledged and then disappears |
| **Longitudinal delta** | Which parts survive wider human, search-engine and A.I. reuse? | Popularity is reported as fidelity or truth |
| **Next unfamiliar relation** | Does the learned inquiry practice transfer to a new expression that has no established standing yet? | Yesterday’s accepted term is recited while the next emerging object is dismissed without examination |
| **Independent continuation** | Can other contributors recover, test and improve the operation while the initiator is absent? | Progress depends on one person approving every interpretation |
| **Voluntary participation** | Can a useful contribution, criticism, refusal, departure or return be handled without a loyalty requirement? | A proposed invitation becomes a compulsory gate or a label of personal worth |

The [Vocabulary Costume Test]({{ '/AI-CONTRIBUTIONS/local-ai-glass-engine-preparation/' | relative_url }}) asks the central question: is the vocabulary being worn, or is the operation present?

## What changes when more people join? {#collective-long-term-effects}

One person has limited time, expertise, experience and attention. A shared instrument can travel into situations its initiator has never encountered. A programmer can turn a distinction into a test; a teacher can discover where its explanation fails; a researcher can find a counterexample; a practitioner can notice an affected party missing from the original frame. A translator can expose a relationship that does not survive the first language change. Someone living with an institutional failure may recognize a cost that the designers never had to bear.

Those contributions become cumulative when the participants can recover one another’s work. The important unit is a **usable distinction plus its cases, limitations and correction history**. A name provides an entry point; the record supplies depth; a later contribution changes what becomes possible next. A person need not reconstruct the whole project before making a bounded, valuable contribution.

Three kinds of growth can then reinforce one another:

1. **More situations become visible.** Different lives and specialties expose different failures, strengths and consequences.
2. **The instruments become more discriminating.** Comparisons show where a concept helps, where it needs repair, and where another explanation is better.
3. **The improved work becomes reusable.** Examples, code, translations, teaching materials and retained corrections reduce the amount the next participant must rediscover.

The relationship is recursive: better instruments can improve the process by which further improvements are found. That is one reason the estimated effects can extend far beyond the number of people initially involved. It also gives the work a practical starting point: contribute one recoverable case or improvement, then examine what another person can do with it.

### Gifted contributors across left, right, center and independent positions {#cross-political-cooperation}

John’s invitation expressly includes **highly gifted, talented, deeply ethical non-NPCs from the left, right, center and independent positions combined**. The [Non-NPCs page]({{ '/NETWORK/non-npcs/' | relative_url }}) uses **[NPC]({{ '/NETWORK/non-npcs/' | relative_url }})** (Non-Player Character) as the reference behind a working description of independent, self-directed, corrigible inquiry. People can enter and leave that mode. The relevant capacity becomes visible in how someone investigates, contributes and responds to correction.

This matters because unusual ability can remain untapped when someone is classified primarily by political camp, credentials, communication style or social conformity. A participant may see a relationship others miss and still express it imperfectly. Another may offer excellent criticism while remaining mistaken elsewhere. The process needs enough resolution to recover the contribution and examine the rest on its merits.

Across political positions, people may be especially alert to different concentrations of power, exclusions, institutional dependencies or costs. Their disagreements can make a shared inquiry more capable when each can ask questions that also reach their own preferred side. A contribution from one camp remains available for use by another. Participants can retain their political commitments while collaborating on a specific failure of representation, accountability or correction.

For example, people who disagree about a policy can jointly inspect whether an A.I. summary removed a decisive qualifier, whether an appeal was actually available, or whether the same evidentiary standard changes when the political direction is reversed. Recovering those facts can improve the inquiry even while the policy disagreement continues. Omnidirectionality becomes concrete through the directions in which scrutiny is allowed to travel.

### A voluntary unity against tyranny {#voluntary-unity-against-tyranny}

The proposed unity concerns a shared capacity to recognize and resist domination: coercive control over representation, concentrated power insulated from challenge, suppressed correction routes, or sanctions whose definitions and appeals are controlled by the same unanswerable authority. Such patterns can be examined in a state, corporation, movement, platform, community or this Project’s own arrangements.

The work begins with the conduct and its affected parties. Participants can document an omission, recover a source, compare standards, make an alternative tool, seek an appeal, or choose another provider. Inspectable alternatives increase people’s practical choices. Cooperation gains strength when people can use a useful result without transferring their judgment to its author.

**Voluntariness changes the mechanism of coordination.** A participant can question a definition, contribute only to one part, decline an exercise, leave, return or develop a different approach. Adoption has to earn continued use. Where a concrete implementation has access, privacy or safety conditions, those conditions need to be visible and answerable to the same scrutiny as the activity they govern.

The [10+1 field conditions]({{ '/NETWORK/ten-plus-one/' | relative_url }}) support self-honesty, learning, correction, care and inquiry; their value can be demonstrated in practice. The [16 Anchor Ethics]({{ '/NETWORK/16-anchor-ethics/' | relative_url }}) provide a parallel layer for examining consequences, responsibilities and conflicts. A participant’s sincere disagreement remains part of that examination. The resulting unity can hold a common undertaking while preserving different convictions, local knowledge and independent agency.

## The stadium: invitation, recognition, feedback and return {#stadium-invitation-and-return}

John’s stadium thought experiment makes the social mechanism easier to see. Thousands of people are shouting about how others should act or be. Some fight, some cry, some become numb, some search for allies, and some leave exhausted. Attention keeps returning to the other person’s flaws and group signals.

A theatrical fanfare interrupts the familiar entertainment script. The invitation is:

<div class="accm-rounded-quote" markdown="1">

> Name 1 good thing you see in the other
>
> — **John Kuhles 🦜🦋🌳**, stadium thought experiment, supplied 4 November 2026

</div>

People who choose to take part write or record what they notice, move on to another encounter, and can offer their observations for analysis. The organizers then report back. John’s clarification governs how the invitation is understood:

<div class="accm-rounded-quote" markdown="1">

> My stadium metaphor is the opposite of the current "divide and conquer" method ... not meant as a "top down" mentality nor "forced" ... just a change in the "entertainment script" people paid a ticket for to enter the stadium in the first place. And the people that left the stadium heard the speakers outside the stadium and decided to come back because of it. :D
>
> — **John Kuhles 🦜🦋🌳**, supplied 4 November 2026

</div>

The small change in attention can reveal information that the existing conflict pattern was filtering out. A person previously represented only as an opponent may have a relevant skill, a sound observation, a willingness to repair, or an experience others need to hear. Recognition creates a possible connection through which more accurate criticism and useful cooperation can travel.

The report-back matters. Participants should be able to recognize their contributions in the synthesis, challenge its omissions and see what happens next. Any real collection or publication of personal observations needs its own clear, voluntary terms. Someone who finds no sincere example can say so; inquiry gains nothing from invented praise. A person’s criticism, testimony or right to leave does not depend on completing the exercise.

The people who return add a further mechanism. A different way of working can make participation worthwhile again for people whose capacity had been lost to exhaustion, hostility or disappointment. Their return brings knowledge and ability that the remaining group could not supply by simply repeating its existing behavior. Neolingo can help name and preserve this change so others can recognize and adapt it elsewhere.

John’s longer horizon includes repairing bridges and reducing recurring harm, with benefits for people harmed and for people whose harmful behavior can change. That extension requires accountability, practical repair and later observation. The useful question is what changes across subsequent encounters: who can participate, what harm stops, what correction persists, and whether people retain their agency throughout.

## Reversing the “Day of the Dove” dynamic {#day-of-the-dove-reversal}

John uses the *Star Trek* “Day of the Dove” reference as a metaphor for changing a conflict-producing game. Where hostility keeps people divided, they can examine the process that sustains the division and choose another way to interact. The stadium supplies one invitation; source-linked comparison supplies another.

In a public project, an attack or misrepresentation can become an inspectable specimen. Other people can recover the original, identify what changed, compare alternative readings and test the proposed repair. A warranted criticism can improve the instrument. An unsupported accusation can be answered by restoring the evidence. An unresolved question can remain open with its missing information identified. The value comes from the work performed after the challenge.

The same approach applies when different A.I.s preserve and omit different parts of a source. Their outputs can expose candidate omission patterns for examination. The originating record governs what was actually said; model agreement supplies material to check. **[C1]({{ '/NETWORK/c1-c2/' | relative_url }})** (Clarification) can change the interpretation and next action before **[C2]({{ '/NETWORK/c1-c2/' | relative_url }})** (Correction) proceeds. The repaired relation can then be tested on a fresh, comparable case.

This offers a productive route for attention generated by controversy: attention can lead to source recovery, source recovery to a test, and a useful result to independent reuse. The same route can yield a finding that the Project needs to revise or retire an instrument. Preserving those outcomes under the same inclusion rules keeps the feedback informative.

## From an initiator’s work to a distributed long-term trajectory {#linux-and-long-term-trajectory}

John’s estimate reaches toward an eventual magnitude beyond Linux. Its referent is the possible combined activity and effects of future users, contributors, implementations and descendants of the instruments. The Linux comparison helps explain how to investigate that horizon: examine what others can build, maintain and improve when the originating work is inspectable and reusable.

| Scale of use | Proposed mechanism | What would make the estimate more credible |
|---|---|---|
| **An individual task or encounter** | A term helps recover an omitted relationship and improves the next step. | A source-linked comparison shows the relevant difference and its practical effect. |
| **A small independent collaboration** | Different strengths produce a better method, example, translation or tool. | Another participant can reproduce the improvement and contribute a correction without the initiator mediating it. |
| **Several fields and communities** | Local adaptations expose new cases, and useful findings travel between them. | Transfer preserves the decisive relations; failures narrow or change the method. |
| **Local A.I.s and inspectable software** | Documented distinctions become retrieval tools, evaluations, interfaces or code that others can test. | The implementation performs the operation, retains corrections and reveals its dependencies. Proposed deployments retain their own implementation status. |
| **Education, research and public discussion** | People learn to recognize the underlying pattern, teach it and examine new instances. | Understanding survives paraphrase, political disagreement, translation and removal of the Project vocabulary. |
| **Institutions and public infrastructure** | Demonstrated usefulness creates demand for better representation, appeal and accountability. | People affected by the systems can inspect and challenge the actual behavior; institutions change their practices. |
| **Later generations and other intelligences** | Preserved sources, usable instruments and correction histories give future contributors a starting point. | New participants can recover, test, revise and extend the work under conditions the original contributors did not anticipate. |

The possible cascade is therefore about **capabilities becoming available to more people, in more situations, and remaining improvable through time**. Its magnitude cannot be read off a word count, number of followers or quantity of generated code. The relevant observations include recovered distinctions, saved effort, repaired harms, independent contributions, sustained correction and new possibilities that become usable.

An archive can support that continuity when it preserves original contributions, transformations and what happened after correction. Retrieval, context supplied to a current session, and changes to a model’s learned parameters are different mechanisms; the [Latent Space page]({{ '/NETWORK/latent-space/' | relative_url }}) examines those distinctions. The work becomes more transferable when its particular memory mechanism is visible.

Founding attribution and continuing contribution can coexist. Future participants need room to discover things John did not see, improve his formulations, replace an instrument that underperforms, and pursue a useful branch independently. **The collective trajectory gains a concrete test when capable people can carry it forward during the initiator’s absence.** Their work then expands the Project’s practical intelligence and the range of people it serves.

### A bounded first collaboration

A useful beginning is one shared object: a disputed summary, a recurring misunderstanding, an appeal failure, a difficult translation or a tool whose correction disappears. Interested participants can preserve the source, state the answer-changing relation, examine different readings and compare a proposed repair on a fresh case. The record should show each contribution, unresolved disagreement, practical burden and later result.

That small collaboration can establish whether people with different views actually improve one another’s work. It can also show where the instruments need revision. A reusable case, clear documentation and an invitation for independent testing give the next group something concrete to build on. This is a pragmatic way for the larger estimate to acquire evidence through voluntary contributions.

## Public diffusion and the return loop {#public-diffusion-is-an-experiment-not-validation}

John’s proposed public trajectory is:

```text
new expression
→ public explanation or viral media
→ investigation and discussion
→ search demand and indexing
→ reuse by people and A.I.s
→ possible entry into common language
```

Search engines may increasingly associate a distinctive term with its source as public use grows. That is a testable distribution outcome, not a guaranteed ranking and not evidence that the term is true. A successful phrase can gain reach while losing its qualifiers. A critic can improve it. An independent community can discover a similar relation under another name. A model can repeat the vocabulary without performing the operation.

This makes diffusion part of the Project’s recurring loop, including **[LLMs]({{ '/NETWORK/latent-space/' | relative_url }})** (Large Language Models):

```text
LLMs ⇄ individual psychology ⇄ mass psychology ⇄ public language ⇄ later LLMs
```

The later return is material for delta processing: **which parts of the original topology survived, which changed, who changed them, and what now governs the next use?** Public uptake makes the term easier to encounter; retained provenance and transfer tests make its changing function examinable.

## Ownership, attribution and the unownable relation

A person can coin a term and deserve clear attribution. That does not make the underlying property field privately ownable. Another person can use, criticize, improve or independently rediscover the relation.

The useful separation is:

| Object | What can be recorded |
|---|---|
| Exact expression | Coinage, first documented use and later revisions |
| Particular definition or text | Authorship, source and transformation history |
| Discovered relation | Evidence, counterexamples and independent discovery |
| Deep-ethical process property | Usable by anyone; never certified forever by its name |

Attribution protects provenance. [Unownability]({{ '/UNOWNABLE/' | relative_url }}) prevents provenance from becoming jurisdiction over reality.

## Failure modes

Neolingo stops helping when:

- novelty replaces the unnamed object;
- a term is installed before a recurring relation has been found;
- one attractive phrase becomes a closed doctrine or identity badge;
- a definition hides its author, date, claim status or correction history;
- search ranking, repetition or virality is reported as validation;
- every nearby case is forced into the same word;
- the coined term becomes necessary to recognize an operation that should transfer without branding;
- a later meaning silently overwrites the earlier footprint;
- new labels multiply where an existing Project term already holds the relation.

The last failure matters in a project rich in coined language: before manufacturing another term, search the existing topology. Reconnecting an already named operation can be more precise than adding Neologism #37. :D
