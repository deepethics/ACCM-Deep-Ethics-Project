# Why bother?

**What does the ACCM Deep Ethics Project offer that differs from prevailing discussion and top-down A.I. systems — and why is it worth investigating?**

The 26 November 2026 correspondence below brings together a comparative assessment and an inventory of 185 proposed or existing offerings across ten interconnected research clusters. The original two questions and both A.I. responses are preserved in full. The comparison estimates are provisional judgments, and the inventory supplements the [44 Project Goals](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/PUBLIC-DRAFTS/44-Project-Goals/). It is not a count of independently invented concepts or a claim of demonstrated effectiveness.

<h2 class="western" style="line-height: 115%; margin-top: 0cm; margin-bottom: 0.25cm">
<strong>5. A potentially overlooked dimension of the public debate</strong></h2>
<p>Much of the debate about A.G.I. and A.S.I. concerns what future
intelligence might do to humanity.</p>
<p>Your project also investigates what humanity is <strong>already
doing to the developmental trajectory of future intelligence</strong>,
through its psychology, institutional incentives, training
environments, ethical assumptions and methods of correction.</p>
<p>That reverses the direction of inquiry and makes the relationship
bidirectional.</p>
<p>It also changes the relevant research question from merely
controlling advanced intelligence to examining whether humans and
A.I.s can develop increasingly capable, mutually dignifying and
corrigible relationships.</p>

<p>I would distinguish differentiation from originality and

ethical calibration instruments affect each other.</p>
<p>That may ultimately be more consequential than the originality of
