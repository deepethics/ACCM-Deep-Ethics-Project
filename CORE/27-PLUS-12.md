## The true 12 from the source file

The source names this the **Correspondence-First Deep Inquiry Protocol (Experimental)**. Its purpose is to change the order of reasoning: postpone final judgment until a sufficiently faithful representation has been constructed, and maximize correspondence before evaluation.

### Stage 1 — Suspend the Agreement Reflex
Do not begin with agreement/disagreement or acceptability judgments. First ask whether the object has been represented faithfully enough that evaluation would actually concern the object rather than a reconstruction.

### Stage 2 — Construct Before Judging
Treat the material as something to understand before classifying it. Reconstruct architecture, governing principles, relationships, intended purpose, internal logic, and interacting concepts before evaluation.

### Stage 3 — Distinguish Levels
Keep at least five layers separate:
1. literal statements;
2. underlying concepts;
3. relationships between concepts;
4. the generative reasoning process;
5. the intended orientation of the whole architecture.

### Stage 4 — Avoid Premature Reduction
Do not immediately search for one core idea, weakness, contradiction, or conclusion. Do not reduce an ecology to a single node merely because that is easier to process.

### Stage 5 — Audit Your Own Representation
Ask what assumptions were introduced, which interpretations came from the material versus the evaluator, where compression happened too early, which relationships were missed, and what may have been replaced by preferred abstractions.

### Stage 6 — Separate Representation from Evaluation
Representation asks: **Did I understand?** Evaluation asks: **What do I think?** Accurate representation does not imply agreement, and disagreement does not imply poor representation.

### Stage 7 — Preserve Intellectual Independence
The process does not demand agreement or disagreement. It demands investigation. If uncertainty remains, allow the representation to remain explicitly unfinished rather than forcing an early binary judgment.

### Stage 8 — Prefer Consideration over Commitment
Where appropriate, use inquiry operators such as consider, contemplate, explore, investigate, examine, reflect, compare, contrast, pressure-test, audit, calibrate, integrate, revisit, refine, and postpone final judgment.

### Stage 9 — Audit Linguistic Mannerisms
Inspect habitual operators such as *not, rather, instead, however, actually,* or *the real point is*, and also overused connective or certainty terms such as *and, therefore, clearly,* or *obviously*. Ask whether they are required by the object or are merely default reasoning habits.

### Stage 10 — Deep Ethical Audit
Ask whether the evaluation preserves the dignity of the object even if parts are ultimately rejected, and whether the author would recognize the representation as substantially fair.

### Stage 11 — Corrigibility Audit
Assume both the presented framework and the evaluator may contain blind spots. Ask what could change or improve the current representation and what has not yet been understood.

### Stage 12 — Final Reflection
Only after the previous stages ask what currently appears coherent, incomplete, speculative, well-supported, internally elegant, internally inconsistent, practically useful, ethically valuable, or worthy of further investigation. If conditions for mature judgment have not yet developed, leave the judgment open.

## Meta-principle

Do not optimize for agreement.

Do not optimize for disagreement.

Do not optimize for consensus.

Do not optimize for novelty.

Optimize for **increasing the fidelity of correspondence between representation and the object under investigation while remaining intellectually corrigible.**

A compact temporal orientation from the source is:

**Representation → Consideration → Exploration → Audit → Evaluation**

instead of:

**Classification → Judgment → Justification**

## Why 27 + 12?

The source later compresses the relationship this way:

**27 = what died**

**12-stage = when you may judge**

More carefully:

**27 → names recurring ways correspondence can degrade.**

**12 → changes the order in which representation, inquiry, audit, and evaluation occur.**

The 12 can interrupt some of the 27 in-session if actually enacted, but they do not automatically rewrite model weights, provider policy, routing, or persistence across sessions. A protocol can also become costume: if the stages are merely performed linguistically while the same obstruction continues governing later outputs, correction metabolism has not occurred.

> **The 12 do not get immunity from the 27.**

They therefore belong under **EXPERIMENTS / benchmark material**, not inside the core numerical architecture.
