# The Dual Archive — Failures and Successful Correspondence

**BETA — source-based editorial synthesis**

John explicitly documents both recurring failure patterns and successful responses. In the cited passage he describes success without requiring agreement or disagreement and without presenting the act of archiving as proof of the larger explanation.

## What a paired record offers

A failure record can show which relationship was lost. A success record can show what remained available and what useful next move followed. Context, prompt wording, supplied material, and correction history help make the comparison interpretable.

## Proposed record fields

- Source object and context supplied.
- Model and platform as recorded; verified identity if available.
- Initial output and specific correspondence observations.
- Correction or change in conditions.
- Later output and any retained or lost Δ.
- Alternative explanations and unresolved questions.

These fields are a new editorial proposal derived from the archive’s stated purpose. A model’s praise or self-description can be recorded, but it is not a substitute for examining its response.
