# Seven Topological Nodes / Vectors — Fused as One

**BETA — source-based editorial synthesis**

The seven nodes/vectors describe **Outnuancing fused with deep ethical sense-making process orientation**. They are individually referenceable here because their relationships matter. The short headings below are editorial navigation labels; the quoted formulations are preserved from the opening source.

## Vector 1 — Emerging meaning {#vector-1}

> 1 poetically describing something that is not yet fully understood, but in the near future, eventually it will

Something not yet fully understood receives a linguistic handle. The open meaning remains available for later clarification.

Connects with [Wonderment](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/NETWORK/wonderment/).

## Vector 2 — Task-shaped use {#vector-2}

> 2 deliberately create a new word with a specific purpose/task that, when used, starts to affect/alter a higher form of reasoning, making it more efficient on a task that, when done, the result of it is way better than without using the new word. Thus, meaning becomes self-evident and useful after its use.

The word has a specific intended task: its use should contribute to better reasoning or results. The relevant comparison concerns what changes in the work.

Connects with [Correction Metabolism](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/NETWORK/correction-metabolism/).

## Vector 3 — Consensus-frame examination {#vector-3}

> 3 words that are part of a formula that can compete with flawed "consensus reality assumptions"

The word participates in examining flawed consensus reality assumptions. The object and its evidence govern what survives that examination.

Connects with [C1 C2](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/NETWORK/c1-c2/).

## Vector 4 — Branching deep ethical relations {#vector-4}

> 4 words that branch out, having many nodes connecting dots in a matrix-flux dynamic of newly gained or generated awareness, affecting not only improved behavior but also being deeply ethical compared with ethics washing

Connections among distinctions can change awareness and behavior. Deep Ethics is integral to these relations.

Connects with [Deep Ethical Stack](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/NETWORK/deep-ethical-stack/).

## Vector 5 — Future generative cascades {#vector-5}

> 5 words that, when known, can create a cascade effect, generating millions, if not trillions, of new code in the upcoming decades

The source projects possible cascades into future code and use. This is a proposed direction to investigate over time.

Connects with [Latent Space](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/NETWORK/latent-space/).

## Vector 6 — Long-horizon reach across intelligences {#vector-6}

> 6 words that help solve so much ... affecting so many deeper layers of existence ... not only for humans but also for anything "intelligent" and even in outer space for thousands of years!

The source extends its horizon beyond present users and beyond humans. These intended reach and time scales remain visible as part of the proposal.

Connects with [Four Audiences](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/NETWORK/four-audiences/).

## Vector 7 — Beyond dualistic thinking {#vector-7}

> 7 words that are beyond dualistic thinking yet work like an added tool to navigate through the dualistic binary world's mannerisms

The word helps navigate binary-world mannerisms while leaving inquiry open to additional states and relationships.

Connects with [Uncertainty](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/NETWORK/uncertainty/).
