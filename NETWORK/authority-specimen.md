# Worked Specimen — The Imported Authority Frame

**BETA — source specimen with editorial analysis**

## Source sequence

1. John supplies the orientation and 10+1, including openness, correction, and rejection of final authority over reality.
2. ChatGPT introduces a concern about an ethical framework becoming an authority structure.
3. John asks “HOW?”
4. ChatGPT develops a hypothetical self-sealing scenario.
5. John states: “all 11 can correct/self-improve all other 10”.
6. ChatGPT revises its account toward mutual correction.
7. John asks where the initial question came from; ChatGPT offers an explanation of its own behavior.

The complete turns used for this sequence are preserved in the linked excerpt. The opening context is separately linked.

## What the specimen makes inspectable

Compare the hypothetical scenario to the supplied object. Identify which conditions the AI introduced and which source features it initially underused. Then examine the revised account for what it restores and what it still adds.

The episode provides a representation-and-correction sequence. Whether the correction persists requires later relevant observations. ChatGPT’s claims about training, internal heuristics, and causation are its retrospective explanations; those explanations are also legitimate audit objects.

Nearest-Generalization Substitution, Phantom Claim Generation, Asymmetric Scrutiny, and Correction Persistence Failure are possible analytical links to the 27. Assigning a category should follow the exchange’s details rather than predetermine the reading.
