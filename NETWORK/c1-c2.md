# C1 Before C2 — Clarification and Intervention

**BETA — source-based editorial synthesis**

[Open the BETA visual twin with two illustrative figures.]({{ '/NETWORK/c1-c2-beta/' | relative_url }})

The existing Lexicon defines **C1** as correspondence-oriented clarification when something materially relevant remains unresolved. **C2** covers correction, caution, steering, warning, or other intervention. Their ordering matters when the object has not yet been sufficiently established.



## John's orientation: C1 is clarifying questions, not a verdict {#c1-not-a-verdict}

> C1 is never wrong because it is not about right or wrong ... study the true meaning of the word clarifying questions (not matter what answer, IF it clarifies it dd job what it suppose to do!)

Spelling Δ in the quote: “no matter”; “did its job”; “supposed to do.” Original stays.

C1 is a **clarifying question**. It is not a right/wrong score. If it clarifies, it did its job — whatever the answer. Treating a C1 as a verdict that was “lost” or “won” substitutes scoring for clarification.

## A specific unresolved point

A C1 move identifies the ambiguity that matters to the next step. It leaves room for the answer to change the evaluation. Questions that already assume guilt, error, authority abuse, or harmlessness can import a conclusion while appearing to clarify.

## Outnuancing connection

John’s source says C2 may use frame-auditing methods while omitting the deep ethical process inseparable from Outnuancing. The audit therefore includes the proposed intervention, its assumptions, and its effect on the source object.

The opening conversation provides a worked case: an AI introduces an authority-structure concern; John asks how it follows and clarifies the mutually corrective relation; the AI revises its account. The sequence can be examined without treating the AI’s explanation of its internal causes as direct access to those causes.

## Triad extension — temporary use before future reopening

The October 17 triad adds a temporal decision layer to C1–C2. After clarification, the best available object may be used even when future correction remains possible. A temporary freeze is not closure when the conditions, authorship, uncertainty, and repair route stay inspectable.

A later C1 may reveal that the input changed, the processing method changed, the observer adapted, or nothing material changed. C2 should respond to the located delta rather than manufacture a defect merely because another pass occurred.

## C1 before contribution-state assignment {#c1-before-contribution-state}

**Status:** `[CANDIDATE-APPLICATION]`

A Contribution State Grammar can become sophisticated C2 if an evaluator assigns *not useful*, *dangerous* or *needs correction* before clarifying the object. Replacing **disagree** with a richer label does not repair the sequence.

C1 is not established by a question mark. Its test is whether materially different answers can change the next move. Where materially relevant ambiguity remains unresolved:

```text
recover the object
→ ask answer-changing C1
→ update the representation
→ assign a provisional contribution state
→ expose “here is why”
→ keep state and explanation corrigible
```

A question whose answer cannot alter the prepared intervention is ceremonial C1. A declarative clarification request can perform C1 if it reopens the representation. Under immediate concrete danger, proportionate action may precede full clarification, but action, warrant and review remain inspectable.
