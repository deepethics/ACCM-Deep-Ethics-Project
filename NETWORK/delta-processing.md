## Distinguish the changes

| Change | What a record can preserve |
|---|---|
| Representation | Which claim, condition, relation, or qualifier changed. |
| Process | Which next move changed after a correction. |
| Relation | Which connection between concepts became visible. |
| Uncertainty | What remains open or now has a different evidential status. |
| Persistence | Whether the change remains relevant later. |

This is an editorial recording aid. The categories do not replace the larger observation field.
