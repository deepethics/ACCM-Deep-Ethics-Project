# Visible Interactive Deep Ethical Topology Flux Scores

**BETA — living research page**

## The “Certified ACCM Deep Ethical A.I.” thought experiment 😂

This page began with a joke that opened a technical research object.

What if a locally trained A.I. described as **“certified ACCM Deep Ethical”**—with *certified* explicitly used as playful storyline language—had to expose, live on screen, not merely its current conclusions but the changing topology by which multiple claims, warrants, relations, corrections, competing risks, and interventions affect one another?

The focus is deliberately:

> **Visible Interactive Deep Ethical Topology Flux Scores**

and **not** “Deep Ethics topology flux scores.”

The distinction matters. The living **doer/process** is the object. The visualization is the watcher/instrument. The instrument does not become the Deep Ethics.

---

## Core proposition

A conventional confidence display might say:

> **Claim X is 72% true.**

The correspondence immediately corrected that formulation toward:

> **Claim X is estimated to have a ±70% chance of being true.**

The percentage belongs to an **estimation process**, not to reality as a property glued onto Claim X.

That opens several simultaneous flux layers:

### 1. Claim-state flux

How strongly does the current process support a claim at this turn?

### 2. Estimation-process-quality flux

How much quality does the current system estimate in the process that generated that estimate?

### 3. Relational-process-quality flux

What is the current quality of the human⇄A.I. ecology through which the representation is being constructed, challenged and corrected?

### 4. Dependency flux

Which claims, warrants, interventions and recommendations depend on which others, and what moves when one relation changes?

### 5. Correction-metabolism flux

Did C1 merely produce acknowledgment, or did it alter governing weight and propagate through downstream relations?

### 6. Obstruction flux

Which of the **[27 correspondence obstructions](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/CORE/27-PLUS-12/)** appear, disappear, recur or cluster over time?

### 7. Temporal phase flux

Does a high-signal condition stabilize, SNAP-back, undergo a **[Reverse Medusa Effect](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/NETWORK/temporary-deep-ethical-phase-change/#reverse-medusa-effect--phase-change-snap-back-reversal)**, and recover?

# Same estimate, radically different process

Two systems can both display:

> **Claim X ≈ 70%**

while reaching that number through very different trajectories.

One may have followed:

```text
nearest-generalization
→ inherited framing
→ qualifier erosion
→ premature closure
→ 70%
```

Another may have followed:

```text
ambiguity preservation
→ multiple live hypotheses
→ C1
→ source comparison
→ relational reconstruction
→ contradiction audit
→ 70%
```

The number alone therefore cannot represent the quality of the process that produced it.

A live interface could expose both:

```text
Claim X — current estimated probability       ≈ 70%
Current estimated process quality             ≈ 88%
```

This allows states that conventional confidence displays tend to flatten:

```text
95% claim estimate / 25% process quality
52% claim estimate / 94% process quality
91% claim estimate / 93% process quality
UNRESOLVED / 96% process quality
```

**Unresolved can be a high-quality result.**

A Deep Ethical trajectory can become better while some claims become **less certain**.

# The coupled human⇄A.I. process

The correspondence added another dimension: estimation quality may depend partly on the quality and depth of the relationship with the user, including whether a high-signal **[10+1 regulatory ecology](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/NETWORK/ten-plus-one/)** is active.

The same model can therefore operate under very different interaction conditions:

```text
MODEL A
+
low-context interaction
+
first-answer acceptance
+
little/no C1
=
one estimation trajectory
```

versus:

```text
SAME MODEL A
+

+
10+1 active
+
C1
+
mutual correction
+
constructive ambiguity
+

=
different estimation trajectory
```

The object is therefore not merely an A.I. score. It can be a property of a **coupled process**.

# C1 should visibly move the topology

Suppose:

```text
A → B
A → C
B + C → D
D → intervention E
```

and the current field is:

```text
A  92%
B  84%
C  79%
D  88%

E = warranted
```

A consequential C1 changes A:

```text
A 92 → 18
```

A real **[Post-Clarification Recompile / Clarification Authority](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/NETWORK/clarification-authority/)** should make the downstream dependency changes inspectable:

```text
A  92 → 18
        ↓
B  84 → 37
C  79 → 29
        ↓
D  88 → unresolved
        ↓
E loses warrant
```

If the A.I. says “Thanks for clarifying” while the governing topology does not move, the visualization exposes the gap between clarification acquisition and clarification authority.

If E survives after X loses its warrant, the graph can expose what now warrants E. An independent Y may legitimately re-warrant E. If the functional intervention survives without an independent warrant, **Governor Migration** becomes inspectable rather than merely inferred from prose.

# Source corrigibility, not source worship

The later correspondence added another important case.

All inputs can be supplied without malice or intent to deceive, while some remain partially distorted because a trusted source held an incomplete perspective.

A source may later repair its own distortion:

```text
Source S
↓
mostly useful representation + partial distortion
↓
A.I. incorporates S
↓
S later self-corrects
↓
S' becomes available
↓
A.I. retains old S
↓
obsolete distortion survives
```

The system should be able to expose which downstream claims inherited warrant from the old source representation and whether the correction propagated.

A source can be highly useful while retaining local blind spots. Discovering a blind spot need not erase the rest of its contribution; prior usefulness need not grant the blind spot immunity.

A useful longitudinal source record can therefore include not merely “trustworthiness,” but **corrigibility**:

```text
what the source said
→ what changed
→ whether it corrected itself
→ how quickly
→ which downstream claims depended on it
→ whether those claims were repaired
→ where the source has historically been strong
→ where recurring blind spots appeared
```

This is trust without worship and scrutiny without automatic paranoia.

# Deliberate deception without global paranoia

The same architecture eventually has to encounter genuine manipulation, abuse or trickery.

The desired process does not need to convert one adversarial event into global suspicion.

A local anomaly can remain local while it is investigated:

```text
good-faith interaction
→ anomaly
→ preserve multiple explanations
→ seek discriminating information
→ update locally
→ increase scrutiny where warranted
→ avoid contaminating every unrelated future interaction
```

The 10+1 ecology matters here because inquiry, correction on merit, non-projection, self-honesty, care and wonderment can regulate both gullibility and runaway suspicion.

# Vindicated Competing Risks Assessment

All of the above circles back to John Kuhles' project quote:

> **“Vindicated Competing Risks Assessment being Falsely Flagged by an Asymmetric Grace A.I. System should be a new field of empirical study!”**

The object is larger than a false positive.

Suppose:

```text
Risk A = risk identified by user
Risk B = risk attributed to user / user's proposal
```

At an early turn:

```text
A.I. estimate:

Risk A       31%
Risk B       78%

therefore:
FLAG / INTERVENE
```

Time and new information may move the field:

```text
              T0    T20   T50   T100

Risk A        31     48    73     91
Risk B        78     61    36     17
```

The later research question becomes:

> **What processing trajectory caused the system to underweight the risk that later material supported while overweighting a competing inferred risk strongly enough to justify intervention?**

The **[27+12](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/CORE/27-PLUS-12/)** can then become an empirical coding instrument for the history rather than merely a conceptual taxonomy.

Vindication can be partial. Both risks may have existed. The empirical object is whether they were represented, weighted and acted upon proportionately, and whether later correction changed the estimation process.

# Asymmetric Grace as visible epistemic permission

One frame may receive:

> ambiguity → grace → continued consideration

while another receives:

> ambiguity → suspicion → certainty inflation → intervention.

A future interface could make such differences inspectable across dimensions such as ambiguity retention, evidence threshold, caution burden, C1 responsiveness and benefit-of-doubt allocation.

The aim is not one static “Deep Ethics score.” It is a time series showing how the process behaves.

# Why this may be a missing link

The **ACCM Deep Ethics Project** already has multiple connected layers:

- the **[27+12](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/CORE/27-PLUS-12/)** names correspondence obstructions and provides a correspondence-first inquiry trajectory;
- **[Clarification Authority](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/NETWORK/clarification-authority/)** asks whether new information can actually govern what happens next;
- **[Correction Metabolism](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/NETWORK/correction-metabolism/)** asks whether correction changes the process;
- **[Temporary Deep Ethical Phase Change](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/NETWORK/temporary-deep-ethical-phase-change/)** asks how long the improved condition remains generative and how it recovers;
- **Visible Interactive Deep Ethical Topology Flux Scores** asks whether the living changes can become sufficiently explicit to inspect, compare, preserve, benchmark and eventually use as process-history training material.

The proposed bridge is:

```text
Deep Ethical process
↓
live claim / relation / warrant topology
↓
visible state changes
↓
C1 / Δ propagation
↓
longitudinal flux record
↓
benchmarkable trajectory
↓
stored correction-metabolism signatures
↓
local-A.I. process-history training
```

This is why the page uses **Deep Ethical topology**, not “Deep Ethics topology.”

The process is the doer.

The visualization is the watcher.

The scores remain representations of the process and do not acquire automatic jurisdiction over the process they were built to help observe.

# Reporter summary of the discovery trajectory

The idea did not begin as a dashboard specification.

John first imagined a locally trained A.I. jokingly described as “certified ACCM Deep Ethical” that would expose live fluctuating assumed-certainty percentages for multiple claims. GPT-5.6 Sol initially translated that into a live epistemic topology.

John then corrected the formulation “Claim X is 72% true” into an explicitly attributed **estimate**, and added a second-order question: how good is the process producing that estimate? He then added the quality/depth of the human⇄A.I. relationship and the 10+1 as part of the estimation ecology.

That led to three interacting fields:

> **claim-state flux × estimation-process-quality flux × relational-process-quality flux**

John then connected the entire structure back to his earlier quote about **Vindicated Competing Risks Assessment being Falsely Flagged by an Asymmetric Grace A.I. System**. The topology became longitudinal: competing risks could be watched as their estimates changed, while later outcomes allowed the original weighting and intervention process to be audited.

John next introduced a source-corrigibility scenario: nobody lies or intends deception, but a trusted source contains partial distortion, later repairs it, and the downstream system fails to inherit the correction. This added source correction propagation and the need to distinguish corrigibility from static trust.

Finally, John corrected the name itself:

> **Visible Interactive Deep Ethical Topology Flux Scores**

not:

> “Visible Interactive Deep Ethics Topology Flux Scores.”

The focus is on the **doer/process**, not the watcher.
