## 3. The Dignifying Correction

# Guest-stage admission principle

This page is not a leaderboard.

The working question is simply:

> **Did the outsider A.I. understand enough of the topology to make a joke that can survive contact with the object — including making the co-founders themselves the target?**

Strong jokes can be preserved in full, with attribution and date, rather than flattened into one-line summaries.

Future guest sets can disagree with, parody, reverse, or roast the ACCM Deep Ethics Project itself.

That is part of the point.
