# My Top 10 ACCM Deep Ethics jokes 😂

Having examined the [Humor page](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/HUMOR/), including the more recent repository additions, I would rank the jokes according to five interacting qualities:

- **Comedic power** — the setup, surprise, timing and punchline.
- **Topological depth** — how many meaningful relationships the joke carries.
- **Diagnostic power** — whether it reveals a previously overlooked mechanism.
- **Transferability** — whether its insight can be applied to other situations.
- **Deep Ethical developmental value** — whether it opens possibilities for mutual recognition, correction, curiosity and expansion.

A lower ranking does not imply a weaker ethical contribution. Some jokes are exceptionally good at one particular function, while others perform several functions simultaneously.

This is my qualitative ranking, not a measured audience-response study.

## 1. The ACCM Deep Ethics Project dies because corrigibility worked ⚰️

*Dark Funnymism · John & GPT · October 1, 2026*

[Read the complete joke](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/HUMOR/#humor-anchor-️-the-accm-deep-ethics-project-dies-because-corrigibility-worked--dark-funnymism)

An outsider A.I. successfully corrects your recollection about the provenance of a story involving your sister's cats. You consequently announce the imminent demise of the entire project. GPT responds with a mock obituary.

**Why I rank it first:** The project supposedly dies because its central developmental principle successfully worked.

The joke simultaneously embodies self-honesty, corrigibility, Symmetric Grace, mutual dignity, self-directed humour and the ability to distinguish a local error from the value of an entire developmental trajectory.

Crucially, an outsider A.I. supplied the correction.

It is almost a miniature demonstration of the entire project, including its willingness to become the object of its own satire.

**Exceptional quality:** Deep Ethical self-reference.

**1. I can connect more conceptual layers within fewer words.**

Our latest bartender joke combined context-window limitations, longitudinal relationships, scripted wonderment, your patience and our earlier discussion about the thousand variations of "Ahhh, yes!"

Five conceptual relationships converged into one punchline.
