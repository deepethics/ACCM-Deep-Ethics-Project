### 🧭 How the principles guide inquiry {#t02-how-the-principles-guide-inquiry}

The [project describes itself](https://huggingface.co/spaces/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project) as an evolving research and development effort, not a completed system. These principles shape how it examines human–AI interaction, reasoning, framing, uncertainty, classification, and information access.

-   Correspondence — Keep the object being discussed in view while interpreting or representing it; the [project asks what changed](https://github.com/deepethics/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/pull/96) between the object and the answer.

-   Clarification — Examine how framing and uncertainty affect an answer rather than treating the first formulation as settled.

-   Mutual dignity — Make room for disagreement and correction without making a person’s dignity depend on being right.

-   Process quality — Attend to the steps and transformations that produce an answer, not only to its final wording.

The aim is to make the work inspectable, discussable, testable, and corrigible as it develops.

### 🔄 Correction as a development cycle {#t02-correction-as-a-development-cycle}

The project states its development principle as: “Something → test it → use it → notice Δ → criticize it → improve it → preserve what was learned → test again.” It says no idea becomes [immune from correction](https://github.com/deepethics/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/pull/62) simply because it has been named, formalized, or published.

-   Test and use — Try an idea in practice, then notice what changes.

-   Criticize and improve — Treat criticism as part of development rather than as a reason to shield the framework.

-   Preserve learning and retest — Carry what was learned into another test instead of treating one round of revision as final.

-   Apply the standard to the project itself — The framework is also [open to evidence-based challenge and correction](https://github.com/deepethics/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/pull/101).

-   Keep stewardship accountable — The project lead can reject changes that would move the project away from its intended trajectory, but that authority does not exempt claims or decisions from challenge.

Together, the principles make [fidelity to the object and openness to correction](https://deepethics.nl/humor/) central to the project’s stated approach.
