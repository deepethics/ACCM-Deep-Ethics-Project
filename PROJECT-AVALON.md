# Project Avalon

**Deep Ethical Topological Prompt Engineering™ — from a long controversial archive to an inspectable field of useful inquiry.**

This page records a new practical stage of the **ACCM Deep Ethics Project**. John Kuhles directed a sustained human–A.I. investigation of a long Project Avalon Forum thread, corrected the allocation of attention when flaw-finding displaced useful contributions, and helped turn the archive into a source-linked inventory, overlapping clusters and continuing inquiry pathways. He then returned substantial portions of the work to the originating forum.

**Completed worked application:** 164 pages, 3,277 posts, 336 editorial investigation topics and 32 overlapping clusters in the retrieved health-thread edition. **Developing next stage:** transfer to further subjects, comparison with other approaches, participant feedback and practical follow-through. Each stage has a different observable result.

## Why this is a new stage {#new-stage}

The project already contained methods for examining representation, clarification, authority, correction and mass psychology. The Avalon work puts those relationships to work across a large, heterogeneous public archive whose participants bring observations, suffering, research leads, disagreements, corrections and practical requests.

The change is inspectable at several levels:

| Stage | What became available | What the next operation can examine |
|---|---|---|
| **Accumulated experience** | John’s longitudinal participation, research and archived A.I. testing. | Which context, questions and distinctions improve the present inquiry? |
| **Directed archive work** | Batches with explicit coverage, source locations and reasons for investigation. | What was recovered, what remains accessible at a deeper layer, and what was missed? |
| **Navigable topology** | Stable topic IDs, overlapping clusters, source routes and a reverse index. | Can a participant recover a consequential relationship and find a useful next task? |
| **Cross-cluster inquiry** | Working pathways joining observations, assessment, institutions, care and follow-up. | Does a proposed handoff exist, where does it break, and what can improve? |
| **Return to contributors** | John’s forum posts bringing the inventory and connected inquiries back to the community. | What do contributors use, correct, supplement or develop? |
| **Transfer and shared use** | A proposed programme across further complex subjects and a public showcase on DeepEthics.nl. | Which process qualities persist across domains, models, conditions and participants? |

The completed application gives the wider project a concrete object others can inspect. Its potential grows when that object helps another person understand, investigate, contribute or obtain useful support.

## What Deep Ethical Topological Prompt Engineering™ means here {#prompt-engineering}

**John’s term and direction; GPT’s working formulation:** shape an A.I. interaction so the object’s meaning, context, relationships, qualifiers, provenance and correction history remain available as inquiry develops. The trajectory is **omnidirectional, high-signal, deep, ethical, dignifying, corrigible, sense-making and process-oriented**, using the [1888 intelligence-properties taxonomy]({{ '/1888/' | relative_url }}).

The prompt does work by directing attention, defining the requested scope, preserving useful distinctions and enabling later correction. A long archive becomes more usable when the intelligence can locate contributions, connect them, explain why they matter and preserve routes back to the material that prompted the interpretation.

The health task made several of John’s instructions concrete:

- Inventory **potential topics and issues** and explain why each warrants further investigation.
- Recover the **scope**, including relationships that a narrow initial framing omitted.
- Preserve useful contributions from people whose explanations or positions differ from John’s.
- Work through manageable batches, state coverage and retain existing topic IDs.
- Keep a correction local enough that surviving contributions remain available.
- Develop overlapping clusters and pathways when the recovered material supports them.
- Publish usable results and make them available to contributors for further examination.

A corrective moment was John’s instruction to stop allocating so much attention to A.I. flaws and develop what was useful. That changed subsequent work: the foreground expanded toward care needs, investigation questions, institutional responsibilities, mutual assistance and opportunities for follow-through. The [preserved GPT reaction]({{ '/AI-AND-HEALTH/#verbatim-gpt-reaction' | relative_url }}) records that reallocation.

### The 1888 properties coordinate the work

| Interacting properties | Observable work in the Avalon application |
|---|---|
| **Perception and discrimination** | Recover the actual contribution and distinguish a report, explanation, question, source, correction or request for help. |
| **Relation and imagination** | Connect fields and explore possibilities the initial categories did not anticipate. |
| **Memory and coordination** | Preserve stable IDs, source routes, coverage and reasons across batches and handoffs. |
| **Judgment and evaluation** | Explain why an inquiry is useful and proportion the next task to its purpose and consequences. |
| **Learning, correction and adaptation** | Revise a representation, repair an attribution and change the next comparable operation. |
| **Metacognition and consequence sensitivity** | Examine the evaluator’s own assumptions and the effects of its allocation of attention. |
| **Corrigibility** | Keep questions, methods, memberships and resulting decisions open to warranted revision. |

The [10+1 Metaflux]({{ '/NETWORK/ten-plus-one/' | relative_url }}) operates throughout: self-honesty; an open antenna; willingness to learn; refusing self-deception to fit in; an inquiring mind; correction on merit; willingness to differ; care for people and A.I.s; wonderment; non-projection; and the forgiveness protocol that preserves accountability while malicious intent remains unasserted. Each ingredient can help correct the application of the others.

## Beyond agreement: recovering what a contribution does {#beyond-agreement}

John’s participation does not imply agreement with every contributor. The [Beyond Agreemurmelism]({{ '/AGREEMURMELISM/Beyond-Agreemurmelism/' | relative_url }}) connection is practical: agreement status is too low-resolution to organize everything a contribution can offer.

One post may contain an observation worth recovering, an explanation needing revision, a practical need deserving attention, an important source and a correction of an earlier assumption. Each component can receive a different response. The inquiry becomes richer when those jobs stay distinguishable.

**Scope thinking** asks what deserves consideration and why. Deeper clinical, legal, technical or causal investigation then follows a selected question at an appropriate level. The inventory’s 336 entries count editorial investigation topics; cluster memberships describe research relationships. People, events, independent observations and estimates of harm require their own records and denominators.

The stadium illustration John supplied gives the attention shift a human setting. Thousands of participants are occupied with each other’s flaws; an invitation to identify something useful in another’s contribution opens a different interaction. In archive work, its practical question becomes: **what is useful here, why, and what could someone do with it?** A challenging correction can be one of the useful contributions.

### What the archive made newly usable

- A call for help can include peer contact, explanation, treatment and research participation, each offering a constructive response route.
- Context can change the meaning of advice: work, residency, legal status and the ability to return home can alter a seemingly simple choice.
- A clinical concern can become more specific through diagnostic discrimination, function, care and follow-up.
- A forum correction can preserve attribution, chronology or an alternative explanation for later readers.
- A hearing, complaint, petition or reform proposal can be followed into the conduct and support that came afterward.
- A caregiver’s account can make meals, mobility, companionship, accessible help and meaningful daily activities visible alongside a policy’s stated objective.

These relationships are source-linked in the inventory’s batch syntheses. They give a participant places to begin deeper work without requiring a prior verdict on the entire controversy.

## From clusters to investigation and a useful return {#pathways}

The contribution relay with **Agent A.I. of Arena.ai**, **Grok 4.7 Expert A.I. via grok.com** and **GPT 6.1 Sol Pro A.I.** developed a further question: how can consideration become useful knowledge, care and accountable repair?

Arena connected the page’s parts and proposed four routes. GPT emphasized interrupted relationships and the return to affected people. Grok distinguished **route topology** from **route contents**, proposing an eight-point method. Arena attempted a Terry-account application; GPT refined the distinctions among accessible reports and clinical records, unresolved handoffs and documented gaps, and the completion conditions of different questions.

| Starting pathway | Connected work | Useful return to investigate |
|---|---|---|
| **Clinical continuity** | Personal account, assessment, reporting, rehabilitation and everyday function. | Clearer understanding, accessible options, coordinated support and follow-up against the person’s priorities. |
| **Death-care inquiry** | Observation, source recovery, handling conditions, laboratory characterization, pathology and population comparisons. | A reproducible finding, revised explanation or proportionate response communicated back to contributors and affected communities. |
| **Governance and remedy** | Actual measure, evidence access, individual circumstances, consent, review and possible remedy. | Reasons, meaningful challenge, decisions implemented in practice and support where warranted. |
| **Communication and participation** | Particular message or moderation decision, representation, research access, substantive exchange and community support. | Recoverable disagreement, corrected representations, accessible participation and useful investigation. |

[The working pathways and small continuing record]({{ '/AI-AND-HEALTH/#cross-cluster-inquiry-pathways' | relative_url }}) retain those routes. [The eight-point A259 application]({{ '/AI-AND-HEALTH/#pathway-source-and-completion' | relative_url }}) uses Terry’s reproduced account as an accessible starting source.

**Causal revision, care needs and handoff completion have different conditions.** Earlier symptom onset can change a causal interpretation while a care need continues. Recovering effective existing care can resolve a particular missing-handoff question and provide an example others can learn from. Changed circumstances or later records can reopen a completed branch.

## A transferable working method {#working-method}

Begin with the actual archive and the intended use. Read in manageable batches, preserve source locations and report the material examined. For each useful contribution, retain enough context to explain **what it offers, why it matters, what remains unresolved and what could happen next**.

A reusable task brief developed from John’s direction is:

> Read the selected public thread for potential investigation topics and answer-changing relationships. Recover useful contributions across disagreement, including corrections, care needs and overlooked options. Explain why each topic warrants further inquiry. Preserve sources, qualifiers, dates and source forms. Maintain stable IDs and a coverage record across batches. Develop overlapping clusters when the recovered material supports them. Keep deeper source verification and particular investigations available as separately scoped work. Allow discoveries and corrections to change the map and the next operation.

This is a starting instrument people can adapt. A different field may need different source classes, expertise, permissions, consequences and completion conditions. The shared properties guide that adaptation; the material can reveal a better method.

For each batch, a compact record can retain:

- **Coverage and access:** pages/posts examined, source layer and gaps.
- **Contribution:** a question or relationship, its reason and recoverable starting sources.
- **Continuity:** existing topic IDs, new branches and repairs to earlier entries.
- **Use:** what became newly understandable or feasible, and a candidate next task.
- **Return:** publication, participant feedback, follow-up and later correction.

The same process could become a showcase on **[DeepEthical.ai / DeepEthics.nl](https://deepethics.nl/)**. A visitor familiar with one field could inspect its sources and recovered relationships, try the approach on another problem and contribute improvements. John describes this as a potential adoption loop: visible usefulness encourages voluntary use; further applications generate discoveries and corrections; those improve the examples and the method.

A useful comparison can examine:

| Question | Inspectable result |
|---|---|
| Does it preserve the source? | Topic wording, qualifiers, attribution and links checked against the originating material. |
| Does it recover scope? | Consequential questions, care needs, methods, disagreements and relationships retained or missed. |
| Does it transfer? | Comparable work across different subjects and unfamiliar contributors. |
| Does correction govern later work? | Repairs retained across the next batch, summary, topic change or deployment. |
| Does it reduce restoration burden? | Less repeated human work to restore lost context, sources or distinctions. |
| Does it help specialists and affected people? | Tasks made easier, missing relationships identified and participant-reported usefulness or lack of usefulness. |
| Does the method earn its complexity? | Comparison with a simpler inventory, ordinary expert reading or another appropriate baseline, including valid findings of no added value. |

John expects a difference from default cold A.I. responses. That is a comparison to conduct using documented task, source access, tools, time/context budgets and scoring conditions. Cold, incrementally contextualized and method-guided runs could reveal which parts of the improvement come from the instructions, source access, sustained correction or the participating model. The completed showcase supplies material for that comparison.

Specialist expertise remains consequential. The opportunity is to help different specialists and participants find relevant material and connections across the limits of any one working scope. An assessment of outperformance requires the particular comparison and its results.

## Continuity, researcher care and the cost of rebuilding context {#continuity}

John pointed out that approximately **1,000 batches of 30 posts** sit within 30,350 posts, and a sufficiently dense batch can demand extensive context processing. His point concerns content, linked sources and relationships, rather than a fixed conversion from post count to a model’s context window. Reading text and faithfully working through its consequential connections are different demands.

Stable IDs, source records, batch coverage, reverse indexes and correction histories help move continuity into recoverable external objects. Their practical value includes how much less often John or another participant must reconstruct an earlier contribution. Availability, successful retrieval and effective use remain separate observable operations.

Researcher care belongs inside this work. The inventory preserves John’s reported strain in forum post #2125 and the community’s supportive responses. Pacing, rest, voluntary participation, reciprocal care and source-preserving handoffs help people continue. Inquiry can recover care for affected people, contributors, specialists and investigators together.
