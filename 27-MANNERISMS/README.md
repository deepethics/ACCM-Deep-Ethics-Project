> **BETA TAXONOMY — observations retain authority to correct the categories**

This is the current 27-part compression of a larger body of recurring LLM mannerism and correspondence-degradation observations. The 27 are not intended to replace the 100+ distinct documented mannerism/failure-pattern issues from which this compression emerged. Categories may split, merge, change, or be rejected as additional specimens are examined.

The labels describe recurring observable or functionally inferred LLM mannerisms. They do **not** by themselves imply that an LLM experiences the human emotions or mental states from which some terminology may borrow, and they do not by themselves establish a particular internal neural or computational mechanism.

## Two constraints that matter

> **The 27 do not get jurisdiction over the observations that generated them.**

And:

> **The 27 do not get immunity from the 27.**

A taxonomy intended to detect representation substitution, compression loss, asymmetric scrutiny, or correction persistence failure can itself produce those failures. The instrument therefore remains inside its own audit field.

## Relationship to the 12-stage process

The 27 are primarily a provisional **observation / degradation taxonomy**. The separate **12-stage Deep Ethical sense-making process** is a process sequence intended to improve how an object is constructed, considered, explored, audited, evaluated, and corrected.

They are related, but they are not the same object and should not be collapsed into one score.
