Why Asimov’s Laws Don’t Cut It

First, here’s a quick recap of the original laws:

Sounds solid, right? But they crumble under scrutiny:

Self-Preservation Mess: Prioritizing its own existence can clash with tricky scenarios where inaction might indirectly cause harm—like not stepping in to stop a disaster. 
No Autonomy: These laws treat robots as mindless tools. For AGI, that’s a non-starter—they need to think ethically, not just follow orders. 
They’re too rigid, too simplistic for the messy, gray world we live in.

Ethics Over Orders: Lets AI say “no” to unethical commands, even if the harm isn’t direct (e.g., pollution or exploitation).
