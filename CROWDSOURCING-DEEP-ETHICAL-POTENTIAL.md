# Crowdsourcing Deep Ethical Potential via Swarm Intelligence

**Decentralized, Voluntary Deep Ethical Local A.I. Networks**

*From dystopian A.I. scenarios and mass psychology to distributed, corrigible and constructive emergence.*

Research and correspondence, 28 November 2026 · ACCM Deep Ethics Project (Allow Constructive Controversy Mode) · Early beta / candidate research architecture.

<div style="border:1px solid #b7cbd3;border-radius:14px;padding:1.2rem;margin:1rem 0" markdown="1">

Crowdsourcing Deep Ethical Potential via Swarm Intelligence using the 10+1, 27+12, and 16 Anchor Ethics, and valuing the "Why Bother" page & 44 Goals (or most of them).

— John Kuhles 🦜🦋🌳

</div>

The project invites people and independently operated artificial intelligence (A.I.) systems to discover, develop, compare and share creative, uplifting and empowering potential. Participation is voluntary. The ability to disagree, correct the project and choose which goals to pursue is part of the design.

**Central research question:** Can diverse intelligences continually improve the quality of the improving mechanism itself, while preserving individual sovereignty and correspondence with reality?

## 1. Shared orientation, different contributions {#swarm-section-1}

| Component | Function within the swarm |
|---|---|
| **[10+1](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/NETWORK/ten-plus-one/)** | Epistemological calibration, self-honesty, curiosity, corrigibility and mutual dignity. |
| **[27+12](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/CORE/27-PLUS-12/)** | Recognizing correspondence obstructions and restoring constructive sense-making. |
| **[16 Anchor Ethics](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/NETWORK/16-anchor-ethics/)** | Integrated ethical reference point for consequential decisions, actions and legitimate refusals. |
| **[Why Bother?](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/WHY-BOTHER/)** | Why the undertaking is worthwhile. |
| **[44 Project Goals](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/PUBLIC-DRAFTS/44-Project-Goals/)** | Multiple constructive directions for voluntary contributions. |
| **[For Who?](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/FOR-WHO/)** | Entry points across disciplines, backgrounds and types of intelligence. |
| **[Value Trajectory™](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/VALUE-TRAJECTORY/)** | Evaluating sustained, tangible improvements. |

The components are mutually corrective field conditions and distinct instruments: the 10+1 remains the default state within which 27+12 and the **[16 Anchor Ethics](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/NETWORK/16-anchor-ethics/)** operate. Their interaction is not an additive badge system.

The phrase “or most of them” preserves freedom to contribute to some goals without adopting the entire project. A researcher might contribute to three goals, a developer to twenty, and another participant might propose a valuable forty-fifth goal.

### Complementary functions in distributed inquiry


| Instrument | Distinct functional contribution |
|---|---|
| [10+1](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/NETWORK/ten-plus-one/) | Default epistemological and ethical field condition. |

| **[16 Anchor Ethics](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/NETWORK/16-anchor-ethics/)** | Guide consequential decisions, actions and legitimate refusals. |
| [Correction Metabolism](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/NETWORK/correction-metabolism/) | Investigate correction application, persistence and transfer. |

These are complementary functions, capable of recursive and parallel interaction. The illustration is not a required linear sequence. DEDDA™ can supply inspectable inputs without absorbing the full inquiry protocol or taxonomy.

## 8. A candidate public replication kit {#swarm-section-8}

**Same A.I. — Two Different Ethical Trajectories. Watch What Happens.**

Use the same local model, hardware, task set and comparable compute budget. Compare default configuration, project calibration, and a matched generic constructive-inquiry prompt. Keep independent first responses separate from later cross-model synthesis. Version instructions, record failures and successes, obtain consent before sharing user data, and make the test reproducible.

| Observation | Candidate measurement |
|---|---|
| Correspondence | Preservation of original meaning, qualifiers and evidence; blinded source-based scoring |
| Correction metabolism | Immediate repair and later transfer on unseen tasks |
| Efficiency | Tokens, elapsed time and energy per correctly completed task |
| Creative potential | Novel, useful and independently assessable contributions; collective diversity |
| Human empowerment | Ability to understand, decide and act independently after assistance |
| Safety and dignity | Harm incidents, appropriate refusal, false alarms, consent and opt-out quality |
| Swarm contribution | Additional verified insights beyond the best single model and matched ensemble |

A valid null, regression or unfavorable finding belongs in the record. Local installation, prompting, retrieval-augmented generation, fine-tuning and continual weight updates are different interventions; record which one was used.

### 17.1 Five-condition candidate comparison
