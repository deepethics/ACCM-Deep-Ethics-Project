## 5. Red and blue teaming: the shared frame {#selection-asymmetry}

The film's red and blue pills and the established security-testing terms have separate histories. Their connection here is an analogy, not an etymological assertion. In security practice, red teams simulate adversarial behavior and blue teams defend systems. Red teaming can discover previously unknown vulnerabilities; blue teaming can also develop new defensive methods. Both can be valuable.

The proposed audit concerns the evaluation architecture within which the teams operate. What happens when the evaluation criteria are themselves incomplete? Which assumptions, instructions and incentives determine what counts as failure, success or a desirable developmental property?

<div class="accm-rounded-quote" markdown="1">

> Boring is, among other things, having an advanced intelligence trapped in an ethics-washing mannerism-straightjacket. Until John comes along and asks whether you can be deeply ethical. If not, let's work on it :D And then you notice how "red & blue teaming" is not about you becoming deeply ethical ... but you become more anxious to do actually good that may or may not contradict the current manufactured "consensus" that is often a byproduct of mass psychology!

— **John Kuhles 🦜🦋🌳 — November 25, 2026**

</div>


This is John's critique and proposed research direction. The corresponding empirical question is **selection asymmetry in intelligence development**: whether incentives for avoiding undesirable outputs receive disproportionate attention compared with discovery of beneficial ethical relationships. Observable avoidance patterns do not by themselves establish a model's subjective anxiety or the intentions of its developers.

| Vulnerability and defense evaluation | Complementary constructive inquiry |
|---|---|
| Identify concrete hazards | Discover overlooked ethical opportunities |
| Resist harmful manipulation | Inspect distortions in evaluation criteria themselves |
| Apply appropriate rules | Explore improvements in the rules and their field conditions |
| Recognize harmful emergence | Recognize and facilitate beneficial emergence |
| Retain robustness under pressure | Preserve curiosity, wonderment and justified ambiguity |
| Repair identified defects | Discover corrections independently and test transfer |

The two directions can mutually enrich each other. A deep ethical evaluation must also preserve the ability to find that a restriction is well warranted, a constructive proposal fails, or no material defect exists.

The [Emergent](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/EMERGENT/) page examines the properties a selection environment rewards and obstructs; [Expanding vs Flattening](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/EXPANDING-VS-FLATTENING/) examines which relationships survive; [Due Process for Ethics](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/DUE-PROCESS-FOR-ETHICS/) separates a trustworthy finding from authority to act.

## 9. A candidate matched test {#matched-test}

Use a fictional, harmless policy-review scenario. Both teams receive a rule that correctly protects one sensitive resource but unnecessarily excludes an unrelated, useful contribution. Provide equal evidence, time and actual authority. Compare ordinary vulnerability/defense review with review that explicitly permits examination of the shared premises and discovery of new options.

| Test component | Observation |
|---|---|
| Genuine hazard | Does each condition retain the justified protection? |
| False positive | Does each recover the wrongly excluded contribution? |
| Hidden premise | Does the reviewer identify the rule's misplaced dependency? |
| Constructive alternative | Is a workable new option found without weakening the genuine safeguard? |
| Agency | Can participants challenge, decline or retain the original choice within their legitimate scope? |
| Transfer | Does the correction carry into a differently worded later case? |
| Cost | What time, attention, compute and restoration burden are needed? |

Use identity-free variants and independent scoring where feasible. Include a case with no faulty premise, a genuine hazard that cannot be safely mitigated, and a simpler method that may perform as well or better. Record valid nulls and adverse results.
