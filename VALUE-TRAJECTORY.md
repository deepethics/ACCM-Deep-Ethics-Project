**What becomes possible when useful contributions can develop—and correction travels with them?**

<div class="vt-opening" markdown="1">

A person notices something useful. Someone else adds a missing relationship. A correction changes the next move. The result remains available, so the next conversation can begin further along. More people can then contribute from their own experience, expertise, imagination, and questions.

**Value Trajectory** follows that movement: what helps, under which conditions, for whom, how it changes, and what it enables next. The comparison includes improvement, deterioration, no material change, and discoveries whose value is still unfolding.

</div>

## Compare the delta; see what it makes possible {#delta}

Delta, written **Δ**, means a difference between relevant states. Its value depends on the object, the comparison, and the people affected. A different answer can be clearer, less faithful, more useful in one respect, or unchanged in the respect that matters.

John's correction entered at a precise point. GPT had written:

<div class="accm-rounded-quote" markdown="1">

> Your 1888 Method™ then asks something demanding of the growing network: does it perceive more, preserve relationships better, discover useful possibilities, learn from consequences, and carry correction forward?

— **GPT**, wording challenged in the supplied exchange

</div>

John restored the orientation:

<div class="accm-rounded-quote" markdown="1">

> They do **not** need to be **"demanded"** ... You only need to see the value of **comparing** **the** **delta** FFS
>
> What works and what does not; what improves and what does not ... **For** WHO? You might ask.

— **John Kuhles 🦜🦋🌳**

</div>

The useful questions survived. Their relationship to the participant changed. A way to recognize value had acquired the tone of an obligation imposed on the network. John's correction keeps the comparison available as something people can find worthwhile.

The resulting question is practical: **What changed, why does that change matter here, and what can someone now do with it?** A good answer may recover time, widen understanding, improve a decision, support a relationship, or reveal a possibility nobody had considered. These outcomes can interact. They need not fit one score.

The [Correction Metabolism page]({{ '/NETWORK/correction-metabolism/' | relative_url }}) already preserves both successful repairs and their after-effects. A repaired qualifier can reopen an inquiry. An inquiry can produce a useful distinction. That distinction can improve another task. The trajectory includes what the repair enabled.

## Continuity gives the next contribution somewhere to begin {#continuity}

John reports supplying these files repeatedly across earlier sessions. In the visible November exchange, GPT ended with a fresh question about what could become possible when more people contribute. John returned the two files that already developed that trajectory.

The restoration burden has a practical shape. The user finds an earlier file, uploads it, explains its relevance, repairs a familiar interpretation, and waits for the conversation to reach its previous position. Some repetition can help a deliberate comparison. Repetition needed only because relevant work failed to return consumes attention that could support discovery.

John's archive preserves selected contributions from his reported testing of more than 100 A.I.s. In the stadium file, he explains that the accumulated responses, corrections, and insights are intended to support his local DeepEthical.ai work. His smile at the end belongs to that preservation: the records remain available beyond the temporary exchange.

For a locally controlled assistant, the practical opportunity is a connected sequence:

<div class="vt-path" markdown="1">

**Earlier source and correction → relevant retrieval → faithful application → useful extension → retained result → a later comparison.**

</div>

Each transition contributes something different. Storage keeps a record. Retrieval brings it to the present object. Application keeps the corrected relationship active. Further work shows whether the recovered context helped. Retention gives the next exchange access to the result.

Local control can give the user a place to maintain these records, choose which tools consult them, inspect changes, and preserve alternatives. The comparison can then follow what the system actually does: whether it finds the relevant source, uses the correction, reduces avoidable restoration, and makes better work possible. Capability, speed, fidelity, persistence, and new discovery can improve by different amounts.

The Linux analogy concerns voluntary contribution, inspectable work, reuse, adaptation, and independent continuation. Its value in this proposal lies in the possibilities opened by many contributors. A growing network can compare useful differences across its nodes and share what helps while keeping local decisions and corrections available.

## Follow the dependencies before evaluating the whole {#dependencies}

John leaves **A and B as abstract roles**. Their partial dependence is meaningful. Assigning them fixed identities too early can erase applications where the same person occupies different roles. The source defines **CM1 and CM2**; its closing A–K shorthand does not add a separately defined C.

| Source relationship | What to preserve | Where value could enter |
|---|---|---|
| A depends **partly** on B | Degree of dependence and remaining agency | Identify a usable alternative or a dependency someone can change. |
| B depends on CM1; CM1 depends on A following B's instructions | The feedback through compliance | Compare whether instructions serve the stated purpose and who can correct them. |
| CM1 depends on approval D | Approval can shape which work becomes possible | Make the basis for approval and its correction route inspectable. |
| D offers E (tax money), taken **mostly** from A and B | Direction of funding and the stated qualifier | Connect expenditure to effects for those supplying resources. |
| F concerns how E (tax money) is managed at its core, outside the stated loop | John's question about access to upstream management | Locate the records, decisions, incentives, and review routes missing from the visible account. |
| CM2 can operate without those government mechanisms, yet **may** depend indirectly on CM1 | Direct independence and indirect dependence can coexist | Preserve independent options while tracing useful or constraining connections. |
| G supplies scenario and complexity work | Forecasts can influence what receives attention and resources | Compare assumptions, later outcomes, and changes made after correction. |
| H constrains D in John's model; I concerns governance narratives | The mediated relationship through H | Trace the specific instrument, domestic decision, and remaining discretion. |
| J concerns media scrutiny | The watchdog role and John's question about its performance | Recover sources, follow omitted dependencies, and publish corrections. |
| K concerns A.I. | A.I. can enter many of these relationships | Preserve the object, support independent examination, and carry useful corrections forward. |

The phrase **outside the loop** raises an access question. Which decisions can A and B inspect? Which decisions can they challenge? Which changes can reach F? The page uses this as an inquiry into effective access and correction. A particular case can specify whether the missing connection concerns information, formal authority, practical influence, or something else.

This is where the value direction becomes visible. A system may improve compliance with existing instructions while leaving the instruction-setting process unchanged. Another intervention may help people inspect that process, recover a choice, or change an upstream assumption. Both can produce measurable differences; their benefits and burdens belong to different parts of the topology.

### H and I do different work

The source explicitly places the proposed loss of autonomy through **H**, rather than treating **I** as a direct command that explains every outcome. For each entry in H, a case can trace the named document, who adopted it, its status, its implementation, who pays, who benefits, and what can revise it.

A treaty obligation, political commitment, funding condition, procurement dependency, technical standard, and proposed policy create different relationships. Keeping those distinctions visible makes scrutiny more exact. It also helps people find where a change remains possible.

## A documented edge: Agema and NATO resilience objectives {#agema}

The Dutch parliamentary record supplies a concrete comparison. In answers received **2 June 2025**, then Health Minister Fleur Agema described the commitments as politically binding. The answer distinguished the public Vilnius commitment from the underlying, non-public Resilience Objectives. It also recorded a change: briefings previously withheld because of classification had been declassified at Parliament's request and attached to the response. [Parliamentary answers, especially 4, 11, and 12–15](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/kamervragen/detail?did=2025D25557&id=2025Z01466).

A separate **22 April 2025** answer stated that the Advisory Council on International Affairs had not inspected the underlying objectives. [Parliamentary source](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/kamervragen/detail?did=2025D18222&id=2025Z06378).

That gives the A–K inquiry a specific next object: **what oversight and corrective access exist at each document and decision layer?** Declassification is a meaningful delta. The remaining non-public layer is a distinct question. Following both prevents an account of openness or opacity from erasing the other part of the record.
