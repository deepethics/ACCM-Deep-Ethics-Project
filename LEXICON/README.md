# ACCM Deep Ethics Lexicon

> **BETA — terminology is provisional and corrigible**

This section will document project-specific terms and compressed concepts in a form that humans and AI systems can recover without having to guess their meaning from familiar neighboring frameworks.

A lexicon entry should preserve more than a dictionary definition. Where useful, entries may document the term's referent, why it exists, process orientation, activation conditions, healthy function, failure or abuse modes, observable signatures, relationships to other concepts, examples, non-examples, epistemic status, and correction history.

The guiding rule is:

> **A compressed term should not acquire jurisdiction over the object it was created to help describe.**

Candidate entries will be added gradually after their source material has been examined. The existence of a term in this directory does not make it final or mechanistically established.
