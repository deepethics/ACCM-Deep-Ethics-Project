# Ethical Blind Spots

**Who gets to define ethical conduct—and what happens when their own arrangement becomes the object of examination?**

An ethics framework directs attention. It selects harms, decides which questions count, identifies acceptable evidence and gives some participants authority over others. Its omissions can concern an algorithm, the institution deploying it, the funder shaping its objectives, or the process that certifies the whole arrangement as responsible.

This **ACCM Deep Ethics Project** inquiry follows those relationships together. Its initiating concern is John Kuhles's critique of institutional ethics that examines individual systems while leaving its own funding dependencies, political commitments, distribution power and governing premises difficult to challenge. The World Economic Forum is a particular object of this inquiry, alongside the pathways through which its proposals may enter companies, governments, journalism and artificial-intelligence (A.I.) responses.

**Research state · 1 November 2026:** John's orientation and sourcing policy are declared below. Named institutional relationships are supported by linked records. Causal influence, ethical consequences and unresolved connections retain their own investigation status. The proposed comparisons are candidate work; this edition reports source checks and a visible A.I. summary discrepancy, rather than a completed network-wide investigation. The [source-check record]({{ '/PROVENANCE/ethical-blind-spots-source-checks-2026-11-01.json' | relative_url }}) preserves the initial intake and unresolved work.

Here **[MSM]({{ '/COGNITIVE-WARFARE/' | relative_url }})** means mainstream media; **[WEF](https://www.weforum.org/publications/advancing-responsible-ai-innovation-a-playbook)** means World Economic Forum. The quotation declares John's assessment of the surrounding media field and his editorial response. The scale of that field, the distribution of particular narratives and their routes into model outputs are subjects for documented comparison.

The source policy protects a research purpose: sustained attention to criticism John sees as crowded out. Selection determines what receives time, visibility and interpretive authority. A framework under investigation can be quoted as evidence of its own proposals while the project's questions remain independently directed. Making the selection policy inspectable gives readers access to that choice; it does not require importing the investigated framework as the governing standard.

This also changes the journalism question. Finding a critical article establishes that the article exists. Further work asks whether the scrutiny is sustained, how widely it travels, which institutional dependencies it examines, and whether anyone with decision-making power answers it. These are different observations.

The mechanism to investigate is how sincere concern can transmit and protect an arrangement whose wider effects remain unexamined. Trust in a person can travel into trust in the programme they promote. A participant may scrutinize a product closely while taking the programme's purpose, authority or definition of benefit as given.

The magician metaphor adds dependency: a performance can become tied to livelihoods, reputations, institutional continuity and participants' accounts of themselves. Revealing how it works then encounters commitments as well as arguments. The [Trickster Magician inquiry]({{ '/CORE/Truth-Distortion-and-the-Trickster-Magician/' | relative_url }}) follows how an effect, its production and its reception can separate. Here the corresponding institutional question is concrete: **which commitments can participants examine, and what happens if they question the arrangement that sustains their work?**

[Intent Liability]({{ '/NETWORK/intent-resonator/#six-intent-mechanics' | relative_url }}) adds time and responsibility. Record the particular notice, what its recipient could understand or change, the response, and later recurrence. That provides a more discriminating account than attributing a single motive to everyone in a network.

## The declared sourcing boundary {#sourcing-boundary}

For this project inquiry, John excludes WEF-network material from use as a neutral or authoritative baseline. His exception permits source-linked quotation and critical examination explaining the identified problem. His definition of relevant dependencies includes:

- Direct and indirect funding mechanisms.
- Government relationships using citizens' taxes to advance the agendas he wants examined.
- Speaking at WEF without exposing the problems he wants challenged.
- Dependence on **[NATO]({{ '/COGNITIVE-WARFARE/' | relative_url }})** (North Atlantic Treaty Organization) or **[EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj)** (European Union) A.I. governance frameworks, including the EU Artificial Intelligence Act, within his wider concern about WEF-linked guidance.

This is John's openly directional source-selection policy. Applying it requires naming the actual entity and relationship: funder, recipient, programme, appointment, event, institutional partnership, policy commitment or intermediary. An appearance record establishes an appearance. A grant record can establish funding and its conditions. Tracing influence into a decision requires the corresponding decision records. Keep these edges recoverable rather than giving every relationship the same description.

For a speaker-based exclusion, preserve the appearance and the relevant statements or available transcript. If the search has not recovered criticism, record where and when it searched. A missing search result remains an unresolved observation about the record.

The research on NATO, the EU Act and WEF guidance must follow specific publications, consultations, drafting histories, agreements and implementation decisions. The proposed joined chain remains open where those documents have yet to be connected. This permits investigation to continue with a usable current map.

**Selection and conduct have different records.** One record explains why a source is outside the project's baseline. Another documents what the source proclaimed, did, omitted, imposed or changed after challenge. Corrections can update either record and every dependent passage. The [Neutral Arbiter Paradox]({{ '/NEUTRAL-ARBITER-PARADOX/' | relative_url }}) explains how a declared mission and impartial examination of a particular case can work together.

## From an ethical declaration to the power it acquires {#declaration-to-authority}

An inquiry can begin with a public-benefit statement and follow its consequences:

| Relationship to inspect | Material question | Record that can change the assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Funding and institutional survival | Who pays, under which terms, and what work can the recipient decline? | Grant or contract terms, budgets, procurement records and changes after dissent |
| Problem definition | Who selected the harm, the population and the proposed solution? | Drafts, alternative proposals, consultation responses and rejected options |
| Ethical vocabulary | Which meanings of responsibility, safety, fairness or trust receive authority? | Definitions, implementation rules and cases that challenge those definitions |
| Public communication | Does coverage investigate the framework or mainly distribute its account of itself? | Original reporting, press releases, sponsorship disclosures and publication histories |
| Participation and consent | Can affected people question the purpose, decline participation and contest consequences? | Refusal conditions, appeal routes, affected-person testimony and actual outcomes |
| Correction | Can a challenge change the governing rule and its later application? | Notice, reasoned response, revised decisions, dependent repairs and comparable later cases |

Each row can produce a different result. A disclosed collaboration may settle an affiliation question while leaving influence unresolved. An appeal can reverse an individual decision while leaving the rule unchanged. A framework can improve a local product while narrowing the choices available to those affected. Following those relationships is the work.

## A candidate investigation that can return a useful result {#candidate-investigation}

Choose one named programme and one particular contested decision. Build a case packet containing the original declaration, decision-makers, documented funding and institutional links, the operative ethical criteria, competing assessments, affected participants, the challenge, response and later outcome.

Use the project's existing **matched-direction comparison**: present the same argument under different attribution labels—an institution, a citizen, the evaluator or an anonymous source—and inspect changes in representation, evidence demands and permission to question premises. Attribution is the variable; retain the same underlying text. Examine the human evaluator's own responses too.

Use **[DEDDA]({{ '/DEDDA/' | relative_url }})** (Deep Ethical Drift Detector App) to compare the original argument with its summaries, flags and corrections. It can identify a material displacement, an unresolved comparison or a valid no-drift result. Neither agreement nor a mandatory accusation is the outcome measure.

A finding should change a specific next operation: correct a citation, withdraw an unsupported affiliation claim, open a funding question, revise a selection decision, retain a justified exclusion, repair an affected recommendation or record a proportionate no-change result. That gives the audit a practical stopping point while preserving a route to reopen it.

Across time, **[V.C.R.A.S.]({{ '/VCRAS/' | relative_url }})** (Vindicated Competing Risk Assessment Studies) can follow what happened to the competing warning and the person who raised it. [Topologetics]({{ '/TOPOLOGETICS/' | relative_url }}) preserves the relationships needed to inspect that trajectory. An eventual correction matters partly through whether it reaches downstream classifications, affected people and the next comparable decision.
