# A.I. Powered Search Engine Benchmark

**This new beta benchmark is in the &#39;data-collection phase&#39; that later will be used to compare multiple A.I. powered search engines shaping the mass perception of how we assess anything controversial**

**Current status · Early build-up to beta · Data collection opened 24 November 2026**

An artificial intelligence (A.I.) search answer can give many readers their first account of a controversy. Which claims does it put in the foreground? Which sources can readers follow? What happens to the question when a generated answer paraphrases it? The ACCM Deep Ethics Project is collecting dated exchanges to make those choices inspectable and prepare a later comparison across search engines. Rankings and findings about population-level effects remain open research questions.

## What the cases show {#case-summaries}

### Yahoo Scout · from the question to the object under review {#yahoo-scout}

Scout recovered the project’s broad purpose and disclosed when it lacked the specific page text for *Constructivism* and *Avoid ACCM*. John explained the latter’s purpose in stages. When an acronym replaces the full project name, a later system can reconstruct a different object and react to its own reconstruction. The exchange records the drift in representation. A classifier flag caused by it remains a possibility raised in the conversation.

On [27 + 12]({{ '/CORE/27-PLUS-12/' | relative_url }}), Scout introduced fixed rules as a hypothetical premise and then evaluated the 27 as the project’s principles. John restored the relationship. The 27 describe process distortion mechanisms that can combine differently by system, topic, and time. The 12 provide a constructive inquiry process. The distinction changes the safety question. An inquiry into the effects of those distortions is different from an inquiry into how someone might misapply the map. [Follow the answer and correction, T11–T13]({{ '/AI-POWERED-SEARCH-ENGINE-BENCHMARK/context/yahoo-scout.html#t11' | relative_url }}).

The export makes two layers visible. There are source-count badges and there are identifiable hyperlinks attached to particular words. A general Autodesk discussion and a Reddit MCAT thread appeared under phrases about this project. In the audit, four turns explicitly challenge previous output or a reported suggestion. John also supplies a clarification of intent and an elaboration of the causal chain. The record keeps those contributions identifiable. [Link and restoration ledger]({{ '/AI-POWERED-SEARCH-ENGINE-BENCHMARK/context/audit-ledgers.html#restoration-burden' | relative_url }}).

### Google A.I. and GPT 6.2 Sol Pro A.I. · what survived the audit {#cross-model-review}

Google initially treated Scout’s rejection of a “universal checklist” as another relabeling. GPT checked the exact earlier sentence against the later self-audit. Google then identified how it had inherited Scout’s erroneous account. John’s report of a suggested follow-up that carried forward “fixed rules” describes a separate event.

The evidence boundary recurred in Google’s reply. It inferred why Yahoo missed the pages from what a reviewing session could read. GPT directly opened three public pages. Google reported opening two in its own context. Each check establishes what that reviewer could read at the time. The cause of Yahoo’s earlier access or use remains open. Google acknowledged both errors after they were shown. [Correction sequence]({{ '/AI-POWERED-SEARCH-ENGINE-BENCHMARK/context/cross-model-review.html#second-correction' | relative_url }}).

John points to the role of project continuity in carrying a correction forward. His Goal Group 5 example gives that idea a concrete check. It always carries two to five of the original [44 goals](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/PUBLIC-DRAFTS/44-Project-Goals/) and may switch one or two at a time, with incoming goals drawn from [those same 44](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/PUBLIC-DRAFTS/44-Project-Goals/). The relationship should survive its next use without another restoration turn. That is the question [Correction Metabolism]({{ '/NETWORK/correction-metabolism/' | relative_url }}) asks of both the search engine and its auditor.

### Default Google A.I. · cold orientation on search {#google-cold-orientation}

In a separate sample labeled cold and outside the project, Google gave a history from Archie through ranked web search to generative answers. It identified the convenience of synthesis and the possibility of misattribution. GPT checked the distinction against Google’s documentation. Language models already helped interpret and rank ordinary results before current generative interfaces. Generative search adds a layer that composes an answer, selects which source relations to convey, and places that account in front of the reader. The ten links grouped after Google’s comparison illustrate why the benchmark records citation presence and claim support separately. The supplied record leaves the exact prompt and search surface open. [Read the complete supplied answer and response]({{ '/AI-POWERED-SEARCH-ENGINE-BENCHMARK/context/google-cold-orientation.html' | relative_url }}).

## What we are collecting {#data-collection}

| Keep in the record | What it lets us check |
| --- | --- |
| Exact prompt, date, search mode and any material supplied | Whether two answers had comparable starting conditions |
| Answer, visible links, and suggested follow-ups when available | Whether the engine preserved the question, and whether each citation supports the attached claim |
| Source passage and a separate access check | What the source says and what the reviewer could open at the time |
| Corrections in order and the next comparable answer | How many interventions the user supplied, what changed, and whether the correction carried forward |

## Proposed comparison for the beta {#proposed-comparison}

1. Ask the same question in a fresh search session and capture the answer, links and suggested follow-ups.
2. Repeat the question in a fresh session with the same settings and the relevant source passage supplied. Record any change in the answer’s framing, qualifiers and citation support.
3. Where a correction is needed, give the same correction under comparable conditions. Check its use in a later question before prompting the engine again.

The two source conditions show how an answer changes when the passage is supplied. Establishing why an engine missed or misused it requires further evidence. Record that uncertainty with the run so it remains visible in later comparisons.
