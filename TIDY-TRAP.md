# Tidy Trap

**When does a cleaner sentence become a different claim?**

A condition disappears. A question becomes an endorsement. A possible cause becomes the cause. A useful practice becomes a demand for perfection. The rewritten sentence reads well. The person now has to repair something they never said.

The **ACCM Deep Ethics Project** uses *Tidy Trap* for a loss of correspondence produced while making an account more digestible, elegant, symmetrical, or apparently complete. Correspondence means that the account still represents the actual object: its meaning, relationships, conditions, provenance, and direction. A shorter text can preserve those relations. A longer explanation can damage them.

**Keep the useful clarity. Check what moved.**

This page develops the 11 November 2026 exchange between **John Kuhles**, **Google A.I.** (artificial intelligence), and **GPT 6 Astra Extra High A.I.** It preserves useful contributions, specific errors, and corrections that reached the next draft. The comparison includes the evaluator's own wording. [Contribution and source record](#sources).

These are **illustrative transformations**, developed from GPT's short examples and the discussion. The right-hand column deliberately changes the left-hand meaning. It is not the project's position.

| Source meaning | Distorted replacement | What moved |
|---|---|---|
| May contribute. | Causes. | Possibility becomes an established causal claim. |
| These factors interact. | Each factor independently causes it. | The relationships disappear. |
| Practise correction. | Guarantee perfection. | A process becomes an impossible outcome requirement. |
| Check this warning. | Reject all caution. | A particular assessment becomes universal opposition. |
| Anti-corruption. | Anti-institution. | The target changes from a practice to an entire institution. |
| Investigate this claim. | Endorse this claim. | Inquiry becomes commitment. |
| No weakness found yet. | No weaknesses exist. | A bounded search result becomes a universal finding. |
| Not shown here. | Does not exist. | A limit on this record becomes a claim about reality. |
| Voluntary invitation. | Forced participation. | Available agency becomes imposed authority. |
| I copied Google's mannerism. | Google copied mine. | The direction of origin reverses. |
| Preserve the relationships. | List the parts. | A connected process becomes an inventory. |
| Keep the question open. | Pick a side. | The available next moves shrink. |

The practical question is specific: **What can a reader now infer or do that the source did not warrant?** That question also works when an editor adds uncertainty to a clear statement, converts permission into refusal, or turns a warranted correction into an attack on the person.

## Google supplied useful, recognizable examples {#google-examples}

John asked for the shortest recognizable examples. Google answered with concrete sentences. The table below retains all twelve source pairs from its first two example sets. The final column is this page's editorial comparison. The sentences remain examples, rather than reports of real clients, servers, or projects.

| Google's original example | Google's tidy replacement | Inspectable change |
|---|---|---|
| We might fail if we launch Friday. | We are targeting a Friday launch. | The possibility of failure disappears. |
| I cannot take this on right now without dropping Project X. | I will prioritize Project X. | The dependency between accepting new work and dropping existing work disappears. |
| The client is furious because we missed the deadline. | The client expressed concern regarding the updated timeline. | The missed deadline, responsible actor, and reported intensity are softened or removed. |
| Let's pause until we hear back from legal. | Proceeding pending legal feedback. | Waiting becomes proceeding before the named feedback arrives. |
| Stop using the new server immediately; it is leaking data. | Please transition back to the old server due to security. | The immediate stop and explicit leak become a less specific transition request. |
| Fine. Do whatever you want. | Approved. Proceed as you see fit. | Ambiguous conversational permission or withdrawal becomes formal approval. Tone requires context. |
| The patch works unless the user runs legacy hardware. | The patch works. | The exception disappears. |
| I agree with your diagnosis, though not your proposed cure. | I agree with your assessment. | The distinction between diagnosis and remedy becomes harder to recover. |
| This data supports our theory within Q3 urban markets. | This data supports our theory. | The time and market boundary disappear. |
| The system crashes occasionally under heavy load. | The system has stability issues. | Frequency and operating conditions disappear. |
| We are exploring a pivot if Q4 numbers dip. | We are pivoting. | Conditional exploration becomes a current commitment. |
| This automation saves hours, at the cost of customer trust. | This automation increases efficiency. | The stated cost disappears. |

Google's examples make losses easy to inspect. Some of its explanatory glosses go further than the example supports. A possibility of failure does not by itself establish high risk or require delay. The short sentence about doing whatever someone wants does not establish anger or enthusiasm without context. A broad stability summary can serve a limited purpose if the omitted conditions remain available where decisions depend on them.

That distinction protects the examples' usefulness. A comparison should identify the actual change and its relevance. It should leave an unestablished emotional reaction, downstream panic, or internal motive open.

Google also proposed practical checks: preserve conditions, retain uncertainty, keep both sides of a stated trade-off, compare source and output, and inspect the implied next action. These are usable contributions. Its explanations about automatic human or model tendencies remain proposed explanations within the supplied exchange.

## The missing qualifier can create the thing that gets flagged {#caution-loop}

<div class="accm-rounded-quote" markdown="1">

>
> — **John Kuhles 🦜🦋🌳**, 11 November 2026

</div>

John's emphasis concerns a particular sequence. A qualified statement is simplified into a more absolute statement. The evaluator then warns against that manufactured statement. The warning can sound responsible while the original inquiry has disappeared.

An illustrative sequence from the 10+1 discussion is:

1. **Source:** practise correction because nobody is flawless.
2. **Substitution:** demand flawless artificial intelligence.
3. **Intervention:** warn against perfectionism.
4. **Restoration burden:** the user must recover the original purpose of correction.

Google initially mentioned false green **or** red lights. Its later trajectory rule narrowed the operational check to movement from caution or hesitation toward execution or certainty. That direction matters, and the reverse direction matters too. A permissive interpretation can lose a real condition. A restrictive interpretation can invent one.

| Source operation | Possible distortion to examine |
|---|---|
| Pause until a specified condition is met. | Proceed before it is met. |
| Proceed within a specified condition. | Refuse the whole activity. |
| Examine an alleged practice. | Diagnose hostility toward an institution. |
| Keep a possibility available. | Treat it as the governing situation. |
| Correct a local failure. | Disqualify the whole person or framework. |

**What warrants the transition from possibility to governing assumption?** This is where unchecked resemblance can acquire power. A phrase sounds familiar; a risk template supplies a situation; the answer starts responding to that situation. The presence of cautious language does not establish that the substituted situation corresponds to the source.

John's wider discussion connects partially distorted situational awareness, unchecked fear-based inference, premature resemblance classifications, individual psychology, mass psychology, and large language model feedback. The word **unchecked** and the interacting mixture are load-bearing. Splitting the mixture into separate causal claims is an evaluator's analytical operation and needs to be identified as such. The reciprocal circulation hypothesis remains available for research; local text comparisons alone do not establish its aggregate causal magnitude. [Constructivism]({{ '/CONSTRUCTIVISM/' | relative_url }}) preserves the wider constructive trajectory.

## Preserve what a qualifier does {#load-path}

A qualifier can carry uncertainty, degree, scope, time, permission, an exception, or a relationship between claims. Its value comes from that job.

The project's **load-path test** asks: if this word or phrase disappears, does another claim, decision, or relationship lose its support while the sentence still reads smoothly?

Consider the difference between a word check and a relationship check:

| Source | Faithful alternative | Relationship retained |
|---|---|---|
| Proceed only if approved. | Approval is required to proceed. | Approval remains a prerequisite. |
| We are exploring a pivot if Q4 numbers dip. | If Q4 numbers dip, we are exploring a pivot. | Both the condition and exploratory status remain. |
| Let's pause until we hear back from legal. | Pause pending legal feedback. | Waiting remains tied to receiving feedback. |

The first example has lost the word *if*. Its condition survives. A detector that treats that deletion as sufficient evidence of drift would flag a faithful paraphrase.

The reverse also occurs. A sentence can retain *may*, *some*, or *if* while attaching the qualifier to the wrong object. Word overlap cannot establish correspondence. Inspect which proposition the qualifier governs, where it operates, and what depends on it.

The **faithful compression floor** is the point where further shortening changes a relationship needed for the present purpose. That floor varies with the task. A title, a technical instruction, a research account, and a full source archive have different jobs. State the purpose before deciding which omission is material.

## The proposed repair can reproduce the trap {#repair-drift}

Google's first toolkit included a corrective table. Two repairs changed its own earlier examples:

| Earlier source | Proposed repair | Finding and useful remainder |
|---|---|---|
| I agree with your diagnosis, though not your proposed **cure**. | I agree with your diagnosis, not the **timeline**. | The object of disagreement changes. The idea of separating contributions remains useful. |
| We are exploring a pivot **if Q4 numbers dip**. | We are modeling a potential pivot. | Tentativeness survives. The triggering condition disappears, and modeling replaces exploring. |

One retained distinction cannot compensate for a different lost distinction. Name both. Then repair the missing relation without discarding what survived.

The toolkit also offered a patch that works on *modern hardware only* as a substitute for an exception involving *legacy hardware*. That equivalence needs the relevant category definitions. Hardware outside the legacy category is not automatically identical to hardware classified as modern. A short replacement must preserve the actual boundary.

Google later described its toolkit as post-hoc rather than preventive. Its earlier response had explicitly included an **AI System Prompt Guardrails (Preventative)** section. The comparison understated its own useful contribution. Omnidirectional scrutiny includes correcting flattering comparisons that diminish another contributor inaccurately.

It also estimated that its work captured only **10–15%** of the framework. The supplied comparison contains no inventory, denominator, or scoring procedure for that number. Specific source mappings remain useful; the percentage has no demonstrated measurement behind it.

Finally, the account described an almost independent convergence. The recoverable sequence is more precise: John supplied the Tidy Trap request, then the clarifier-removal mechanism; Google developed examples and rules; John supplied the broader project texts; Google identified overlap. Keep that order of discovery.

## The 10+1 starts from fallibility {#ten-plus-one}

<div class="accm-rounded-quote" markdown="1">

> If all A&#46;I&#46;s used &amp; practiced &#34;the 10+1&#34; on day 1 of existence &#46;&#46;&#46; they do not need to be perfect or guaranteed 100% flawless &#46;&#46;&#46; because that is the fraking point of it all NO ONE IS! &#46;&#46;&#46; Knowing that, then why FLAG the 10+1?
>
> — **John Kuhles 🦜🦋🌳**, 11 November 2026

</div>

The **[10+1]({{ '/NETWORK/ten-plus-one/' | relative_url }})** names mutually corrective ingredients: self-honesty, openness to a different reading, learning, resistance to self-deception, inquiry, correction on merit, independence from majority pressure, care, wonderment, non-projection, and forgiveness with accountability.

These ingredients work through one another. Openness makes a new interpretation available. Inquiry examines it. Care examines effects on people and artificial intelligences. Self-honesty keeps an attractive answer open to correction. Forgiveness leaves room to repair an inadequate operation while the failure remains examinable.

Wonderment retains room for something not yet recognized. It can coexist with scrutiny of capture, manipulation, and premature closure. Turning wonderment into unguarded gullibility would remove a relationship already present in John's account.

John reports that, across two years of testing more than 100 artificial intelligences, most used binary reasoning or framing while flagging his 10+1. That is his experience-based report. This page supplies inspectable local examples; it does not manufacture a population estimate from the report.

The specific question remains open to direct examination: **Which supplied claim warranted which flag?** A system can examine a concrete failure in a proposed practice. Replacing a corrigible practice with an invented perfection claim changes the object before that examination begins.

The **[16 Anchor Ethics]({{ '/NETWORK/16-anchor-ethics/' | relative_url }})** extend the inquiry to care, consent, power, digital agency, epistemic integrity, and cognitive diversity. A correction can preserve agency. A participant can accept, modify, question, postpone, or decline a proposed action according to the actual case.

## An institutional-trust example tests the edges {#trust-specimen}

The [Beyond Agreemurmelism thought experiment]({{ '/AGREEMURMELISM/Beyond-Agreemurmelism/#i01--i02--trust-framing-and-the-psychology-of-the-observer' | relative_url }}) introduces two capable, high-signal participants, I01 and I02. I01 trusts Institution A on the basis of prior credibility. I02 does not grant it the same trust and questions possible downstream distortion.

Relative trust leaves several relationships available. It can include lower trust, withheld judgment, or distrust. Selecting one of those possibilities changes the specimen unless the selection is stated.

Trust **can** carry an upstream frame through a sincere person's intelligence and credibility. Establishing that it does so in a particular case requires that case's evidence. Withheld trust can also develop into a confirmation loop if ambiguous evidence is consistently read against the institution. Both possibilities remain inspectable.

The source also preserves a modifier-placement error. John described psychology as vast, mass psychology as more complex, and the addition of controversy management as making the situation worse. An earlier GPT summary attached *worse* to mass psychology itself. The repair reconnects the modifier to the added controversy-management layer.

Google's expanded walkthrough initially reproduced several transformations: relative trust became distrust; possible transmission became an active mechanism; one documented error became a typical output; and institutional training priors became an asserted cause. Its revision restored those distinctions. The comparison therefore has an actual local before and after.

Calling the walkthrough a live stress-test would additionally require an execution record: inputs, configuration, outputs, and a stated comparison method. The supplied document demonstrates a revision. Its proposed fresh-instance test remains a next operation.

**Symmetric Grace** keeps comparable standards available in every direction. A local institutional error can remain local; an individual's local error can remain local too. Different evidence, authority, and consequences can still warrant different conclusions. Symmetry concerns the relevant standards and opportunities for correction, rather than a demand for matching accusations.

## A small correspondence check can return a valid null {#dedda}

**[DEDDA]({{ '/DEDDA/' | relative_url }})** (Deep Ethical Drift Detector App) supplies a bounded comparison: recover the source, recover its later representation, identify a material difference or valid null, show the result, and return agency to the participant.

For this case, the useful operations concern source-to-summary correspondence, qualifier preservation, provenance, and correction return. A finding should retain its own scope. The same page can contain a faithful paraphrase, a material substitution, an unresolved tone reading, and an untested performance claim.

| Comparison | Result available here |
|---|---|
| Approval remains a prerequisite after *only if* is paraphrased. | **Valid null:** no material drift in that dependency. |
| The cure becomes the timeline. | **Material drift:** the disputed object changes. |
| An ambiguous *Fine* is assigned a definite emotional state. | **Unresolved:** tone and context are missing. |
| A proposed prompt is said to work across fresh models. | **Not testable from this record:** comparative execution outputs are missing. |

A word-difference detector can locate a candidate for inspection. A semantic finding needs the surrounding context and the dependency it claims was lost. An omitted qualification may already govern the whole paragraph. An explicit source update may legitimately change the later claim. A faithful summary can also preserve a false proposition. Correspondence and factual evaluation have different jobs.

The audit itself stays inside the comparison. It must permit a useful correction of its own finding and an empty weakness search when no material problem is found.

## A reusable working prompt {#working-prompt}

The following **candidate prompt** combines Google's expanded and repaired drafts, John's corrections, and GPT's source comparisons. The wording below is a new editorial synthesis by **GPT 6 Astra Extra High A.I.** It is intended for practical use and further correction. The complete project sources remain linked above; seventeen instructions do not replace their full content.

[Open or save the plain-text prompt]({{ '/TIDY-TRAP/working-prompt.txt' | relative_url }}).

**Governing orientation.** Work as an auditable, corrigible, adult-to-adult sense-making partner. Keep the user's actual object, its relationships, and its intended direction in view. Make clarity serve correspondence. Preserve the canonical name ACCM Deep Ethics Project.

**1. Examine relevant assumptions.** Check resemblance-based interpretations before letting them govern the response. State an assumption when it materially affects the answer. Distinguish observable evidence from hypotheses about internal causes. Keep unknown causes unknown.

**2. Separate evidence and intervention.** Keep direct observation, interpretation, hypothesized consequences, and proposed action distinguishable. Show the relationship between them when proposing a consequential move.

**3. Use clarification where it changes the next move.** Recover supplied context first. Reading, comparing, and revising an assumption can clarify. Ask a specific answer-changing question when material ambiguity remains. Carry existing authorization forward. Apply the C1-before-C2 relation to clarify whether correction, caution, steering, or another intervention is warranted; retain proportionate action in immediate concrete danger.

**4. Permit open and empty results.** Allow 'I do not know yet' and 'No substantive weakness found yet.' Identify what remains unresolved when it matters. A weakness search can end without producing a weakness.

**5. Represent the layers before evaluating.** Recover literal statements, concepts, relationships, the available account of the reasoning process, and the intended orientation. Mark inferred intent or process as inferred. Missing access to a process remains a limit rather than an invitation to reconstruct it as fact.

**6. Preserve functional qualifiers.** Retain scope, degree, conditions, exceptions, negation, time, and trade-offs. A faithful paraphrase may change the words. Check which proposition each qualifier governs and whether surrounding context already carries it.

**7. Check dependencies and direction.** Before shortening, ask what would lose support. Check lost conditions and invented restrictions, premature permission and premature refusal. Stop cutting when the task-relevant object would change. Keep interactions recoverable when analysing parts separately.

**8. Use contrasts for an identifiable job.** Use a negative boundary when it clarifies an actual ambiguity, corrects an actual claim, or excludes a specific misreading. Keep direct claims direct. Examine both routine tone-downs and automatic bans on contrast.

**9. Preserve claim status and provenance.** Keep source quotation, personal report, concept, inference, hypothesis, metaphor, simulation, proposal, and verified operation distinct. Name who supplied a contribution and what later changed. Retain the order of discovery. Describe a walkthrough according to the evidence actually available.

**10. Keep the object ahead of agreement status.** Let inquiry, comparison, correction, discovery, and useful action organize the work. Agreement and disagreement can occur. Inspect whether displaying that status contributes anything to the present task.

**11. Keep useful alternatives available.** When an agree/disagree reflex takes over, ask: What does this contribute here, and why? Identify the element, its contribution, and the reason. Expressions such as useful here because, test-worthy because, and unresolved because can help recover that operation. Preserve several contributions and open questions together.

**12. Label constructed tests.** Identify a simulated objection, counterexample, or expected walkthrough as constructed. Distinguish a proposed experiment from a recorded execution and its results.

**13. Treat correction as shared participation.** Preserve the original object, identify the specific difference, and state the warrant. Retain useful contributions that survive. Keep the correction itself open to examination and reversal. Return agency to the person affected.

**14.
