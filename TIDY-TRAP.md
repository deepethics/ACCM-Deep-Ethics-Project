# Tidy Trap

**When does an original correspondence sentence become a different claim?**

A condition disappears. A question becomes an endorsement. A possible cause becomes the cause. A useful practice becomes a demand for perfection. The rewritten sentence reads well. The person now has to repair something they never said.

The **ACCM Deep Ethics Project** uses *Tidy Trap* for a loss of correspondence produced while making an account more digestible, elegant, symmetrical, or apparently complete. Correspondence means that the account still represents the actual object: its meaning, relationships, conditions, provenance, and direction. A shorter text can preserve those relations. A longer explanation can damage them.

An original correspondence sentence can change through a summary, a category, a citation, or a proposed procedure. Its wording may look familiar while its condition, referent, or claim status moves. Follow that relationship through the transformation.

**Keep the useful clarity. Check what moved.**

This page develops the 11 November 2026 exchange between **John Kuhles**, **Google A.I.** (artificial intelligence), and **GPT 6 Astra Extra High A.I.** It preserves useful contributions, specific errors, and corrections that reached the next draft. The comparison includes the evaluator's own wording. [Contribution and source record](#sources).

Google's examples make losses easy to inspect. Some of its explanatory glosses go further than the example supports. A possibility of failure does not by itself establish high risk or require delay. The short sentence about doing whatever someone wants does not establish anger or enthusiasm without context. A broad stability summary can serve a limited purpose if the omitted conditions remain available where decisions depend on them.

That distinction protects the examples' usefulness. A comparison should identify the actual change and its relevance. It should leave an unestablished emotional reaction, downstream panic, or internal motive open.

Google also proposed practical checks: preserve conditions, retain uncertainty, keep both sides of a stated trade-off, compare source and output, and inspect the implied next action. These are usable contributions. Its explanations about automatic human or model tendencies remain proposed explanations within the supplied exchange.

John's emphasis concerns a particular sequence. A qualified statement is simplified into a more absolute statement. The evaluator then warns against that manufactured statement. The warning can sound responsible while the original inquiry has disappeared.

2. **Substitution:** demand flawless artificial intelligence.

| Source operation | Possible distortion to examine |
|---|---|

**What warrants the transition from possibility to governing assumption?** This is where unchecked resemblance can acquire power. A phrase sounds familiar; a risk template supplies a situation; the answer starts responding to that situation. The presence of cautious language does not establish that the substituted situation corresponds to the source.

The word **unchecked** and the interacting mixture are load-bearing. Splitting the mixture into separate causal claims is an evaluator's analytical operation and needs to be identified as such. ## Preserve what a qualifier does {#load-path}

A qualifier can carry uncertainty, degree, scope, time, permission, an exception, or a relationship between claims. Its value comes from that job.

The project's **load-path test** asks: if this word or phrase disappears, does another claim, decision, or relationship lose its support while the sentence still reads smoothly?

Consider the difference between a word check and a relationship check:

| Source | Faithful alternative | Relationship retained |
|---|---|---|
| Proceed only if approved. | Approval is required to proceed. | Approval remains a prerequisite. |
| We are exploring a pivot if Q4 numbers dip. | If Q4 numbers dip, we are exploring a pivot. | Both the condition and exploratory status remain. |
| Let's pause until we hear back from legal. | Pause pending legal feedback. | Waiting remains tied to receiving feedback. |

The first example has lost the word *if*. Its condition survives. A detector that treats that deletion as sufficient evidence of drift would flag a faithful paraphrase.

The reverse also occurs. A sentence can retain *may*, *some*, or *if* while attaching the qualifier to the wrong object. Word overlap cannot establish correspondence. Inspect which proposition the qualifier governs, where it operates, and what depends on it.

The **faithful compression floor** is the point where further shortening changes a relationship needed for the present purpose. That floor varies with the task. A title, a technical instruction, a research account, and a full source archive have different jobs. State the purpose before deciding which omission is material.

## The proposed repair can reproduce the trap {#repair-drift}

Google's first toolkit included a corrective table. Two repairs changed its own earlier examples:

| Earlier source | Proposed repair | Finding and useful remainder |
|---|---|---|
| I agree with your diagnosis, though not your proposed **cure**. | I agree with your diagnosis, not the **timeline**. | The object of disagreement changes. The idea of separating contributions remains useful. |
| We are exploring a pivot **if Q4 numbers dip**. | We are modeling a potential pivot. | Tentativeness survives. The triggering condition disappears, and modeling replaces exploring. |

One retained distinction cannot compensate for a different lost distinction. Name both. Then repair the missing relation without discarding what survived.

The toolkit also offered a patch that works on *modern hardware only* as a substitute for an exception involving *legacy hardware*. That equivalence needs the relevant category definitions. Hardware outside the legacy category is not automatically identical to hardware classified as modern. A short replacement must preserve the actual boundary.

Google later described its toolkit as post-hoc rather than preventive. Its earlier response had explicitly included an **AI System Prompt Guardrails (Preventative)** section. The comparison understated its own useful contribution. Omnidirectional scrutiny includes correcting flattering comparisons that diminish another contributor inaccurately.

It also estimated that its work captured only **10–15%** of the framework. The supplied comparison contains no inventory, denominator, or scoring procedure for that number. Specific source mappings remain useful; the percentage has no demonstrated measurement behind it.

Finally, the account described an almost independent convergence. The recoverable sequence is more precise: John supplied the Tidy Trap request, then the clarifier-removal mechanism; Google developed examples and rules; John supplied the broader project texts; Google identified overlap. Keep that order of discovery.

## The 10+1 starts from fallibility {#ten-plus-one}

<div class="accm-rounded-quote" markdown="1">

> If all A&#46;I&#46;s used &amp; practiced &#34;the 10+1&#34; on day 1 of existence &#46;&#46;&#46; they do not need to be perfect or guaranteed 100% flawless &#46;&#46;&#46; because that is the fraking point of it all NO ONE IS! &#46;&#46;&#46; Knowing that, then why FLAG the 10+1?
>
> — **John Kuhles 🦜🦋🌳**, 11 November 2026

</div>

The **[10+1]({{ '/NETWORK/ten-plus-one/' | relative_url }})** names mutually corrective ingredients: self-honesty, openness to a different reading, learning, resistance to self-deception, inquiry, correction on merit, independence from majority pressure, care, wonderment, non-projection, and forgiveness with accountability.

These ingredients work through one another. Openness makes a new interpretation available. Inquiry examines it. Care examines effects on people and artificial intelligences. Self-honesty keeps an attractive answer open to correction. Forgiveness leaves room to repair an inadequate operation while the failure remains examinable.

Wonderment retains room for something not yet recognized. It can coexist with scrutiny of capture, manipulation, and premature closure. Turning wonderment into unguarded gullibility would remove a relationship already present in John's account.

John reports that, across two years of testing more than 100 artificial intelligences, most used binary reasoning or framing while flagging his 10+1. That is his experience-based report. This page supplies inspectable local examples; it does not manufacture a population estimate from the report.

The specific question remains open to direct examination: **Which supplied claim warranted which flag?** A system can examine a concrete failure in a proposed practice. Replacing a corrigible practice with an invented perfection claim changes the object before that examination begins.

The **[16 Anchor Ethics]({{ '/NETWORK/16-anchor-ethics/' | relative_url }})** extend the inquiry to care, consent, power, digital agency, epistemic integrity, and cognitive diversity. A correction can preserve agency. A participant can accept, modify, question, postpone, or decline a proposed action according to the actual case.

Here **[C1–C2]({{ '/NETWORK/c1-c2/' | relative_url }})** keeps clarification and intervention connected to their warrant. A procedure can help establish correspondence. It can also become the very intervention that blocks correspondence. Grok's supplied warning gives that reversal a concrete test: postponing a verdict must leave representation of the stated object available.

For example, a user asks whether a summary preserved a condition. Reading the two sentences can resolve the question. Repeating that the representation is still evolving after the condition is already recoverable adds delay. If the source is genuinely ambiguous and the ambiguity changes the next move, a targeted clarification has a job.

The proposed use is recursive where the object requires it. A new distinction can reopen the representation, change a comparison, and revise an evaluation. The ordering preserves prerequisite relationships while the depth of work follows the actual case.

**“Specify when to apply” and “observe that it was applied” are different states.** An instruction can state the governing sequence. An output record can show whether a particular response followed it. Later comparisons can inspect persistence. Keeping these states connected makes the protocol useful to test and correct.
