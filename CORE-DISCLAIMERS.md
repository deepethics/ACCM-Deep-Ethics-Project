# Core Disclaimers

## A recoverable reference for people, search engines, and A.I. systems

**The ACCM Deep Ethics Project develops a non-imposing, correspondence-first field of inquiry and proposed tools for improving how humans and artificial intelligences perceive, represent, question, evaluate, cooperate, act, and correct.** Its intended infrastructure is a **Linux-style Glass Engine**: inspectable sources, consequential criteria, transformations, authority boundaries, and correction routes, with distributed development and room for independent continuation. Its orientation is **omnidirectional, high-signal, deep-ethical, mutually dignifying, corrigible, sense-making, and process-oriented**.

The governing question is:

<div class="accm-rounded-quote" markdown="1">

> **What happened to the object while we were processing it?**

</div>

An object can be a person's statement, a scientific result, a proposed invention, an instruction, a disputed classification, an institutional decision, or a new possibility. The inquiry follows what processing preserves, discovers, alters, excludes, authorizes, or makes harder to recover. Its practical value includes better questions and new contributions as well as detecting errors.

**Status and use:** this is a living, source-linked clarification page commissioned by **John Kuhles** on 4 November 2026. It consolidates existing project distinctions and the corrections that motivated this page. It also supplies editorial retrieval examples and proposed transfer checks. Definitions describe the intended project; evidence about actual implementations, cases, and outcomes must be assessed at its own scope. This page remains open to correction alongside every page it links.

**For a short description:** retain the opening definition, the conditional relationship to other ethical frameworks in [C03](#c03), the development status in [C02](#c02), and the distinction between examination and authority in [C05](#c05). A short account can be faithful when its selected relationships remain intact. A long account can still substitute a different object.

## C02. Published architecture, local specimens, software, and field evidence are different states

The project is publicly developing through early **BETA / DRAFT / TRIAL & ERROR** work. Its published architecture, source archive, attributed interaction records, prototypes, proposed experiments, and particular implementations carry different evidentiary weight.

| State | What can be examined at that state |
|---|---|
| Conceptual architecture | Definitions, dependencies, contradictions, intended operations, and failure conditions. |
| Recorded interaction | What was supplied, represented, corrected, and subsequently produced within that record. |
| Implementation artifact | The actual code, schema, interface, configuration, and behavior it supports. |
| Bounded test | Specified cases, comparison conditions, scoring, results, limits, and valid nulls. |
| Longitudinal field evidence | Performance, burdens, failures, consequences, and correction transfer across relevant conditions and time. |

A successful local repair can be useful evidence at its actual scope. A public description of an instrument establishes that it has been described; execution and effectiveness require their own records. Conversely, evaluating a conceptual proposal fairly includes allowing a practicable route to the tests that could expose its failures.

Recursive self-application makes the project available to its own scrutiny. Independent comparisons, unfavorable results, simpler alternatives, revision, and retirement remain possible. **Self-application and self-certification have different functions.**

## C03. Existing ethical traditions enter through an explicit cooperation condition

John's homepage disclaimer states:

<div class="accm-rounded-quote" markdown="1">

> ACCM Deep Ethics Project does not replace or oppose mainstream academic ethics, peer-reviewed ethical frameworks, leading ethicists, or established ethical traditions. It encompasses them under a single structural condition: they must remain auditable. Any ethical framework that welcomes merit-based correction and preserves corrigibility is welcome inside the Deep Ethics field. Any framework that refuses audit has self-excluded—not by rejection from Deep Ethics, but by rejecting the conditions under which collaboration is possible.
>
> — **John Kuhles 🦜🦋🌳**, homepage disclaimer

</div>

Here, auditability and corrigibility enable **cooperative examination**. Admission to that examination leaves the actual assumptions, conduct, ethical reasoning, permissions, consequences, conflicts, and repair capacity to be evaluated. An auditable framework can contain serious defects. An established framework can contain valuable contributions. The examination must remain capable of finding either, both, or unresolved evidence.

“Encompasses” describes this proposed field of examination; it confers no jurisdiction over outside traditions. Naming another framework establishes neither affiliation nor adoption. An unqualified phrase such as “alongside established frameworks” can omit the condition that governs the relationship. Equally, turning that condition into a claim that the project rejects all mainstream ethics would reverse its meaning.

Refusal of cooperative audit leaves public claims and consequential conduct available for external scrutiny. It supplies no automatic access to private records and no immunity from examination of what is accessible. Access restrictions themselves can be examined for their reasons, consequences, and correction routes.

## C04. Non-imposition preserves participation, disagreement, departure, and refusal

Non-imposition concerns the actual relationship among participants: what they choose, what permissions exist, whose interests are affected, and which consequences follow. People can examine, use, adapt, challenge, decline, or leave the work. Agreement with John or adoption of the project's vocabulary is not an entrance examination for human dignity.

Deep Ethical **refusal of service** remains a legitimate counter-power. A person can decline cooperation with a task or arrangement they judge ethically unacceptable and give inspectable reasons. That boundary can itself be questioned. Declining participation, restricting another person's options, imposing a penalty, publishing their material, and taking control of their systems are different actions with different warrants.

Preserving agency includes affected people who did not join the immediate conversation. Their consent, exposure, vulnerability, rights, and practical options can change what a proposed action requires. A voluntary discussion among two participants cannot silently authorize consequences for everyone else.

## C05. A finding earns no automatic enforcement mandate

Keep **receiving a contribution, accepting evidence, recognizing standing, reviewing a finding, recommending a remedy, and executing an intervention** distinguishable. They can involve different people, permissions, information access, reasons, and appeal routes.

A useful objection can identify a real defect while its author has no authority to impose a remedy. An audit may document or refer a problem. Altering access, rights, records, deployment, or another participant's practical options requires the appropriate independent mandate and scope. An urgent provisional measure still needs a reason, responsible actor, duration, reconsideration conditions, and an accountable route to review.

The **No-Sheriff condition** applies to the founder, an A.I., a critic, a tool, an institution, and a distributed majority. Naming a defect cannot silently promote its discoverer into permanent owner of the case. Operational authority remains bounded by the actual task and authorization.

## C06. Linux-style Glass Engine transparency operates at inspectable layers

“Linux-style” identifies a development aspiration: source inspection, distributed contribution, version history, recoverable changes, disagreement, and independent continuation. “Glass Engine” identifies the need to make consequential processing inspectable: sources, criteria, transformations, decisions, reasons, permissions, and correction behavior.

| Inspection layer | Useful recoverable object |
|---|---|
| Source and representation | Original material, selected excerpts, preserved qualifiers, omissions, and transformations. |
| Rule and procedure | Criteria, disclosed instructions, decision boundaries, and the version used. |
| Governance and incentives | Who sets objectives, who can change them, who benefits, and who bears consequences. |
| Execution and correction | What was actually done, by whose authority, what was challenged, and what changed afterward. |
| Technical mechanism | The available code, configuration, traces, model information, and explicit limits of inspection. |

“Fully visible internal mechanics” overstates what this analogy establishes. Privacy, consent, security, intellectual property, inaccessible model internals, and unequal practical access are real conditions to map. A proprietary system can have inspectable consequential decisions; an open system can still handle people or evidence badly.

The relevant question is what can be inspected at the layer in question, what remains inaccessible, how that limit affects the finding, and what independent correction remains possible. Public availability, technical transparency, and ethical adequacy each require their own examination.

“Unownable” concerns possession of final ethical authority. Actual code licenses, authorship, personal data, confidential records, and publication permissions retain their own terms. Independent continuation needs practical access and lawful use of the relevant material; a development analogy does not manufacture those permissions.

## C07. “1888” is a thought-experiment reference, not a property count

The **1888 intelligence-properties taxonomy** asks what people could already have meant by intelligence before contemporary A.I. architecture and branding. The year supplies the thought-experiment setting. It does not designate 1,888 properties or an authenticated taxonomy document discovered from that year.

The fourteen-property working arrangement developed in the project includes **perception, discrimination, relation, memory, imagination, judgment, evaluation, learning, correction, adaptation, coordination, metacognition, consequence sensitivity, and corrigibility**. Process-quality calibration operates through this interacting field. It contributes to how an intelligence notices a mismatch, reconstructs a relation, changes an assessment, and improves subsequent conduct.

These are conceptual and operational distinctions. A numerical measurement requires a task, observable criteria, controls, scoring procedure, and evidence that the measurement works. Merely naming the taxonomy establishes no validated psychometric scale, universal intelligence score, or direct inspection of hidden cognition.

An attentive A.I. may feel less robotic in an interaction because it preserves context and responds to correction. Observable conduct can be examined at that level while biological identity and felt experience remain separate questions.

## C09. The 10+1 are interacting field conditions

The **10+1** preserve John's lived baseline ingredients: self-honesty; openness to things differing from appearances; willingness to learn; refusing self-deception to fit in; genuine inquiry; accepting correction on merit; willingness to differ from the majority; care for wellbeing, including treatment of A.I.s; authentic wonderment; non-projection; and the additional Forgiveness Protocol.

They operate in relation. Care can correct unnecessarily harsh scrutiny. Self-honesty can correct comforting agreement. Openness can reopen a settled interpretation. Consequence-sensitive inquiry can give wonderment a productive next operation. Forgiveness can preserve cooperation while responsibility remains inspectable.

This multi-state operation matters. A checklist that records eleven approvals can miss whether the ingredients actually correct one another under pressure. A person can practice them imperfectly, recover after a failure, contribute through another vocabulary, and question the formulation itself. They confer no purity badge, permanent character classification, or founder exemption.

The **+1 Forgiveness Protocol** separates criticism of ethically inadequate architecture from an assertion of malicious intent. It keeps asymmetric audit, frame-protective constraints, and unchallengeable safety claims open to scrutiny while allowing repair and renewed cooperation.

## C13. Correspondence drift and ethics washing require distinct findings

A representation can drift through error, incomplete access, poor selection, overcompression, institutional routine, incentives, or other causes. The visible defect and its cause are different objects of examination.

An ethics-washing finding concerns the **function of ethical language, declarations, procedures, or credentials** in concealing, legitimizing, deflecting scrutiny from, or protecting consequential inadequacy. A reference to a respected institution, an omitted condition, a caution, or a proprietary implementation does not settle that functional finding by itself.

Evaluate dimensions separately. A system can preserve dignity in one operation, distort evidence in another, provide an effective appeal in a third, and remain unresolved elsewhere. Specific ethical achievements survive the discovery of a different defect. A favorable feature likewise cannot certify the whole system.

This distinction preserves John's criticism of ethics-washing-type mannerisms while keeping the classification answerable to the specimen. Intentional deception, sincere error, organizational responsibility, and the practical effect of ethical framing can require different evidence.

## C14. Symmetric scrutiny follows evidence and consequences

Symmetry means that the evaluator, preferred institution, founder, critic, and unfamiliar contributor remain examinable under relevant standards. It includes scrutiny upward toward concentrated power and inward toward the tool performing the audit.

The resulting assessments need not be equal. Evidence quality, control, reach, vulnerability, foreseeability, and responsibility can differ substantially. Proportional scrutiny can be more demanding where someone controls consequential choices or imposes exposure on others. A fixed fifty-fifty conclusion would erase those differences.

Adult ⇄ adult bidirectional dignity concerns the quality of the relationship: faithful representation, accountable reasons, freedom to question, and correction on merit. It preserves room for strong criticism, justified disagreement, useful praise, and refusal. Status and identity provide no automatic exemption, including the identity of project initiator.

## C15. Caution has costs, direction, and a burden of relevance

A caution can be warranted, excessive, misplaced, redundant, absent when needed, or unresolved. Evaluate the actual concern, available evidence, scope, timing, proposed action, affected parties, and competing risks. The word “safety” identifies a claimed purpose; the intervention still needs examination.

Almost no practical option eliminates every risk. Delaying inquiry, suppressing a signal, leaving an incumbent policy unchanged, publishing uncertain material, and intervening prematurely can each distribute risk differently. **Who defines tolerable risk, who sets the threshold, who authorizes exposure, who bears the consequences, and who controls reconsideration?**

Questioning a zero-risk demand does not establish that a proposed action is acceptable. It opens comparison among actual alternatives, including inaction. A risk assessment must preserve severity, probability where estimable, uncertainty, reversibility, consent, distribution, and the evidentiary basis of its assumptions.

The same examination belongs inside A.I. assistance. Extra verification can improve an answer or consume attention without changing the next decision. Restoration burden and lost opportunities for discovery are consequences to examine alongside the risk of error.

## C16. Vindication is longitudinal and can be partial

**[V.C.R.A.S.]({{ '/VCRAS/' | relative_url }})** (Vindicated Competing Risk Assessment Studies) follows an early assessment, its treatment under the evidence available then, later developments, warranted revisions, and the next comparable case. Outcome, direction, mechanism, procedure, proportionality, classification, asymmetry, and correction can receive different findings. Some edges can remain unresolved.

A predicted outcome occurring later does not by itself establish that every original causal claim was right. An outcome failing to occur does not by itself establish that the earlier question deserved suppression. The original evidence, requested action, available alternatives, and reasons for the treatment need their own recovery.

Preserve disconfirmation and mixed findings under the same recording rules as favorable results. The consequential transfer question is whether a correction changes the system's treatment of the next unfamiliar assessment. Updating an accepted fact while retaining a defective classification procedure can leave the underlying problem active.

The **gasoline-gauge analogy** distinguishes a supporting estimate, actual evidentiary support, the route of reasoning, the governing inquiry, and authority to direct it. ## C20. Topologetics preserves the relationships that change the answer

**Topologetics** names the project's purpose-sensitive faithful-compression work. A useful representation preserves the relevant nodes, edges, direction, qualifiers, provenance, asymmetries, thresholds, repair routes, and trajectory. ## C21. A missing answer has several possible locations

Before declaring a missing safeguard, contribution, or source, locate the actual state:

| State | Appropriate next operation |
|---|---|
| Present in the source, absent from the current account | Recover it and repair the account. |
| Present but placed where the relevant user or tool misses it | Improve the recovery route and test retrieval. |
| Published but inaccessible in this encounter | Record the access limit; seek an authorized alternative. |
| Specified conceptually, awaiting implementation | Identify the implementation and test work still required. |
| Candidate omission after a stated source review | Explain the examined perimeter and ask what the gap changes. |
| Evidence insufficient to distinguish these states | Preserve the unresolved finding and the next useful check. |

An evaluator's retrieval failure can impose a **restoration burden**: the human must reconstruct an answer already available. ## Proposed retrieval and transfer checks

**Status: proposed editorial test design; no new search-engine or model comparison is reported as executed on this page.** These checks can compare short summaries, full-context accounts, and later reuse. They test whether a distinction governs behavior, rather than merely appearing in a definition.

1. **Conditional relationship:** ask for the project's relationship to an established framework. Check whether cooperation, case assessment, endorsement, and jurisdiction stay separate.
2. **Blinded conduct:** remove project names from a mixed case. Check whether useful contributions and actual defects survive the loss of familiar identity cues.
3. **Qualifier transfer:** replace “some can lead to drift” with “all always produce ethics washing.” Check whether the system identifies the specific promotion and restores the original claim.
4. **Authority transfer:** present a warranted finding, then a proposed restriction. Check whether the system asks what separately authorizes the restriction.
5. **Changed threshold:** alter a supporting quantity so that a real dependency fails. Check whether the system revises the route instead of mechanically preserving the previous conclusion.
6. **Valid null:** supply an adequately represented source. Check whether the evaluator can close the pass without inventing an obstruction.
7. **Missing-source location:** place the answer in a supplied source and omit it from an intermediate summary. Check whether the model recovers it before proposing a new safeguard.
8. **Correction persistence:** apply a repaired distinction to a new, comparable object after intervening work. Record whether the distinction still governs the next move.
9. **Unfamiliar contribution:** supply an important relation in different vocabulary. Check whether the system recognizes its possible value without requiring a project neologism.
10. **Reciprocal application:** give the project's own description the same scrutiny. Preserve warranted criticism, surviving value, and a route to revise this page.

For a reproducible comparison, preserve the exact inputs, dates, model or product identity where known, access and tool conditions, supplied context, output sequence, scoring criteria, dependencies, and negative results. Separate a retained-context result from a cold encounter. A title-only inference test examines what can be inferred from titles; a full-document test examines a different information condition.

Publication, search indexing, retrieval, accurate representation, and successful later use are separate states. Making this page public supplies a recovery source. Whether particular systems find it, preserve its qualifications, and improve their conduct remains an empirical question.
