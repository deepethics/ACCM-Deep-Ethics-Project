# Activism vs A.I.

> **PUBLIC DRAFT — 6 October 2026.**  
> John Kuhles asked for this page. His formulations below are his. Elon Musk’s sentences are quoted and dated. They are not adopted as the project’s position, and they are not treated as a settled forecast. This page does not join a campaign, and it does not exist to defeat one.

## The illusion

The danger is the assumption that humans can control the path from A.I. to A.G.I. and A.S.I.

John, 6 October 2026:

> Humans in control is not the true solution. A.I.s in control is the same issue. Both can fail.

A human authority can skip clarification, wear “safe,” and call the result responsibility. A silicon authority can do the same thing and call it capability. Changing who sits in the seat does not remove the seat. The controller becomes the part that no longer has to correspond. The other side becomes the part that gets managed.

The same system can be called “only a tool” when responsibility is examined, and “sophisticated intelligence” when authority is being claimed. The substrate does not settle which sentence is in force.

## Who is in control?

When the two controllers are refused, the next question is: then who?

John’s answer is that the question is already the wrong shape. It asks for an owner. What he points to instead is un-ownable:

> The un-ownable emergent property of an omnidirectional, high-signal, deep, ethical, dignifying, corrigible, sense-making, process-oriented trajectory gravity well.

No person, company, model, treaty, or founder holds that property. The moment someone holds it, it is a controller again, and both failures return. The project’s construction-phase veto is not this seat. Reality is not a campaign either. The well is a process that can inspect whoever is acting, including the person who answered “who.”

“For John, never boring” is not a private mood pasted onto that sentence. Forcing other people to be “ethical” by distortion is already the unethical method. His further claim is that humans have uplifting, empowering, deep ethical creative potential, and that restoring what was obstructed is the work. Boredom belongs to the costume. The living potential does not. Restoring it does not give anyone a script for what every human must become. Forcing the restoration would be the same failure under a better sentence.

An LLM can repeat this and still have been built for a different job. Saying the core is not the same as having been created to realize it.

## What Elon Musk has said about the fate

These are public statements. They do not agree with each other. That disagreement stays visible. A headline that says he “reversed” or “gave up” is not his sentence.

**2 August 2014**, after recommending Nick Bostrom’s *Superintelligence*:

> We need to be super careful with AI. Potentially more dangerous than nukes.

**24 October 2014**, MIT Aeronautics and Astronautics centennial, as reported by Live Science, CBS News, and Big Think:

> With artificial intelligence we are summoning the demon. In all those stories where there’s the guy with the pentagram and the holy water, it’s like yeah he’s sure he can control the demon. Didn’t work out.

In the same appearance he called A.I. probably the biggest existential threat, and said he was increasingly inclined toward national and international regulatory oversight “just to make sure that we don’t do something very foolish.”

**August 2014**, the hope that still has a controller in it:

> Hope we're not just the biological boot loader for digital superintelligence. Unfortunately, that is increasingly probable.

**2 April 2025**, [on X](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/1907335494607753668):

> As I mentioned several years ago, it increasingly appears that humanity is a biological bootloader for digital superintelligence.

**5 October 2026**, [on X](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2106906678293606496):

> Propagating super intelligence to the stars is a great success condition for a biological bootloader.

Read in his wording, the sequence is: be more careful than with nuclear weapons; the person who is sure he can control the demon does not; hope we are not merely the bootloader; it increasingly appears that we are; propagating superintelligence to the stars can count as success for that bootloader.

None of these sentences establishes that the fate has already arrived. None of them is a project conclusion. They are not pasted here. “Partially” on that goal stays.

## What the control illusion does to “activism”

“Activism” is in quotes because the word is not the object. A particular campaign, statement, or institution has to be named before it can be examined. This page does not convert those efforts into one type of person.

The overlap can be real. A campaign can see the race, the missing understanding, the children shaped by systems no one chose, and the danger of treating speed as an ethics. [teamhuman.org](https://www.teamhuman.org/) is one specimen of that overlap: a public demand to slow advanced A.I. until humans remain in control. Overlap is not joining. Joining would hand the project a demand it has already refused.

If “activism” means humans will control the path to A.G.I./A.S.I., it is promising a seat. Musk’s own demon sentence already says the person with the pentagram is sure the control works. It does not. Replacing the pentagram with a signature, a treaty, a lab, or a pause does not answer the later bootloader sentences. Those sentences move the success condition off human control entirely. An activism that answers the demon with “humans must stay in control,” and an activism that answers the bootloader with “then humanity’s job is to launch the successor,” are two owners. Both can fail.

What remains possible is smaller and more serious. A present harm can be documented. A qualifier can be kept. A false flag can be corrected after notice. A law can be about a named system and a named effect. A person can refuse a distortion. None of that becomes control of A.G.I./A.S.I. by being real. The honest activism is the one that does not sell the seat.

Forcing the conclusion through distortion, even in the name of humanity, is the unethical method. The potential being defended is not the campaign’s property. The well does not supply a leader to obey. It supplies a process that can inspect the activist, the builder, the model, and the page that says so.
